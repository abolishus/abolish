# T-29: Tally or decryption manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A trustee or coordinator publishes a wrong decryption share or combines shares wrongly.

## Mitigation

Each trustee's summed shares are checked against the product of the public VSS commitments, so a wrong sum is attributed to its trustee, and the combined aggregate must open the product of the public commitments ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); the verifier recombines and recomputes the tally (P1-15, P1-19).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): the tally is specified ([[tally]]): per cell, the product of the counted ballots' VSS commitments; each trustee's summed shares checked against it with the same equation as a ballot's share check, so a wrong sum or a sum over another set of ballots fails and is excluded and reported with its seat; the `k` lowest passing seats are combined by Lagrange interpolation, and the opening must open each option's aggregate commitment with a count of at most the cell's ballots. `packages/crypto` implements it (`openCell`), with vectors from an independent implementation that solves for every coefficient. Signing and boarding the summed shares (the tally transcript) is part 2.
