## What was decided (part 1)

[[0014-tier1-vouching]], `Accepted (agent) — owner may veto`. It picks option C:

- **Who vouches:** Tier 2 members, anonymously.
- **Each vouch** is two proofs under Semaphore v4's unchanged circuit and keys:
  - one with a per-vouchee scope, so the vouchers are distinct;
  - one with a per-slot scope, so each voucher has a lifetime budget.

  Each proof's message binds the vouchee and the other proof's nullifier.

- **Admission:** a commitment with vouches from `k = 3` distinct members, each member having `L = 12` vouches, ever.

It replaces the Safe's signature for Tier 1 additions, so the verifier checks the rule from the board.

The rejected options:

- an operator-held graph: unverifiable, and it stores the graph;
- a public signed web of trust: it publishes the graph;
- a transitive variant: fake identities could vouch for more, without bound.

Threat files T-02, T-06, T-22 and T-27 are updated.

## Independent review (part 1)

A fresh in-session subagent reviewed the ADR adversarially against the `crypto-review` checklist. Its blocking findings are fixed:

- **One slot could fund all `k` vouches of one addition,** which broke the bound by a factor of `k`. Fixed: an addition's counted vouches need pairwise-distinct slot nullifiers, and they must be unused by every earlier counted vouch. Only the first `k` qualifying vouches of a counted addition consume slots.
- **"Ever" didn't hold against the operator.** Slots were scoped per Tier 1 group, so a second group reset every budget. Fixed: the slot scope binds the chain and the registry only.
- **A T-22 residual was missing:** anyone holding a voucher's secret can recompute every vouch that voucher made (T-69, T-18, T-20). Now stated in the ADR, T-22 and STATUS.

Its non-blocking findings are also addressed:

- **Which Tier 2 roots count:** only tier-2-attributed groups of the same registry, at a period boundary within `W`.
- **Self-vouching and the results label.**
- **Deception in the bound:** `(c·L + d)/k`.
- **Who submits:** the vouchee, and the resulting IP residual is stated.
- **The slot-tracking rule.**
- **Verification cost:** batched Groth16, T-52.
- **The T-27 soft gate.**
- **Removal conditions** for P3-2.
- **The fresh-commitment rule** is now a MUST.
- **The spec pages part 2 must change.**
- **The threat-model index:** left unchanged under `AGENTS.md`'s rule for `THREAT_MODEL.md`, and the PR body says so.

A second pass over the fixes found one more blocking issue: "include `k`" and "the first `k` in order" could pick different vouches, so verifiers could rebuild different roots. Fixed: an addition carries exactly `k` vouches, and every one must qualify. The second pass's non-blocking findings are also fixed:

- the client's held-slot rule (based on consumption, timed in final L2 blocks);
- fetching consumed slots without querying by value;
- the total order over additions and the cost of deployment-wide slots;
- the anchoring period boundary is now defined;
- the one-registry check and mock vouchers;
- the T-27 status line.

## What was built (part 2a)

- [[vouching]], a new spec page: `k = 3` and `L = 12` (also in [[parameters]]); the binding `u64(chain_id) ‖ bytes[20](registry) ‖ u64(group_id)`; the per-vouchee scope `S_V` (tag `abolish/v1/vouch`) and the per-slot scope `S_j` (`abolish/v1/vouch-slot`, chain and registry only, so a second group gives no fresh slots); the two messages (`abolish/v1/vouch-message`), each binding its role, the binding, `C_V`, the Tier 2 group and root block it names and the other proof's nullifier; the 626-byte vouch and the `41 + 626·k`-byte Tier 1 addition record, strictly decoded; the checks of one addition in a fixed order (`forbidden-commitment`, `vouch-count`, `slot`, `root`, `duplicate-voucher`, `duplicate-slot`, `proof` with the vouch and which proof); and the prover rules part 3 builds.
- The record is group-registry calldata with no record header, like the Tier 0 records: its layout is fixed by the registry deployment, so [[versioning]]'s record-type registry gets no entry (the ADR had listed [[versioning]]; a new layout is a new registry, never a version alongside).
- The vouch carries its root `ρ` as well as the Tier 2 group and block that name it, so a vouch can be checked (and a contract can check it as spam control) without rebuilding Tier 2; part 2b requires it to equal the root the verifier rebuilds. Both messages bind the group and block, so a relay can't rewrite which root a vouch names.
- `packages/crypto/src/vouch.ts`: `vouchScope`, `vouchSlotScope`, `vouchMessage`, `decodeTier1Addition`, `encodeTier1Addition`, `checkTier1Addition`, `VOUCH_K`, `VOUCH_SLOTS`; the three tags in the registry (and `circuit.json` regenerated for them).

## Tested (part 2a)

- `docs/spec/vectors/vouch.json`, from `packages/crypto/scripts/vouch-vectors.mjs`, which shares no code with `packages/crypto`: real proofs from Semaphore 4.14.3's own prover over a Tier 2 tier tree (poseidon-lite), every proof judged by snarkjs 0.7.5, hashes from `node:crypto` and Semaphore's own field mapping, and each addition's expected result written from the spec's checks in order. It has 7 scopes, both messages, 27 additions (honest, reordered, re-randomised, a pooled vouch and a second vouchee valid; then a duplicate voucher, a duplicate slot through pooling, a repeated vouch, 0, 2 and 4 vouches, slots 12 and 65535, two forbidden commitments, root 0, and proofs moved to another group, commitment, chain, slot, root block, Tier 2 group or root, swapped or tampered) and 13 rejected encodings.
- `tests/vouch.test.ts`: every vector; each case a cheap check refuses carries proofs that verify, so removing that check would let it through; every accepted record re-encodes to itself; arbitrary and well-framed random bytes decode or are refused without throwing (fast-check); any changed byte of an honest addition fails it (fast-check, capped at 40 runs because each run verifies up to six proofs); the honest addition fails under another chain or registry; context and value validation.

## Known-weak

- **Tier 1 rests on Tier 2.** No real person is admitted before Tier 2 admits real documents ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]'s owner questions).
- **Collusion is bounded, not prevented:** at most 4 fake identities per colluding document.
- **Double admission:** a person with two disjoint groups of three vouchers gets two identities.
- **No accountability:** anonymous vouchers can't be held to account.
- **Metadata:** timing and network metadata around an addition remain (T-11, T-22).
- **No addition counts yet** (part 2a): a vouch's root isn't checked against Tier 2 and its slots aren't checked against earlier additions until part 2b, so nothing may count toward Tier 1 before it merges.
- **Unchecked prior art:** the prior-art facts cited in the ADR weren't re-checked in this session. Part 2 re-checks any it relies on.
