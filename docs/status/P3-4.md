## What was found (part 2a)

Recorded in [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]], Amendment (sources pinned by commit there):

- **Curve.** The curve is Baby Jubjub's prime-order subgroup with `B8`, as assumed. `@noble/curves`' `babyjubjub` has the same parameters, with an explicit subgroup check.
- **Reuse.** zkPassport's salted mode verifies one combined proof against a public key inside every disclosure circuit. That is option B's shape, so its output side isn't reused. Its `oprf-auth` query circuit binds only `comm_in`, binds no registration record and accepts `x = 0`.
- **Our circuits.** The query and output circuits are therefore our own. The query circuit requires `x ≠ 0` in the clear-value form.
- **`H1`.** `H1` follows `hash_to_curve`'s two-map structure under our scoped tag, not TACEO's single-map `encode`. It is evaluated only in-circuit.
- **Audit.** `oprf-nr` v1.0.0 differs from the commits Least Authority audited. Part 2b diffs the gadgets it uses and covers findings A, B, D and E.
- **SOD signature.** The encoding is canonical: ECDSA has low `s` and range-checked `r` and `s`; RSA signatures are below the modulus. Uniqueness also needs the identity sub-circuits to be sound, and a question about that is held for the owner (details withheld until the owner decides on disclosure).
- **zkPassport's app.** Its request can't name our panel, and salted mode needs strict FaceMatch. Real-document Tier 2 waits for our own app (P5-3, P5-4). Part 3's QR handoff runs against MOCK documents.
- **"Poseidon2"** names both the Poseidon2 permutation and circomlib's two-input Poseidon. Part 2b names each without ambiguity.

## Known-weak

- Anyone holding a document's chip data (the issuing state first) can query for it as its holder would, and learns its `N` and whether it registered (passive authentication only). The panel publishes query and duplicate counts so a mass probe is visible.
- A Diffie–Hellman OPRF leaves discrete-log relations on the key: a discrete-log break recovers the OPRF key `sk` from one recorded or self-made registration and, with chip data, maps a stolen attester list.
- `sk` is long-lived and held by people, unlike [[0006-trustees]] rule 7's per-election keys; resharing relies on erasure of old shares, and losing `n − k + 1` shares means re-registering Tier 2.
- Registration needs six of seven seats online; the attesters' nullifier lists aren't rebuilt from chain and IPFS.
- The query and output proofs carry `dg1` in their witness and are only computationally zero-knowledge towards the seats.
- The app that runs the query learns `x` and `N`, so a malicious build learns both (T-19). Since part 2a that app is our own (P5-3, P5-4), because zkPassport's app can't query the panel. Until it exists, real documents can't register at Tier 2 through the panel.
- `H1` and the query and output circuits are our composition of TACEO's gadgets, with no published vectors (T-36). `oprf-nr` v1.0.0 differs from the audited commits, so audit coverage waits on part 2b's diff.
- Uniqueness of `x` for real documents rests on the soundness of zkPassport's identity sub-circuits, on which a question is held for the owner.
