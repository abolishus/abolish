## Known-weak (part 2b-2b)

- A panel change needs `max(k, n − 1)` of the predecessor's seats to deal (the old panel's consent), so any two of them gone, unavailable or refusing block every panel change, the one that would replace them included, as rule 7 already lets two trustees block elections from opening; the key then stays on a shrinking panel until `n − k + 1` losses end it. Panels must be changed or refreshed while at most one seat is missing (T-54).
- Erasure is the whole defence against a mobile adversary: `k` old shares kept by anyone after the successor counts still give `sk`, and no erasure can be proven (T-14).
- A reshare run stalls as the initial sharing does: one corrupt dealer withholding a reshared share from one honest new seat, with one new seat that doesn't approve, stalls it unattributably (rule 7); the predecessor stays in force and the run's approvals expire at its deadline, so the adversary delays a panel change for as long as it keeps withholding, run after run (T-54).
- [[nullifier-key]]'s initial key has no deadline: its approval rule 4 lets a seat approve one key per scope ever, so a stalled initial run (one corrupt seat withholding from two honest seats under `q = n − 1`) blocks the scope's initial key for good. The same deadline rule, with a new approval statement, must reach the initial key before any registry is deployed (part 2b-4).
- A re-signed last-round message gives a second `key_id` for one reshare (liveness only; same shares and `sk`).
- Where approvals are recorded, how attesters learn a predecessor is retired, and verifier 2.4's walk of a scope's chain of keys are part 2b-4's.
- Polynomial evaluation, Lagrange coefficients and share sums are `bigint` arithmetic with no zeroisation (T-39).
- Left to the caller, as in part 2b-2a, and to close before any registry is deployed (part 2b-4): a dealer deals once per run and only for the current key to the pinned successor panel; approval rules 4 and 5; erasure once a successor counts; and a registry that accepts at most one initial key per scope.

## What was found (part 2a)

Recorded in [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]], Amendment (sources pinned by commit there):

- **SOD signature.** The encoding is canonical: ECDSA has low `s` and range-checked `r` and `s`; RSA signatures are below the modulus. Uniqueness also needs the identity sub-circuits to be sound, and a question about that is held for the owner (details withheld until the owner decides on disclosure).
- **zkPassport's app.** Its request can't name our panel, and salted mode needs strict FaceMatch. Real-document Tier 2 waits for our own app (P5-3, P5-4). Part 3's QR handoff runs against MOCK documents.

## Known-weak
