# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires; the result pages and API flag small counts (P3-6).
