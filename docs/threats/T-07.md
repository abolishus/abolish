# T-07: Setup compromise of a proving system

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-1
- Status: **Accepted.** Sound if any one participant in each ceremony was honest.

## Threat

A cheating participant in Semaphore's Groth16 ceremony could forge Semaphore proofs; a compromised universal KZG SRS (used by bb's UltraHonk for our Noir circuits) could forge Noir proofs.

## Mitigation

Semaphore's multi-party ceremony is the one accepted exception to "no per-circuit trusted setup" (brief, Chain). Our Noir circuits need no per-circuit setup, but they rest on the universal SRS bb uses, itself from a multi-party ceremony. Which proof system verifies membership and nullifiers (Semaphore's verifier, or our Noir circuit over Semaphore groups) is settled in P1-17 and recorded here.
