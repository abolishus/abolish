# T-68: Moderation abuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: content anchored on-chain or on IPFS can't be erased, only hidden by our front ends; the moderation log lives in our database, so an insider can withhold rows from what we serve or lose them in a wipe (no result changes either way), and the log's rules bind the tooling, not an insider who inserts rows directly or drops its triggers with DDL; hiding an open poll for `illegal`, `personal-data` or `threat` stops voting through our client and can lower turnout (listed for the owner under Decisions to review).

## Threat

Moderation is used to suppress polls or results, or anchored content can't be moderated at all.

## Mitigation

A written policy ([[moderation-policy]]); moderation acts on poll content, never results (P2-10). Built (P2-10 part 1): the policy, with its closed list of grounds and the grounds that never count (viewpoint, report volume, a poll's result or turnout); an append-only, public moderation log in `packages/core` whose only actions are delisting, hiding display text and restoring, each citing a reason code, with no free text and no moderator in the public view; the tooling never writes the board or a definition, and refuses to hide a poll's text before it closes except for illegal content, personal data or a threat, since hiding stops voting through our client. Results stay verifiable from chain + IPFS whatever the log says (G-9). Planned: reports, appeals and the public API (P2-10 part 2); front-end behaviour (P2-8, P2-9); the legal questions in the policy are the owner's.
