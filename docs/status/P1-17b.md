## Scope

From [[0009-membership-proof]] and [[membership]]:

- Vendor Semaphore v4's depth-32 Groth16 verification key (the only depth Abolish uses, [[membership]], Tree depth) from `semaphore-protocol/semaphore` at the tag and commit [[membership]] cites, with source, commit and sha256 re-fetched in CI. Allowing that publisher needs a `ci:` PR first (rule B).
- Specify `abolish/v1/membership-vk`: the hash of the pinned key set that the election definition's `membership_vk_hash` carries (P1-10b), and the proof encoding a ballot carries.
- Check the pinned depth-32 proving key's reference string for well-formedness once, publicly (Fuchsbauer, PKC 2018), and bind the checked key into `abolish/v1/membership-vk`: without it, zero knowledge against a subverted ceremony isn't established ([[membership]], Security notes).
- Groth16 verification over BN254 in the gated packages, on `@noble/curves`' BN254 pairing: the published verification equation, no new primitive ([[0009-membership-proof]], Consequences). Strict decoding of proof points (canonical coordinates, on the curve, in the prime-order subgroup, G2 included); every public input below `r`; the key's `IC` exactly 5 points; only pinned keys.
- The tier tree ([[membership]], Tier tree): rebuild a tier's root from its additions, as verifier 2.4 needs. It needs circomlib's `Poseidon2` over BN254, from `@noble/curves`' Poseidon with circomlib's published parameters vendored and checked against circomlib's own test vectors.
- Real Semaphore proofs as vectors (generated with Semaphore's own tooling outside the gated packages, recorded with their source), plus tampered proofs, wrong public signals, a proof under another depth's key, wrong scope (including another tier's scope over the same root) a proof against root 0 (proof length above the depth), a hidden-subtree proof (a registered value that is the root of a private tree, proven with a path longer than its leaf's), and removals by commitment, all rejected; and a tier tree with a forbidden addition (`0`, `1`, `Poseidon2(0, 1)`, non-canonical) whose leaf is 0 and whose definition passes, and a tier whose only additions are forbidden (root `Z_32`), whose definition fails; and removals of an out-of-range or already-removed index, which change nothing. The real proofs' scope and message inputs, made by Semaphore's own `hash`, also tie `F` in `membership.json` to Semaphore's output, which P1-17 checked only against Keccak known answers.

## Notes

- Groth16 proofs are malleable; replay detection never compares proof bytes ([[verifier]] 5.7, 6.1 R1).

## Part 1 (#111)

Built:

- [[membership]], Verification key: Semaphore's depth-32 key (vendored `verification-keys.json`, entry 31) and its proving key `semaphore-32.zkey` by sha256; `membership_vk_hash = H(DS("abolish/v1/membership-vk", u8(32) ‖ vk ‖ zkey_sha256))`, so the hash binds the proving key the reference-string check (part 2) covers.
- [[membership]], Proof encoding: `membership_proof`, 256 bytes, A ‖ B ‖ C in EIP-196/197's encoding (Semaphore's packed order), with every point canonical, on its curve, in the order-`r` subgroup and not the identity. The ballot record (P1-12) can now carry it.
- [[membership]], Verification: Groth16's equation on `@noble/curves`' BN254 pairing; signals never reduced; root 0 refused before pairing.
- `Poseidon2`: `@noble/curves`' permutation with constants from its Grain generator, equal to circomlibjs's published constants (t = 3); no constant table in the package.
- The tier tree: root, paths and the zero chain, and `replayTierGroup`, the lenient replay of Commitments and removals. The group registry's groups replay strictly through `applyGroupRecords`, whose leaves `tierTreeRoot` takes.
- `abolish/v1/membership-vk` is specified ([[domain-separation]]); `circuit.json` already listed its field element.

Tested:

- Vendored and re-fetched in CI: `semaphore/verification-keys.json` (v4.14.3), circomlibjs `poseidon_constants.json` and `test/poseidon.js` (v0.1.7), circomlib `test/poseidoncircuit.js` (v2.0.5). The pinned key equals the depth-32 entry point for point; the Grain constants equal circomlibjs's; Poseidon2 reproduces circomlib's three known answers at t = 3.
- `docs/spec/vectors/membership-proof.json`, made outside the workspace by `scripts/membership-proof-vectors.mjs` with Semaphore 4.14.3's `generateProof` (and snarkjs's `fullProve` for witnesses `generateProof` refuses), every verdict from snarkjs 0.7.5: two honest proofs over tier trees (one sparse, after zero leaves), a re-randomised proof (valid, other bytes), tampered A and C, A and C swapped, a wrong nullifier, message, root and scope (tier 1's scope over tier 0's root), swapped signals, signals plus `r`, a proof under the depth-1 key, a root-0 proof (which snarkjs accepts and Abolish rejects) and a hidden-subtree proof; 12 rejected encodings (lengths, coordinates at or above `p`, off-curve, identity, B's component order, B outside the subgroup). The generator checks the artifacts' sha256s and that both zkeys' embedded keys equal the vendored ones. The real proofs tie `F` to Semaphore's own `hash`, which P1-17 checked only against Keccak known answers.
- `docs/spec/vectors/tier-tree.json`, from an independent Python Poseidon over the vendored constants (`scripts/tier-tree-vectors.py`): Poseidon2 answers, `Z_0` to `Z_32`, 11 replayed groups (forbidden commitments `0`, `1`, `Poseidon2(0, 1)`, `r`, `2²⁵⁶ − 1`; a tier with only forbidden additions and one fully removed, both root `Z_32`; stray removals of an index not yet added, a removed leaf and a far index; a freed index never reused; a repeated commitment) and 17 member paths.
- fast-check: any changed byte of an honest proof or any changed signal is rejected; random 256-byte strings are rejected or re-encode to themselves; paths climb to the root and other leaves' don't; the root changes with any leaf and not with trailing empty leaves; removals and stray removals.
- Mutations: removing the root-0 check or the signal-range check fails the tests.
- `vp check` clean; `packages/crypto` (1700+ tests) and `packages/verifier` pass; CI's `gated` and `vectors-provenance` steps pass locally.

Known-weak:

- Zero knowledge against a subverted Semaphore ceremony isn't established until part 2 publishes the reference-string check (Fuchsbauer, PKC 2018) of the pinned `semaphore-32.zkey` (T-07, T-12).
- The proving key is pinned by sha256 from `snark-artifacts.pse.dev`, which isn't a vendored or re-fetched source; its embedded verification key was checked against Semaphore's published one when the vectors were made.
- The real proofs are randomised, so the vector file can't be regenerated byte for byte; it is the record.
- The verifier (P1-19) doesn't call these yet: definition checks 1.3 and 2.4 and ballot check 5.1 wire them in.
- The vendored iden3 files are GPL-3.0 test data; nothing from them is built into the package (the constants come from noble's generator).

Independent review (in-session subagent, `crypto-review` checklist): nothing blocking. It confirmed the pairing equation and signal order against the real proofs, noble's subgroup check on G2 (the outside-subgroup vector is rejected by it), the explicit identity rejection (noble's `assertValidity` accepts the identity on BN254), the Grain constants and the tier tree against the spec, and the vectors' independence. Fixed: `tierTreePath` no longer uses `^ 1`, which wraps above 2³¹; the leaf bound's comment says arrays stop at 2³² − 1; the spec's B-order sentence now says "fails decoding or verification"; and the on-chain hand-off (P1-18) now requires contracts to reject the identity in `A`, `B` and `C`, which EIP-197 and stock verifiers accept, so both verifiers accept exactly the same proofs. Carried to part 2: re-fetch the zkey and check its sha256 reproducibly, since zero knowledge rests on that file.
