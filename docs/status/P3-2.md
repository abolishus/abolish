# P3-2: Semaphore groups per tier

- State: in progress (part 1, the group registry with Tier 0 additions and removals, in review in #104; Tier 1 and Tier 2 addition paths and the registration paymaster follow)
- Branch: `claude/p3-2`
- Phase: Phase 3: Tier 2 ZK identity, Semaphore groups, indexer, per-tier results

## Scope

Semaphore groups per tier on L2, and a test that no on-chain data links a member to any poll.

## Plan

1. **The group registry and Tier 0** (this part): [[contracts]], Group registry. Groups whose tier is fixed at creation; Tier 0 additions and removals approved by a threshold of registrars pinned at deployment (EIP-712, [[0004-l2-choice]]), each signed batch bound to its group, tier and position; forbidden commitments refused ([[membership]], Commitments and removals); every record logged whole through the event accumulator so verifier 2.4 rebuilds tier trees from the chain. `packages/crypto` decodes the records and checks the approvals, for the verifier and the signing service.
2. **Tier 1 additions**, with [[0014-tier1-vouching]] (P3-3 parts 2 and 3), once Semaphore proofs verify (P1-17b).
3. **Tier 2 additions**, with document proofs (P3-4, [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]).
4. **Registration sponsorship**: our ERC-4337 paymaster ([[0004-l2-choice]], Paymaster), and the Tier 0 signing service in `packages/core` (refusing forbidden and duplicate commitments).

No group registry may be deployed before parts 2 and 3 exist: a deployed registry's rules can't change.

## Part 1: the group registry and Tier 0

Built:

- [[contracts]], Group registry: the spec, with two departures from [[0004-l2-choice]] listed under Decisions to review (registrars pinned at deployment rather than read from the Safe; dedicated keys, never the Safe's owners). [[verifier]] 2.4 names the registrars' approvals for Tier 0.
- `packages/contracts/src/GroupRegistry.sol`: `createGroup(uint8)` (Tier 0 only for now, `group_id` a counter up to 2⁶⁴ − 1), `addMembers(bytes)` and `removeMembers(bytes)` with canonical calldata and exact lengths, forbidden commitments refused, each batch bound to its group, tier and position (a removal to the member count it saw), and every approval checked (v ∈ {27, 28}, `r` and `s` in range, low-s, then OpenZeppelin's `ECDSA.tryRecover`). Every event goes through the event accumulator and carries the whole record. No owner, proxy, pause or tree. OpenZeppelin 5.6.1's `ECDSA.sol` is vendored unmodified.
- `packages/crypto/src/group-registry.ts`: strict decoders and encoders for both records, the EIP-712 domain separator and digests, `isForbiddenCommitment`, and `checkGroupApprovals` against a pinned registrar set (`@noble/curves` secp256k1 recovery, `@noble/hashes` Keccak), for verifier 2.4 and the signing service.
- `docs/spec/vectors/group-registry.json`, printed by `packages/crypto/scripts/group-registry-vectors.py`, an independent standard-library implementation that first reproduces EIP-712's own example and RFC 6979's secp256k1 signature.
- Threat files T-06, T-12, T-13, T-17 and T-62.

Tested:

- Both implementations against every vector: domain, type and struct hashes, digests, each approval's signer, and every rejected record with its error and seat. The TypeScript tests reproduce every signature byte for byte (RFC 6979).
- Contract: a test per refusal, stateful ones included (unknown group, wrong position, the 2³² leaf bound, the group-id bound, a removal's size and indices, replays), fuzzing of one-bit flips, arbitrary bytes and signatures by other keys, an invariant suite with a shadow model, the PII checks of the contracts skill, and a test that runs an election through the election registry alongside and finds no group-registry calldata or event carrying its identifier or definition hash.
- `packages/crypto`: fast-check round trips, refusal of arbitrary bytes and of every single-bit flip of a signed record, and a test per check showing that removing it lets a bad record through (where `@noble/curves` itself refuses the same input, the check is kept as defence in depth).

Known-weak (also in [[contracts]], Group registry, Known-weak):

- Tier 0 is the registrars' word (T-06): any `t` of them can add any commitments; the registry makes that attributable and unreplayable, not checked.
- The registrar quorum is held online by the signing service (T-69); dedicated keys keep the blast radius to Tier 0.
- Registrars can't rotate without a new registry and new groups.
- Duplicate commitments and the batch queue are the signing service's to manage (part 4).
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 have no addition path yet; no registry may be deployed until they do.
- `Poseidon2(0, 1)` comes from `poseidon-lite` 0.3.0 run outside the repository, not from our own Poseidon; P1-17b must check it.

## Independent review (part 1)

A fresh in-session subagent reviewed the diff against `.github/review/crypto-review.md`, reran the generator (identical vectors), fetched `poseidon-lite` 0.3.0 and OpenZeppelin 5.6.1 itself to confirm `Poseidon2(0, 1)` and the vendored `ECDSA.sol`, and ran both test suites. Nothing blocking. Findings and dispositions:

- **Non-blocking, fixed:** the registrars are in storage, outside verifier 1.2's bytecode check, so a registry deployed from release bytecode with other keys would accept additions verifiers don't count, and its removals would then zero other members' leaves in the verifier's numbering. The verifier now fails every group of a registry whose `RegistrarsPinned` differs from its pinned set (Verification; verifier 2.4).
- **Non-blocking, fixed:** registrar keys held online for automatic registration must not be the Safe's owners, or seizing the registration service would seize the anchoring authority and timelocks. Registrars are dedicated keys (Deployment), a second departure from [[0004-l2-choice]] under Decisions to review.
- **Non-blocking, fixed:** a removal signed for an index not yet reached stayed valid for good and could later remove whoever landed there. Removals now sign `size`, the member count when signed; the registry requires `size` at or below the count and every index below `size`, with tests that such a removal can't reach later members.
- **Non-blocking, fixed:** T-17's residual named registrars as choosing commitments; registrants do (about 253 bits each, on L2 for good), and registrars choose approval nonces.
- **Nits, fixed:** the verifier's counting rule now matches the code (every approval verifies); the immutables verifier 1.2 compares are listed; "will be specified"; the signing service signs one batch per position and submits its own records.
- **Re-review of the fixes:** all resolved, nothing blocking. Its three nits: a test that the removal checks run in the spec's order (added), verifier 2.4 stating the pinned-registrar rule itself (added), and the removal gas figure (re-measured: about 86,000 per call excluding base and calldata, as stated).
