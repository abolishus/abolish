# T-12: Cross-poll linkability

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: a published Benaloh opening (opt-in, sent after close, canonically ordered) can still be linked to its voter through network metadata (T-11), and usually shows the voter's real choice ([[verifier]] check 6.2).

## Threat

Nullifiers, identity commitments or proof data link one voter's ballots across polls.

## Mitigation

Semaphore nullifiers are scoped per poll and unlinkable without the secret; ballots carry no stable identifier (P1-17). A test asserts no on-chain data links a member to any poll (P3-2).
