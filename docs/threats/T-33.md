# T-33: Re-vote resolution manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Needs design** (P1-13): [[verifier]] check 6.1 states the requirements, and no rule meeting all of them is specified yet.

## Threat

The server or a verifier picks a ballot other than the voter's last one.

## Mitigation

Re-vote resolution is a deterministic rule that meets the requirements in [[verifier]] check 6.1, including independence from the order of unanchored entries, specified in `docs/spec/` and implemented once in `packages/crypto` or `packages/verifier` (P1-8, P1-13). Depends on T-38 holding.
