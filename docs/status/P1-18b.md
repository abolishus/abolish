## Scope

[[tally]] (Tally window) defines `s_S`, the counted extent's last segment, and `t_S`, when its checkpoint is due, from `W_S`, and every term of `W_S` is followed by the **contract-event window** `W_C`: one summed constant of the immutable contracts the definition names, in which a contract event goes from submission to the board. [[P1-18]] was to fix it and closed without doing so ([[P1-18]], Part 4c-2b-2a, Known-weak: "`W_C` itself is still to be fixed by the contracts"), so `s_S` and `t_S` were unknown everywhere, and only their floors were used:

- the trustee node's tally can't read its counted set ([[P2-13]] part 5b-2b), so no seat opens a cell, and with no published tally the everyday results ([[P2-25]], [[P2-24]] part 4, [[P2-9]], [[P2-14]] part 8c) wait;
- the verifier's 4.6 point 2, 5.6, 6.2's `opening-late` and the seal can't be decided ([[P1-19]], [[P4-3]]);
- the release's forced-path windows took `W_C` as 13 hours, which [[tally]]'s own sum (forced inclusion, missed slots, L1 finality, boarding) doesn't fit.

## Part 1 (#641)

- **The window** ([[parameters]], [[tally]], Tally window): `CONTRACT_EVENT_WINDOW = 50,400` seconds, the sum [[tally]] names: forced inclusion at `MAX_SEQUENCING_WINDOW` (3,600 L1 blocks of 12 seconds, 43,200), an hour of missed slots (3,600; with the first term, `F`), half an hour of L1 finality (1,800) and `BOARDING_WINDOW` (1,800), the operator's time to board an event once derivable from finalized L1, by which [[tally]]'s `tally-late` is attributed. Both are `public constant`s of `ElectionRegistry`'s bytecode beside the sequencing ceiling they're sized from, so verifier 1.2's binding of its code fixes them for every election it registers ([[contracts]], Registration, rule 6); nothing on chain reads them.
- **The counted extent**: under every record version so far the complaint window is the latest that must close before the counted set is fixed, so `W_S = COMPLAINT_WINDOW + W_C` (482,400 seconds), `s_S = max(period(closes_at) + 1, period(closes_at + l1_inclusion_bound + W_S))` and `t_S = epoch + (s_S + 1) · period + deadline`. `packages/crypto`'s `countedExtent` computes both; `CONTRACT_EVENT_WINDOW` is exported beside `COMPLAINT_WINDOW`.
- **The delivery windows** ([[delivery]], Direct submit, Capacity) sized with a 13-hour `W_C` now take 14: they still fit (40 of `DELIVERY_WINDOW`'s 48 hours and 28 of the 72 after it); the caps at `R` of one hour and of nine are unchanged, those at 21 hours fall slightly (425,216 members with 3 seats, from 434,688). `apps/trustee-node`'s `RELEASE_WINDOWS` takes the constant, so a seat waits an hour longer before complaining, and `delivery-obligation.json` is regenerated with it.
- The spec's residuals that blamed P1-18 now name the verifier ([[P1-19]]) as what doesn't compute `s_S` yet; [[election-definition]]'s seal cites [[parameters]] for the post-finality window's justification.

Tested: `docs/spec/vectors/tally-window.json` from `packages/crypto/scripts/tally-window-vectors.py`, an independent Python implementation that finds each moment's period by walking the clock rather than dividing (the deployed clock, a period boundary and the second before it, the close at the epoch, the floor binding with the window's end inside and at the edge of close's period, one-second periods, values beyond 2^53); fast-check properties that the extent holds every record in time and boarded within `W_C` and the receipted ballots of client rule 1, is the least such segment, is at least `s_K`, and never moves earlier for a later close or longer bound; the input refusals; `PARAMETERS` against [[parameters]]; the window as the sum of its terms in TypeScript and in Foundry (`test_ContractEventWindowIsItsTerms`); the delivery and capacity tests at the deployed windows (T-25, T-34, T-35, T-50, T-53, T-54).

Known-weak:

- Nothing reads `s_S` yet: the trustee node's tally ([[P2-13]] part 5b-2b), the verifier's 4.6 point 2, 5.6, 6.2's `opening-late` and its seal ([[P1-19]], [[P4-3]]) still use the floors or a bundle-supplied clock, and `packages/sdk`'s simulation still takes `s_S` at its floor ([[P1-21]]).
- The window bounds settlement, it doesn't guarantee it: an L1 that stops finalizing for more than about half an hour, or more than 300 missed slots in a forced-inclusion window, fails an election `tally-late`, unattributed (T-50, T-53). Nothing measures missed slots or finality over a poll.
- `BOARDING_WINDOW` is a commitment of the operator's, sized by judgement (30 minutes), not measured.
- `W_S` is the complaint window's under every version specified today; a later record version whose window ends later must say so, and `countedExtent` takes no version: one that does must take the pinned versions or refuse those it doesn't know, or it gives too early an `s_S`.
- `tally-late`'s attribution compares when an event became derivable from _finalized_ L1 with the end of `s_S` less `BOARDING_WINDOW`, and when L1 finalized a block isn't in execution-layer data: [[P1-19]] must define how a verifier measures it (beacon finality checkpoints, or conservatively the batch's L1 block time plus the finality term) before it attributes one.
