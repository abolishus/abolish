# P2-20: The ballot client's UI from the design mockups

- State: in progress (part 1, the frame in both registers and the current screens restyled, merged in #237; part 2, the check screens, merged in #238; part 3, the question screens as components, next)
- Branch: `claude/p2-20`
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

Build the ballot client's frame and the screens it can already show from the mockups in `apps/ballot/src/mockups/` (Storybook `Mockups/Ballot/…`), so [[P2-8]] part 2b's election screens and later parts land on a finished design rather than restyling afterwards. [[language]] is binding; on the ballot itself the Center for Civic Design rules are a Keep: instructions above options, full-width targets, a selected state with a check and words, no pre-selection by us, plain messages for an undervote or overvote, and no motion on choosing, reviewing or casting.

Rules for every part:

- **The bundle allow-list holds.** Only `react`, `@abolishus/ui` (its root export, or primitives built on Base UI, `cva`, `cn` and `lucide-react` only), `lucide-react` and `@abolishus/crypto`. `tests/bundle.test.ts` and the CI copy of its policy must stay green; anything else is an owner-reviewed change.
- **CSP-safe.** The client's CSP has `style-src 'self'`, which blocks inline `style` attributes; the screens and any kit component they use must not set one.
- **No hard-coded strings.** Screens take text from `apps/ballot/src/messages.ts`.
- **No network in this item.** Fetching is P2-8 part 2b's, behind its `connect-src` change; these parts render from what the client already has (the origin check, the worker's election check) or from props.
- **Tested like the kit.** Each screen has a story with a play function passing axe at desktop width and 360px; a mockup story is deleted once its real screen matches it.

## Plan

Each part is one PR on this branch's name. `apps/ballot` needs the independent in-session `crypto-review` subagent review (AGENTS.md, Before opening any PR, step 5).

1. **The frame and the current screens.** The everyday frame (`everyday-frame.tsx`: top bar with the poll's seal and the client's release, "Question n of m", the bottom action bar) and the civic frame (`frame.tsx`), chosen by the poll's class once definitions carry it; the existing "what this client is" screen and the refusal on a shared origin (`welcome.stories.tsx`, Origin refused), restyled.
2. **The check screens.** The arrival check ("This is the real poll, checked") and the election-check refusal (`welcome.stories.tsx`, Election check failed; `everyday-arrive.stories.tsx`), driven by the worker's election check from P2-8 part 2a: each refusal names the failed check in words, with the two fingerprints side by side.
3. **The question screens as components.** Pick one, pick any, up to N and exactly N (`ballot.stories.tsx`, `everyday-questions.stories.tsx`) and "Check your answers" (`everyday-check.stories.tsx`), taking a decoded election and returning the voter's selections, with the skip and over/undervote rules of [[0026-multi-question-ballots]]. P2-8 part 2b wires them to fetched elections; sealing and sending stay P2-8 part 3's.

Later, as their protocol work lands: sending, Done and the receipt (`everyday-done.stories.tsx`, `receipt.stories.tsx`), the first-answer journey ([[first-answer]], after [[P2-17]]), recovery (`recovery.stories.tsx`), sharing and "your place on the board".

## What was built (part 1, #237)

- `apps/ballot/src/frame.tsx`: `EverydayFrame` (the default register: the poll's seal, who asked and when it closes, "Question n of m" with a bar hidden from assistive technology, one task, the bottom action bar under soft rules) and `CivicFrame` (the civic record: the election's short name, fingerprint and hash in the evidence face, "Step n of m" and the step's name, heavier rules), ported from `mockups/everyday-frame.tsx` and `mockups/frame.tsx` with props in place of fixtures and every string in `messages.ts`; `Frame` chooses by the poll's class (definition version 6's `poll_class`: organisational votes and civic referendums take the civic record, everyday polls and an unknown class the everyday register); `ScreenTitle`, `Part`, `Note` and `ACTION`.
- `app.tsx`: the "what this client is" screen and the shared-origin refusal restyled in the everyday frame. The refusal shows the refused address (origin and path only, never the query or fragment, which a ballot link may use to carry the poll), that nothing was sent, and where to open the ballot instead; no button.
- Stories: `Ballot/Frame` (everyday and civic, both themes, no class yet, no poll) and the app's three, each with a play function; axe at desktop width and 360px.
