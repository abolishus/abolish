# P1-15: Summed shares, tally and destruction

- State: in progress (part 1 merged in #55; part 2a merged in #87; part 2b, the summed-share message, merged in #93 and its partition follow-up in #100; part 3, destruction, in #103, with board-entry version 4 now that P1-12 part 3b (#101) has merged; part 4 to follow)
- Branch: `claude/p1-15`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Per-trustee summed shares checked against the public VSS commitments, tally combination and the opening of the aggregate commitment (only over the cells of one partition), the tally transcript, and signed destruction of keys and stored shares after the tally.

## Plan

The item is built in parts, each its own PR on this branch's name:

1. **Summed shares and cell openings** (merged in #55): [[tally]] and [[0015-tally-publication]]. The cell aggregates, each trustee's summed shares and their check, the combination of the `k` lowest passing seats, the published opening and its checks, and coarser totals. Needs only P1-12 part 1's committed vote and shares.
2. **The opening record and the messages**, in two PRs:
   - **2a, the opening record** (merged in #87): the tally opening (`0x0006`, version 1), one cell's opening signed by the trustee that combined it under `abolish/v1/sig/tally-opening`; which openings are a cell's and which are stray; attribution by the registered signing keys; the per-cell checks over several openings (`tally-conflict` included); the tally window; [[verifier]] 7.3 and 7.4; retiring `abolish/v1/fs/tally-share` and `abolish/v1/tally-transcript`.
   - **2b, the summed-share message** (merged in #93): the private message carrying a trustee's summed shares to each other trustee, encrypted under P1-12 part 2's hybrid KEM to the recipient's registered KEM key and signed with the sender's per-election key.
3. **Destruction** (in progress): each trustee's signed statement, on the board, that it destroyed its per-election KEM key and stored share ciphertexts once the tally is anchored ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], Consequences; T-14, T-69). Its board admission is board-entry version 4: #101 merged first and its vectors froze version 3, so the statement takes a version of its own.
4. **Regional cells** ([[verifier]] 2.8, T-16): per-region roots, the size floor in [[parameters]] and a later definition version. Waits on the owner's answer to the small-cell question in [[0006-trustees]]; until then the partition is the tiers only.

## What was built (part 1)

- [[0015-tally-publication]] (`Accepted (agent) — owner may veto`): option C, the tally publishes only each cell's opening; trustees exchange summed shares privately. Rejected: publishing summed shares (A, ADR 0002 as written: leaks ballots with fewer than `k` posted shares to a future adversary; narrowing ADR 0002 is the owner's) and re-randomising them with zero-sharings (B: keeps public attribution, but adds an interactive round with its own agreement and liveness problems).
- [[tally]], a new spec page: aggregates `Ê_{c,j,t}` (identity for an empty cell); summed shares `(V_{c,i,j}, R_{c,i,j})`, sent only to other trustees; the trustees' summed-share check; `Q` = the `k` lowest passing seats and Lagrange at 0 (`tally-quorum` while fewer than `k` pass); the published opening and the verifier's checks `tally-missing`, `tally-opening` and `tally-range`, which fail the election; coarser totals derived by adding cells, never opened; residuals.
- [[verifier]] 7.1, 7.2, 7.4 and 8.4 follow it; the spec and vectors READMEs list it; [[STATUS]] lists the ADR under Decisions to review.
- `packages/crypto/src/tally.ts`: `aggregateCell`, `sumShares`, `checkSummedShares`, `lagrangeAtZero`, `openCell` (a trustee's combination) and `checkOpening` (the verifier's check), `deriveTotal`.
- Threat files T-14, T-15, T-16, T-29, T-54.

## Tested (part 1)

- `docs/spec/vectors/tally.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`packages/crypto/scripts/tally-vectors.py`, ballots from `ballot-validity-vectors.py`; openings found by Gaussian elimination over every coefficient and cross-checked against the Lagrange coefficients): 11 cells (honest plurality; approval with two excluded trustees; a quorum failure with a sum missing a ballot, swapped options and an absent seat; high seats only; empty; one ballot; `MAX_TRUSTEES`; `MAX_OPTIONS`; a vote of 2 failing `tally-range`; two tiers of one election), 16 published openings checked by the verifier (every valid cell's opening; a count or randomness off by one and swapped options, `tally-opening`; short, missing and a count encoded as `V + ℓ`, `tally-missing`; the vote-of-2 cell's, `tally-range`) and a derived overall total. - fast-check properties (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): honest sums from any `k` or more seats open each cell to its counts, and any other `k` seats interpolate to the same opening; any changed summed share fails and is excluded; a sum over the cell minus any one ballot fails for every seat.
- Mutation checks by hand: dropping the summed-share check or the range check fails tests. An honest combination always passes `tally-opening` (an identity in the exponent), so the combiner's copy of that check is reached only by the verifier's vectors.

## Known-weak (part 1)

- **Nothing sends, posts or signs summed shares or openings yet** (part 2), so 7.3 and 7.4 and signer attribution aren't implemented.
- **A corrupt trustee sees every seat's summed share** ([[tally]], Residuals; T-14, T-15): wherever posted shares fall into the pattern (a relay or trustees' downtime) and with a later break of their classical encryption, it can complete a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares, or disclose the sums unattributably; a seized trustee yields the same. Not mitigated; [[0015-tally-publication]] option B would close it only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1`.
- **Ballots readable after a break shrink a cell's anonymity set**: [[verifier]] 8.2 now reports the number of counted ballots with fewer than `k` valid posted shares per cell, for P1-19 to implement.
- **Several openings per cell** are specified (`tally-conflict`), but `checkOpening` takes one; part 2 implements the rule with the transcript.
- **A wrong summed share isn't publicly attributable**, only known to the trustees; a wrong opening is (T-29).
- **No published vectors exist for this composition.** The independent implementation and the reviews carry the weight (T-36).
- **Scalar arithmetic isn't constant time.** Summing and interpolation use JavaScript `bigint` on a trustee's machine; per-ballot shares are summed, not branched on.

## Known-weak (part 3)

- **Board-entry version 3 is never usable by a plurality or approval election.** Its vectors froze it before the statement could join it, so version 4 repeats its set plus `0x000f`, and 2.3 requires version 4 wherever tally-opening version 1 is pinned. Versions are append-only, so version 3 stays decodable.
- **The profile combination isn't enforced yet:** [[verifier]] 2.3's `profile-combination` waits for P1-19, and the election registry's refusal for P1-18.
- **Destruction can't be proven** ([[tally]], Residuals; T-14, T-40, T-69): `destroyed` reports a signed claim, nothing more.
- **The operator can withhold a statement**, and `destruction-missing` can't tell that from the seat's silence; there is no contract path, since no result depends on a statement.
- **The destruction segment needs `s_T`**, which needs `t_S`, which needs the windows P1-12 part 3, P1-13 and P1-18 still specify; `checkDestruction` takes it as an input until P1-19 computes it.
- **A trustee that destroyed at `t_D` can't combine a late tally**; it still resends openings it already signed, which count until the seal (T-54).
- **No trustee node implements destruction yet** (P1-21 or later); this part specifies it and gives the library its statement and report.

## What was built (part 2b)

- [[tally]], Summed-share message: record type `0x000b`, version 1 (`definition_hash`, `cell`, `aggregate_hash`, `sender`, `recipient`, `ct_mlkem`, `ct_dh`, `sealed`, `signature`), one message per cell, sender and recipient. The summed shares are sealed under [[share-encryption]]'s hybrid KEM (ML-KEM-768 and ristretto255, X-Wing's combiner, ChaCha20-Poly1305) to the recipient's registered KEM key, under new tags `abolish/v1/kem/summed-share` and `abolish/v1/kem/summed-share-aad`, so a ballot's share ciphertext and a summed-share message never open as each other; the AEAD's associated data binds the record type and version, election, cell, counted set and both seats. The sender signs the whole record but the signature, header included, with its per-election signing key under `abolish/v1/sig/summed-share`. No profile pins the type: tally opening version 1 fixes summed-share message version 1, so [[election-definition]]'s `profile-types` rule is unchanged.
- The recipient's checks, in order: `summed-malformed`, `summed-context` (election, partition cell, itself as recipient, a registered sender other than itself, the counted set it computed), `summed-signature`, `summed-decrypt`, `summed-check`. Context and signature come before decapsulation, so only a registered seat can make a trustee decapsulate a chosen ciphertext (T-39). No proof of knowledge of the ephemeral: nothing a recipient derives is ever revealed. The signature gives no formal public proof of a wrong sum (that needs the recipient's ML-KEM secret), though publishing a message's `K` would give convincing evidence; no such complaint is defined, since it would publish per-seat values, so wrong summed shares stay attributed only among the trustees (T-29). Trustee nodes exchange messages over hybrid post-quantum TLS.
- [[versioning]] registry row `0x000b`; [[board]]: the envelope refuses it (it was already outside the allowed set); [[domain-separation]]: the three tags; [[trustee-identity]] and [[share-encryption]] point at the message; `circuit.json` gains the new tags' field elements.
- `packages/crypto/src/summed-share.ts`: `sealSummedShares` (takes the key set `checkCeremonyTranscript` returned for the definition's transcript, refusing any other, and looks the recipient's KEM key up by seat, refuses an unregistered recipient, a sender not registered under its signing key, sums failing the sender's own check, and a message to itself), `openSummedShares`, `encodeSummedShareMessage`, `decodeSummedShareMessage`. `RECORD_TYPES.summedShareMessage`.
- Threat files T-15, T-29 and T-39.

## Known-weak (part 2b)

- **The transport between trustee nodes is specified only as hybrid post-quantum TLS**: a store that keeps messages after delivery becomes a harvest target (T-15). The node software (P1-21 or later) must delete them.
- **Recipients' ML-KEM isn't constant time** ([[share-encryption]], Residuals). Only registered seats reach decapsulation through this message, but the same key serves share intake, which anyone can reach: a key recovered by timing there also exposes recorded summed-share messages to a later discrete-log break alone (T-39, T-15).
- **A frozen vector's description is now stale:** `identity-backup.json`'s `decode-type-unassigned` calls `0x000b` unassigned. Its verdict still holds (the identity-backup decoder doesn't know the type), and frozen vectors are never edited. ## What was built (part 2a)
