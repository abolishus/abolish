# T-19: Third-party proof app privacy

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-1, A-2
- Status: **Needs decision** (P3-1).

## Threat

The Tier 2 proof apps (zkPassport, Self) see the document; a malicious or compromised app leaks it or links it to the identity commitment.

## Mitigation

ADR compares apps and requires client-side proving (P3-1); our native NFC plugin later removes the dependency (P5-3).
