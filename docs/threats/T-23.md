# T-23: Board tampering

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: entries added since the last anchor can be dropped or reordered undetectably by anyone but the voter holding a receipt, for up to an hour.

## Threat

An insider alters, drops or reorders board entries.

## Mitigation

Entries are hash-chained and committed to by an append-only Merkle tree (RFC 9162), and each names its election and position ([[board]], P1-16); the app's DB role has no UPDATE or DELETE, enforced by grants and triggers (P2-1); checkpoints (size, chain head and root after each anchoring period with entries) are anchored hourly to L1 (P4-1) and the board is mirrored to IPFS (P4-3); the verifier recomputes the chain and every anchored checkpoint from the entries, and requires every segment with entries to be anchored (P1-19, [[verifier]] 4.1 and 4.2).

Built so far (P1-16): the envelope codec, entry and checkpoint hashes, the tree with inclusion and consistency proofs, and the board checks, in `packages/crypto`, tested against `docs/spec/vectors/board.json` (from an independent Python implementation) and transparency-dev's RFC 6962 vectors. Not yet: storage (P2-1), anchoring (P4-1) and the verifier that runs the checks (P1-19).
