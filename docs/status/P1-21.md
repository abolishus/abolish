# P1-21: Simulation surfaces in admin-cli and sdk

- State: in progress (part 1 merged in #PR; part 2, the reference election's stages under simulation, next, after [[P1-20]])
- Branch: `claude/p1-21`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

`apps/admin-cli` and `packages/sdk` surfaces for simulations (for example "10,000 voters, 7 trustees, 2 offline, mixed tiers, servers down halfway").

## Plan

1. **Scenario, plan and the in-memory tally simulation** (this part). `@abolishus/sdk`: `parseScenario` (strict JSON: voters, plurality or approval, options, panel, threshold, offline and faulty seats, tier weights, a server outage as a share of the voting period, a seed), `planElection` (who votes how, in which tier and when, from the seed; the expected counts per tier) and `runSimulation`, which runs every ballot through `@abolishus/crypto`'s sharing, share encryption, validity proof and each online seat's intake, then each tier's cell through the summed shares, `openCell` and the verifier's `checkOpening`, and reports each cell against the plan. `apps/admin-cli`: `simulation plan` and `simulation run`, which need no database.
2. **The reference election's stages under simulation**, once [[P1-20]] lands its runner: a registered definition and the signed ceremony, membership proofs, the board and receipts at cast, Benaloh challenges and re-voting, the direct-submit path for ballots cast during an outage, anchoring on Anvil, destruction, wipe and rebuild, and the verifier over the output. Also seats that go offline partway, and running intake in worker threads.

## Tested

- `packages/sdk`: property tests that every well-formed scenario parses unchanged through JSON and that the plan is reproducible, covers every voter once in time order, puts voters only in weighted tiers, expects exactly the planned votes and sends a voter direct exactly during the outage; refusals of unknown, missing and out-of-range fields with their paths; runs that open every cell to the plan with an offline seat, exclude a faulty seat's summed shares and still open, report `tally-quorum` when fewer than `k` honest seats remain, and open an empty cell to zero.
- `apps/admin-cli`: `simulation run` and `simulation plan` without a database (the test's connection throws if opened), their agreement, and usage errors for malformed scenarios.

## Known-weak

- Every report lists its stubs (`SIMULATION_STUBS`): a random definition hash, per-election keys without the ceremony rounds, nullifiers that are voter indices with no membership proofs, in-memory delivery with no board or receipts, so a direct ballot differs from a server one only in its label, and no challenges, re-voting, anchoring, destruction or rebuild. Part 2 replaces them with the reference election's stages.
- A run costs about 0.3 s per ballot on one core at 7 seats, mostly each online seat's intake, so the brief's 10,000 voters take about an hour.
- Offline seats are down for the whole election, and faulty seats lie only in their summed shares.
