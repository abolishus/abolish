# T-05: Unsound circuit or proof verification

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-9, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: soundness rests on review and tests, not formal verification.

## Threat

An under-constrained Noir circuit or a verifier bug accepts a forged membership or nullifier proof.

## Mitigation

Membership uses Semaphore v4's published circuit unchanged, so we write no membership constraints ([[0009-membership-proof]]). Groth16 verification over `@noble/curves`' BN254 pairing under Semaphore's pinned depth-32 key, with strict proof decoding (canonical coordinates, on the curve, in the order-`r` subgroup, G2 included), signals never reduced modulo `r`, and real Semaphore proofs, tampered proofs and signals, another depth's key and the root-0 proof as vectors ([[membership]], Verification; P1-17b); verifier negative tests and `crypto-review` on every change (P1-19). Our own Noir circuits (Tier 2, P3-1) get soundness tests (tampered witnesses must fail) and proof round-trips through bb. Residual: membership soundness also rests on BN254 (about 100–110 bits).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): membership moves to Semaphore v4's own circuit ([[0009-membership-proof]]), so the under-constrained-circuit risk for membership is in Semaphore's published circuit rather than ours; verification code is P1-17b's.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): Semaphore's circuit hashes leaves and inner nodes alike and leaves the path length unconstrained; the tier tree (complete, depth 32, rebuilt by verifiers) and the root-0 rule are what keep that from admitting extra identities ([[membership]]).
- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): Tier 2 soundness rests on zkPassport's circuits, which have no public audit yet, and on passive authentication only. The verifier pins the inner-circuit set itself rather than accepting zkPassport's circuit registry root ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-17b): Groth16 verification, the pinned key and its hash, the proof encoding and the tier tree are implemented in `packages/crypto` and checked against real Semaphore proofs with snarkjs's verdicts.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4b-3a): the board contract's on-chain check of membership proofs, over the EVM's BN254 precompiles with the pinned depth-32 key as constants and the same strict decoding, run against every vector the verifier runs ([[0028-board-contract-checks]]; [[contracts]], Board). On chain, soundness also rests on the L2's `ecPairing`, including its G2 subgroup check; the verifier still checks every proof with `@noble/curves`.
- 2026-10-17 (P3-4 part 2b-3b-2b-2a): the Tier 2 query and output circuits have committed artifacts and verification keys, rebuilt and compared by a test and by `repro-build`, pinned by sha256 in [[nullifier-query]] (Keys and proofs), with the proof length (14,656 bytes) and bb's target. Proof round trips through bb on every vector: each proof verifies under its own public inputs and fails with any one changed, under the other circuit's key and under the other vector's inputs. The reference string is vendored from Ignition and its structure tested (T-21). The checks `query-proof` and `output-proof` as code that seats and attesters call are part 2b-3b-2b-2b; until then nothing checks these proofs outside the tests.
- 2026-10-18 (P3-4 part 2b-3b-2b-2b): `query-proof` and `output-proof` are code in `packages/circuits` (`checkQuery`, `checkOutput`) that a seat and an attester call: Barretenberg through bb.js under the keys and reference string, each refused unless it has its pinned sha256, with public inputs from the checker's pins and records, never the prover's. A proof of another length or with a non-canonical element is refused before verifying, one Barretenberg aborts on is refused too, and proofs made through bb.js are accepted by the pinned bb binary. Tested on both vectors' queries and outputs, proved afresh to their `B` and `N`. No seat or attester runs them yet: the trustee node's seat comes with P2-13, the attestation with part 2b-4.
- 2026-10-18 (P3-4 part 2b-4d-1, [[identity-chain]], Circuit set): the seats verify zkPassport's sub-proofs directly, so neither its outer aggregation circuit nor its circuit registry root enters soundness; each role's keys are built by us from its source at a pinned commit with our pinned toolchain, under an algorithm floor (RSA of 2048 bits or more, curves of 256 bits or more, no SHA-1). Soundness still rests on zkPassport's unaudited sub-circuits, with the question held for the owner.
