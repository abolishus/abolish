# T-62: Contract admin abuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A single key upgrades or pauses a contract, or rewrites a group.

## Mitigation

Bulletin-board contracts are immutable; anything upgradeable sits behind a Safe multisig plus timelock; no single-key admin anywhere (P1-18).

## History

- 2026-10-12 (P3-2 part 1): the group registry has no admin: its registrars and threshold (at least 2) are fixed at deployment, and only a threshold of them can add or remove a Tier 0 member, each change logged with its signatures ([[contracts]], Group registry). Rotating them means a new registry.
- 2026-10-16 (P3-2 part 4c, [[contracts]], Registration paymaster): the paymaster's admin, the timelock behind our Safe (deployment refuses an admin without code or with only an EIP-7702 delegation), can only withdraw its deposit and manage its stake; what it sponsors (the EntryPoint, registry, service account and cost cap) is immutable, so a new rule means a new paymaster.
- 2026-10-17 (P3-2 part 4d-2b-2a, [[contracts]], Registration account): the signing service's account, the paymaster's only sponsored sender, is ERC-4337's reference `BaseAccount` with one owner key and the EntryPoint fixed at deployment, with no proxy, upgrade entry, delegatecall or owner change, so nothing can later change what its `execute` does; a new key is a new account and paymaster.
- 2026-10-17 (P3-2 part 4d-2b-2b, `apps/worker`, `connectTier0Bundler`): before the batch job submits anything it reads the registration account's EntryPoint and owner and the paymaster's EntryPoint, registry and service, and refuses to start unless they are the configured deployment's, so a paymaster or account swapped in configuration signs nothing.
