# T-22: Tier 1 social graph exposure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1, A-10
- Status: **Needs design** (P3-3).

## Threat

The vouching graph is sensitive personal data, and publishing or storing it links people to each other and to their Tier 1 membership.

## Mitigation

Store only what Tier 1 strictly needs; the vouching design states what is public and what the server learns (P3-3).
