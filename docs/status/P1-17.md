## Scope

Originally "`packages/circuits` scaffold (Noir 1.0.0-beta.22, bb 5.0.0-nightly.20260522): a membership + per-poll nullifier circuit compatible with Semaphore groups, with soundness tests". [[0002-everlasting-privacy]] requires membership proofs to be statistically or perfectly zero-knowledge, and asks this item to show that for bb's zero-knowledge UltraHonk or fall back to Semaphore's Groth16 circuit. [[0009-membership-proof]] takes the fallback, so the item is re-scoped:

- the ADR choosing the proof system, the scope and the tier binding;
- [[membership]]: the statement, public inputs, scope, message and field mappings, tree depth and the verifier's checks;
- `packages/crypto`: the scope, the field mapping and the protocol-wide depth, with cross-language vectors.

The verification keys and Groth16 verification move to [[P1-17b]]. The Noir workspace scaffold, the in-circuit tag-to-field rule and the packing rules move to [[P1-17c]].

Requirement carried from verifier 2.4, 2.8 and 5.3 (P0-ops-8): a ballot's tier is fixed by its membership proof's public inputs. Met by the scope, which binds the tier explicitly (not only its root, since two tiers can have equal roots).

## Known-weak

- No membership proof is verified yet: Groth16 verification, the pinned keys and real Semaphore proofs as vectors are [[P1-17b]].
- The Semaphore source is cited by tag, commit and file hash, not vendored; P1-17b vendors what it verifies against.
- `F` is checked against an independent Keccak and published Keccak answers, not yet against Semaphore's own output (P1-17b).
- Root 0 is the one degenerate root the circuit admits for anyone; any other root needs a real member's secret.
- The tier tree isn't implemented yet (P1-17b), and group contracts (P1-18, P3-2) must expose the additions verifiers rebuild it from.
- Membership soundness rests on Semaphore's ceremony and on BN254 (about 100–110 bits); privacy against a subverted ceremony needs P1-17b's public check of the pinned proving keys, and the prover's use of exactly those keys (P2-8, P5-4).
- One identity in two tier groups can have one ballot counted in each tier (G-2 allows it); results are only ever reported per tier.
- Depth 32 means the largest Semaphore proving key and 32 levels of hashing per proof, whatever the group size; P2-8 and P5-4 measure it on target devices.
- The message `M` is P1-12's and P1-13's to define.
