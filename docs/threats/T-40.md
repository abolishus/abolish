# T-40: DKG manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: custody of per-election KEM keys and of the stored share ciphertexts until they are destroyed after the tally is each trustee's responsibility; T-14 covers collusion.

## Threat

A rogue-key attack, or an adversary biasing the joint key (possible in plain Pedersen/Feldman DKG, per Gennaro, Jarecki, Krawczyk and Rabin), or a trustee leaking or losing a share during or after the ceremony.

## Mitigation

No DKG for plurality and approval: voters share each ballot, so there is no joint key to bias ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); a ranked-choice mixnet that needs a threshold key uses a DKG variant secure against bias (GJKR-style), chosen in its follow-up ADR (P1-14); complaints and disqualification recorded in the public transcript; share custody guidance in a runbook.

## History

- 2026-10-12 (P1-14 part 2, [[0018-ceremony-possession]], [[ceremony]]): the ceremony is key registration with proofs of possession, in four signed, hash-chained rounds; complaints and disqualification are computed by the verifier from the transcript, each attributed to one seat (`possession`, `duplicate-key`, missing messages, `bad-challenge`, `wrong-answer`). Plurality and approval still have no joint key to bias. A ranked-choice DKG, if its ADR needs one, is a new transcript version.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 3, [[tally]], Destruction): the custody of per-election KEM keys and stored share ciphertexts ends at a fixed point: once every cell has a passing opening anchored, or at `t_D` (the later of the tally window's end and the anchoring deadline of the segment before the one containing it, read from L1), each trustee destroys them, with its summed shares, and signs a destruction statement for the board, then destroys its per-election signing key. The statement can't prove destruction (verifier 8.5 reports it only).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 1, [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]]): the Tier 2 nullifier adds the panel's first long-lived joint secret, the OPRF key `sk`, shared by GJKR's first phase (Pedersen VSS, uniform if one dealer is honest; no public key is ever published). It lives for the deployment's Tier 2 scope, with proactive resharing on any panel change; resharing relies on old shares' erasure, so any `k` members of one past epoch keep it, and losing `n − k + 1` shares means re-registering Tier 2.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-1, [[keyed-nullifier]]): the Tier 2 key's share commitments `C_i` are recomputed by anyone from the qualified dealers' coefficient commitments, and an answer is checked against `C_i`, never against a value in the answer. The sharing transcript and its qualified set are part 2b-2's.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-2a, [[nullifier-key]]): the Tier 2 key is dealt by every seat (Pedersen VSS in Baby Jubjub with GJKR's complaints and qualified set, no public key); a seat qualifies only if it deals and answers every complaint with a share that opens its commitments, and `sk` is uniform with one honest qualified dealer, rushing included. An honest seat approves the key only with `max(k, n − 1)` qualified seats and a share that opens its own commitment, and at most one key per Tier 2 scope. Resharing is part 2b-2b.
