# T-72: Poll-creation service misuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-5, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Decided in [[0032-poll-creation-operations]]; built by [[P2-22]] parts 2 to 5. Residual: during launch the operator can refuse any poll, and nothing the author signs binds what is registered to their draft.

## Threat

The operator creates and registers every website poll from its own creation key ([[0032-poll-creation-operations]]), so:

- the operator, or whoever compels it, refuses or silently stalls a poll an author submitted;
- the operator registers a definition that differs from the author's draft (other options, another closing time, another electorate);
- a thief of the creation key spends its balance, creates elections in the operator's name, or registers a definition under an election it created;
- a flood of cheap accounts submits polls to burn the creation key's gas and hold the trustee seats' capacity.

## Mitigation

- **Rule 8 bounds the key.** A definition registers only with a quorum of the pinned panel's signatures over its stub ([[contracts]], Registration, rule 8), and our seats approve only stubs our coordinator hands them, which the worker builds only from submitted drafts. A stolen key can't change a registered definition or register under another creator's identifier (rule 5).
- **The key is kept apart.** One externally owned account for creation only, held only by `worker-create`, never beside a seat's key, with a float of a few days' creations; rotation and compromise in [[poll-creation]] (T-65).
- **The worker checks what it registered.** Before a poll opens, the worker decodes the definition the registry holds and compares it with the draft, and fails the poll on any difference; the poll's page shows the author their question and the registered hash (T-34).
- **Abuse limits.** Account-gated creation, Turnstile on submit, 3 polls in setup and 10 a day per account, and a global daily ceiling checked before any transaction, so a flood spends at most the ceiling's gas a day and holds at most that many seats' slots (T-52).
- **No pre-moderation.** The worker refuses a draft only for the abuse limits or a definition the registry would refuse, each with a plain reason; moderation acts on registered polls only, in the public log with appeals ([[moderation-policy]], T-68), so a refusal for any other reason is the operator's own act, not a policy one.

Residuals:

- During the transitional panel every seat is ours and answers only our coordinator, so the operator can refuse any poll and no one else can get a ceremony from the pinned panel. Independent seats that serve other coordinators would give authors a direct-creation path (the SDK, [[0032-poll-creation-operations]], option C).
- The operator could register a definition other than the author's draft; only the author, comparing the poll's page or the verifier's report with what they wrote, would notice. A passkey assertion over the display-text hash, kept with the creator record, would make it evidence; not built.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 1, [[0032-poll-creation-operations]]): added.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 2): drafts and the submission step are built. A draft is checked by display text version 2's shape rules and definition version 5's question rules from `packages/crypto` before it is stored or submitted, so a poll the registry would refuse is refused at the form with the rule's code; a submission costs a Turnstile solve for `poll-create`, after every cheaper check, and the per-account limits (20 unsubmitted drafts, 3 polls in setup, 10 submissions in any 24 hours) are held under a per-account lock in Postgres rather than Dragonfly, so they are atomic with the state change. The global daily ceiling is the worker's, before the creation transaction (part 3).
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 3a): the creation step is built. Each creation is signed by the creation key and recorded (key, nonce, signed bytes and hash in an append-only ledger that outlives polls and accounts, its hash on the poll's row) before it is sent, so a crash never makes a second creation; the global daily ceiling (`CREATE_DAILY_CEILING`, 200 in any 24 hours) is counted from the ledger under one lock before signing, so deleting accounts frees no slot. A worker holding the key runs only the creation queue and refuses to start beside any other job (rule 2). A creation's nonce taken by another transaction of the key fails its poll as `creation-replaced`, the runbook's sign of compromise. Not mitigated yet: no fee bumping (the fee cap is three base fees ahead), so one stuck creation holds the key's later nonces until it lands; the sweep and status commands are part 3b's.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 3b-1): a created poll goes on only once its identifier passed the creator's per-slot check against the board anchor on L1 (T-69); an identifier that fails it is abandoned and its poll created again, at the cost of another creation counted against the daily ceiling, so a quorum anchoring ahead spends at most two extra creations per poll before the poll fails as `creation-anchored`. The creation worker now also needs an L1 node and the anchor's address (`CREATE_L1_RPC_URL`, `CREATE_L1_CHAIN_ID`, `CREATE_ANCHOR`), all or none with the rest. The sweep and status commands move to part 3b-2.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 3b-2): the roots step runs beside the registry sync, holding no key, and states only the deployment's published groups at a block an output root proves; the electorate is not the operator's choice per poll. A poll whose tier can't be counted fails in plain words rather than being given a smaller electorate. The sweep and status commands move to part 3b-3.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 3b-3, #371): the runbook's status and sweep commands are built. `status` sets the key's included nonces against the worker's ledger, so a transaction the worker didn't send shows as `unrecorded` without waiting for a poll to fail as `creation-replaced`, and lists the polls the key still has to finish before rotation; `sweep` sends the whole balance, less the transfer's fees, to the funding account. Not mitigated yet: nothing alerts on the balance or on `unrecorded` (an operator has to run `status`), and a pending transaction of the key blocks the sweep.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 4a, #374): the stub step is built in `packages/core`. A rooted poll's stub states only the draft's questions and text (under a fresh salt), the electorate the roots step recorded and the deployment's pins, and is checked by definition version 5's rules and the verifier's profile combination before it is recorded for the coordinator, so the operator's code can't hand seats a stub of its own making. Not mitigated yet: the pins are the worker's configuration, checked against one deployment record only from part 4b, and nothing runs the step until then.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-22 part 4b, #377): the stub job runs beside the registry sync, holding no key, and takes every pin from one deployment: the election registry's own immutables must match the sync's contracts and supply the anchor and relay, and the panel must be one the trustee registry pins. The creation worker's startup check now requires the registry to name its L1 and anchor, so identifiers are checked against the anchor their stubs pin. Residual: the creation and stub workers still read the election registry's address from separate settings.
