# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result. Regional cells (election-definition version 3, [[0019-regional-cells]]) increase this exposure, to be bounded by the `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` floor on every cell of a split tier, which counts registrations, not people: specified, with ballot version 2 naming its cell, and enforced once the verifier, trustee nodes and the ballot client check it. The verifier reads version 3 since P1-15 part 4b-2b and checks the stated cells against the fold of the stated region counts (`cells`); recomputing the counts and cell trees from the board (2.4, 2.8) is P1-19's.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires. The tally partition is fixed in the election definition registered before voting opens and is the tiers only until P1-15 specifies regional cells, whose region must then come from a membership proof, never a voter-chosen, grindable or per-ballot field, with a protocol-wide floor on each cell's admitted electorate ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision; verifier 2.8). Trustee nodes and the ballot client check the partition before releasing a share or casting. Openings are checked per registered cell (7.4), small tiers are flagged (8.2) and the result pages and API flag them too (P3-6). Tiers are never withheld or merged; every tier is opened and flagged. Regions below the floor are folded into their tier's remainder cell, decided from the registered electorate before voting opens ([[0006-trustees]], Small cells).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-5): mitigation updated for the registered partition (tiers only until P1-15 specifies proven regional cells with a size floor), checked before any share is released or ballot cast, per-cell openings (verifier 2.8, 7.4) and per-tier flags (8.2). This closes two paths `crypto-review` found in a looser draft: a per-ballot partition that opened every ballot, and a voter-ground regional cell that made a vote-sale receipt.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): [[tally]] opens one aggregate per cell and derives coarser totals by adding cells' counts, never by a second opening, and states that an opening over one ballot reveals it. Only the opening is published ([[0015-tally-publication]]): per-trustee summed shares on the board would, with posted shares, let a future adversary isolate sub-aggregates down to one ballot (T-15).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): verifier 8.2 also flags a cell whose counted ballots with passing postings at fewer than `k` distinct seats are few, since a future adversary reads the rest and the opening gives their sum.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 2b follow-up, [[tally]], Summed-share message): `packages/crypto` seals and opens summed shares only for cells of the definition's registered partition, so a node bug or a coordinator asking an honest trustee to sum over a subset of a counted set as another cell can't make it disclose a difference of sums that isolates a ballot. Checking that the set summed is the anchored counted set, once per cell, stays the node's.
- 2026-10-12 (owner, [[0006-trustees]]): small cells: regions below the floor merge into a per-tier remainder cell; tiers are never merged or withheld.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4a, [[0019-regional-cells]]): regional cells come from registered region groups, folded by the owner's rule at a floor of `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` = 1,000 registered members, each proven against one cell tree, so a ballot's region is fixed at registration and the remainder cell hides which small region a ballot came from. Not mitigated: the floor counts registrations, so at Tier 0 (and Tier 1 up to its collusion bound) padding a target's region with known-vote identities still isolates the target; two elections whose remainders differ by one region can difference it; and a creator can list one small region as an unsplit tier.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b, part 1): [[election-definition]] version 3 and [[membership]] (Cell tree) specify regional cells: per tier one root block, its groups each in one region, the stated region counts and the cells the fold derives from them (the `cells` rule), one cell tree per cell with each commitment counted once per tier (first occurrence), and `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` = 1,000 in [[parameters]]. `packages/crypto` decodes and checks version 3 and recomputes region counts and cells from the groups' leaves; vectors from an independent implementation. At most 256 cells per definition (the tally opening's `u8` cell). No ballot names a cell yet, so 2.3 refuses version 3 with any ballot version until P1-12 adds one; the verifier and clients don't read version 3 yet (part 2).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-2 part 4a, [[0024-tier0-registration]]): the planned self-paid Tier 0 path (P3-2 part 5) would let anyone, not only registrars breaking the no-repeat rule, move a voter to another regional cell by copying their commitment into a lower-numbered self-paid group. Part 5 must first change [[membership]]'s first-occurrence rule so that signed groups win over self-paid ones, and among self-paid groups the earliest addition in chain order wins. Residuals: a self-paid copy landing before a self-paid registration (front-running) moves that registrant; and since a signed copy always wins, the registrars or a compromised signing service (T-06, T-69) can move any self-paid registrant, even with a later copy. An honest service checks self-paid groups against the latest chain state when a request arrives and again before signing, so it never signs such a copy. That second check has its own residual: a party that sees a B request before its batch is signed (Cloudflare, Railway's logs, a compelled deploy; T-11, T-69) can self-add the queued commitment into a self-paid group of its chosen region, the check then drops it from the batch, and the registrant lands only in the copier's cell. The client detects it by checking where its commitment landed; part 4d decides whether to sign anyway a commitment copied after its request was accepted.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b-2a): [[ballot-validity]] (Ballot record (version 2)) specifies the ballot that names its cell: the cell's position in the partition where version 1 names a tier, bound by the statement and ballot hashes; the verifier takes the cell's tier, root and scope from the definition (`ballot-cell` refuses any other cell), so no voter chooses a cell by choosing a root, and a commitment repeated in another cell's group can't prove from its zeroed leaf. Definition versions 3 and 4 pair with ballot version 2 only (2.3). `packages/crypto` decodes and admits version 2; vectors from an independent implementation with snarkjs proofs against cell trees. The verifier doesn't read version 3 yet (part 4b-2b).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b-2b): `packages/verifier` reads definition versions 3 and 4: each decodes and is checked under its own layout and rules (`cells` and `receipt-keys` reported on 2.1), its hash and the stub hash the key ceremony binds are taken over its own encoding, and 2.3 runs over it. The per-cell recomputation from the board's group additions (2.4, 2.8) and the ballot checks against each cell's root (5.1 to 5.3) are still unverifiable, with the same checks for version 1 (P1-19), so the floor is checked only against the stated counts until then.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-16 part 1a, [[election-definition]] Layout (version 6)): a live poll's schedule (`live_every`) and minimum batch (`live_batch`, at least `MIN_LIVE_BATCH` = 20) are fixed in the stub before opening, and civic referendums are always sealed (`results`, `civic-sealed`); the running set and batch rule are P2-15's. Padding a batch at Tier 0 stays unmitigated ([[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]], Residuals).
