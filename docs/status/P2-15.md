# P2-15: Live results

- State: in progress (part 1, the spec, started; parts 2 to 7 follow)
- Branch: `claude/p2-15`
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

Live results as [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]] (Part 2) decides: an author may choose running totals, decrypted at scheduled anchors during voting, instead of sealed-until-close; civic referendums are always sealed. Running sets hold each nullifier's one ballot while it has exactly one and it is receipted by every qualified seat; re-voters are held out until the final tally. A tier's running totals are released only when every cell has changed by at least `B` nullifiers against every earlier release, and by `B` again after the released snapshot (the one-batch lag), with `MIN_LIVE_BATCH = 20` and a default of 50. The final tally is unchanged.

## Plan

Each part is one PR on this branch's name. "Gate" means the CI `crypto-review` check (`docs/spec`, `packages/crypto`, `packages/verifier`, `packages/contracts`); "in-session" means the independent in-session `crypto-review` subagent review that AGENTS.md (Before opening any PR, step 5) requires for `core`, `sdk` and `apps/ballot`.

1. **Spec** (gate). [[tally]]: the running set, the change count, the release rule with its lag, the schedule (`live_every` anchoring periods from opening, skipped releases never made later), the nodes' completeness check, the running opening record (a new record type in [[versioning]]) and its signature tag, per-release destruction of summed shares, and either a proof that pairwise differences bound every combination of releases under the running-set rule or a stronger check. `MIN_LIVE_BATCH` in [[parameters]]. [[verifier]]: the release checks (snapshot, choice, batch, opening), disclosure reports attributed to signers, and `live-final-batch` in 8.2. Vectors from an independent implementation, including re-voters, ties, late receipts, a skipped release and a final tally within `B` of a release.
2. **`packages/crypto`** (gate): running-set selection, change counts, the release choice, the running opening's codec, signing and checks; fast-check properties (every published pair differs by at least `B` in every cell; each nullifier's run in the running sets is unbroken; the choice is independent of board order within a snapshot).
3. **`packages/verifier`** (gate): the release checks and report entries of part 1.
4. **Trustee node** ([[P2-13]]; in-session if it lands in a gated or protocol path): scheduled releases, the completeness check against its own receipts, refusal outside the rule, and destruction of each release's summed shares once its openings are anchored.
5. **Coordination** (`apps/worker`, `apps/api`, logic in `packages/core`; in-session for `core`): collecting summed-share exchanges per release, boarding running openings, and the API for results so far.
6. **UI** (`apps/web`, `apps/ballot`, `packages/ui`; in-session for `apps/ballot`): the creation flow's choice (sealed by default; live with the influence trade-off and the batch explained; unavailable for civic referendums), the notice before answering a live poll, and results so far per tier with snapshot time, ballot count and "votes changed since they were first cast are counted at the close"; no ticker, countdown or urgency. Storybook stories with interaction and a11y tests.
7. **Reference election** (outside the gate): a live poll with re-votes, a tie, a skipped release and a regional tier whose small cell delays releases, checked end to end by the verifier.

## Notes

- Threats: T-16 (differencing), T-45 (re-votes held out), T-01 (padding at Tier 0, not mitigated), T-29, T-54.
- Known-weak from the ADR: padding by an adversary who can cast `B − 1` known ballots into a quiet interval; combinations of three or more releases until part 1 settles them; a final tally can differ from a release by fewer than `B` after late ties or exclusions (flagged, never withheld).
