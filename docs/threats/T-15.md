# T-15: Harvest now, decrypt later

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-11
- Status: **Partial by design.** The public board itself is perfectly hiding. Residual: share ciphertexts harvested in transit are only post-quantum conjectured (Module-LWE), or classical if ML-KEM can't be used unaudited; shares posted publicly (offline or refusing trustees, direct submit) have only classical (Shor-breakable) privacy, and each trustee offline during voting lowers the harvest threshold for the ballots it missed.

## Threat

ElGamal ciphertexts on the public board are only computationally hiding; a future quantum computer or discrete-log break reveals every historic vote.

## Mitigation

[[0002-everlasting-privacy]] (P1-3, accepted: option B): perfectly hiding Pedersen and VSS commitments on the board, with each trustee's shares sent off the board under a hybrid ML-KEM + Diffie–Hellman KEM, so nothing the protocol normally publishes reveals a vote to any future adversary.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].
