# T-47: Proof of vote

- Area: Coercion and vote buying (G-11)
- Adversary: A-4
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: a published Benaloh opening (opt-in, sent after close, canonically ordered) can still be linked to its voter through network metadata (T-11), and usually shows the voter's real choice ([[verifier]] check 6.2).

## Threat

The Benaloh challenge or receipt is abused to prove a vote to a buyer.

## Mitigation

A challenged ballot is spoiled and never counted; the cast ballot's receipt reveals nothing about its plaintext. Challenged ballots never reach the board during voting, and an opening is boarded only after the counted extent, sorted within its period ([[challenge]], Publication).

- 2026-10-11 (P1-13): a ballot's L1 block publishes its making time to about 12 seconds, which challenged-entry placement must not let link to the cast ballot ([[revoting]], Residuals).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P1-16): the canonical order is specified and checked: within each anchoring segment, openings come last, sorted by their bytes ([[board]], Spoiled-ballot openings). This hides when an opening was sent, not which challenged ballot it opens; keeping that challenged ballot's board entry from being tied to the cast ballot is P1-13's.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3a, [[0021-challenged-ballots]]): challenged ballots never reach the board during voting; an opening carries its own core and is published only after close, at the voter's request. An opening whose core is a cast ballot's never changes what counts, and the operator must refuse it. Residual: an opening gives the core's randomness, so a voter can turn a challenge into a receipt by casting the opened core under their own nullifier with their own tooling, and anyone given an opening during voting can cast it; re-voting still overrides a sold ballot unless the buyer watches until close (T-45).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3b): [[challenge]] specifies publication: openings only through the board path, in segments after the counted extent's last (`s_S`) and up to `t_S + OPENING_WINDOW`, so no ballot that lands later can count; `opening-cast` looks for any shared vote commitment `C_j` (same `definition_hash`, at any option), never tracking codes or whole `vss`, since an opened core's commitments can be cast again under fresh share hashes, another tier or re-blinded coefficients; an opened `C_j` cast in another election is a residual nothing reports; `opening-cast`, `opening-early`, `opening-late` and `opening-repeat` are reported against the board operator, never changing what counts. The residual above stands ([[challenge]], Residuals).
