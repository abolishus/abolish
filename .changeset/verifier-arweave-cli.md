Reads the permanent archive from the command line: `--arweave <url>` (up to 16 Arweave nodes or ar.io gateways, asked in order) with `--max-archive-manifests`, `--max-archive-bytes` and `--max-archive-reads`, whose defaults are the library's. `loadBundle` takes an optional `archive` (`ArchiveSources`: a transaction source and `readArchive`'s budgets), reads the archive before the board copy, and serves the copy from the objects read; a format-1 or 2 bundle with an archive is rejected. `DEFAULT_ARCHIVE_BYTES` is exported.
