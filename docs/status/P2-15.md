## Scope

Live results as [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]] (Part 2) decides: an author may choose running totals, decrypted at scheduled anchors during voting, instead of sealed-until-close; civic referendums are always sealed. Running sets hold each nullifier's one ballot while it has exactly one and it is receipted by every qualified seat; re-voters are held out until the final tally. A tier's running totals are released only when every cell has changed by at least `B` nullifiers against every earlier release, and by `B` again after the released snapshot (the one-batch lag), with `MIN_LIVE_BATCH = 20` and a default of 50. The final tally is unchanged.

## Notes

- Threats: T-16 (differencing), T-45 (re-votes held out), T-01 (padding at Tier 0, not mitigated), T-29, T-54.
- Known-weak from the ADR: padding by an adversary who can cast `B − 1` known ballots into a quiet interval; combinations of three or more releases until part 1 settles them; a final tally can differ from a release by fewer than `B` after late ties or exclusions (flagged, never withheld).
- Part 1a found that [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]]'s running set doesn't work with its pairwise rule: when ballots leave running sets (re-voters, posted ballots), a single difference can have one voter alone on a side, three releases can give one ballot exactly, one re-vote freezes a tier's releases, and one seat's trustee can force departures by posting its share of any ballot. So running sets only grow (first fully receipted ballot, kept until the final tally; a posting never lets a ballot enter, since its public check doesn't prove the share valid), and [[tally]] proves that the smallest non-empty side of any combination of totals is a least positive directed cut, which with grow-only sets the pairwise batch bounds; the verifier computes it exactly for the final tally against the releases. The lag counts entries. All listed under Decisions to review. Two in-session independent review rounds and the first CI `crypto-review` found these step by step (the sign problem, the freeze, the final tally's other side, trustee-forced postings, a re-vote slipped past the completeness check, invalid postings poisoning releases, a pending first ballot, a voter freezing releases with a ballot delivered only to intake), and the rolling-ballot, lag-counting, deferral and receipt-withholding points, all addressed in the spec or stated as residuals.
- Tested (part 1a): `live-release.json`, printed by `packages/crypto/scripts/live-release-vectors.py`, which computes every cut two ways and checks the theorem by exhaustive search on small graphs; `packages/crypto` consumes it in part 2. The model takes admission per ballot as given, so the pending rules get ballot-level cases in part 1c.
- Known-weak (part 1a): the final tally can disclose a lone released re-voter's released ballot (flagged, never withheld); a zero count in a side of `B` discloses (`(1 − p)^B`), which also weakens re-voting against coercion in live polls (a higher floor needs a new definition version); live polls run only where we hold at least `k` default seats until [[P2-13]]'s withholding check exists, so authors who add trustees, and organisational votes, can't be live yet, and one compromised board host can quiet an interval there; a share posted after entering stays in releases; one trustee can keep ballots out by not receipting them; padding at Tier 0.
- Unblocked (2026-10-14): #268 had been labelled `blocked` after seven rounds of CI `crypto-review` findings, the last three all in the nodes' completeness check against operator withholding (a re-vote entering at an unchecked snapshot, a coercer's failing first ballot, a voter stalling releases by delaying intake). Re-reviewing the section as a whole: that check protected nothing in any poll that could run, since under the transitional panel the operator holds every seat, and author and independent panels were already barred until [[P2-13]]; and it kept failing because what it needs (forwarded board receipts, per-period boarding deadlines, receipt exchange) doesn't exist yet. So part 1a states what the check must achieve and leaves its specification to [[P2-13]], with those mechanisms; live polls run only with no author panel and under a default panel with at least `k` seats pinned as ours until then (not merely kind `transitional`: a handover step can leave us fewer than `k`), enforced by [[verifier]] 2.3 (part 1c). The coercer fix to condition 1 stays. The model's `completeness` vectors are removed with the check.
- Part 1b (#283): the running opening (record type `0x0018`, version 1; [[tally]], Running opening record): one cell's opening of one chosen release over its running set, naming the snapshot, signed under `abolish/v1/sig/running-opening` over its header and fields, attributed as a tally opening is. Which running openings are a release's (another definition, a cell outside the partition or an unchosen snapshot is outside the schedule) and when one passes; the operator boards only a passing, attributed opening of a chosen release, the first per seat, release and cell, through the board path only. No boarding window: a node signs only within step 3's window and the verifier reads every running opening in the sealed extent, so the seal gains no term. Releases reuse summed-share message version 1 (the aggregate names the set; the values are fixed by it). Tally-opening version 2 is version 1 plus the running opening, pinned exactly by live definitions with board-entry version 6 (version 5 plus `0x0018`), so a sealed poll's board can't carry one ([[verifier]] 2.3). No record carries a rolling tier's eligible count at a release: every reader derives it, as the final result's counts.
- Tested (part 1b): `running-opening.json`, printed by `packages/crypto/scripts/running-opening-vectors.py` from the spec (records, decode rejections, attribution, boards judged against chosen releases, the boarding rule), which also checks every seat's summed shares of every running set against its aggregate; `packages/crypto` consumes it in part 2. `RECORD_TYPES` and `TAG_REGISTRY` gain the new type and tag, tied to the spec by `registries.test.ts`.
