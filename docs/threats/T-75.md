# T-75: Creator profile misuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-1, A-2, A-10
- Status: **Planned.** Decided in [[0040-creator-profiles]]; built by [[P2-27]] parts 1b to 1d (1b, the tables and rules in `packages/core`, done; 1c-1, their moderation in `packages/core`, done; nothing is shown before 1c-2). Residual: "Asked by" is our database's word, which an insider can change; a rename changes the name on old polls; copies made while a poll was linked outlive unlinking.

## Threat

A creator's profile ([[0040-creator-profiles]]) publishes a name the author chose and links the polls the author chose to it, so:

- a profile's name impersonates a real person, group or authority, so its polls borrow standing they don't have; or a name carries a private person's details, harasses or threatens;
- a profile builds standing under one name and renames itself to mislead ("bait and switch"), changing what every old poll says about who asked it;
- a name uses invisible or bidirectional-override characters to read as something else on the page;
- the link exposes an author: a profile publicly ties together polls on sensitive subjects, and anyone who learns who is behind one name (A-1, A-10) learns every poll it lists;
- an insider (A-2) attaches a poll to a profile its author didn't choose, drops one from it, or shows a name other than the author's.

## Mitigation

- **Opt-in, per poll.** No account has a profile until it makes one, and each poll is linked only if its author chose that for it, changeable at any time; an unlinked poll names no author anywhere. With no profile, every poll is anonymous, as before.
- **Nothing permanent.** The profile and its links are account-side rows only, never on chain, IPFS, the board or the archive (T-17), deleted with the profile or the account and lost in a wipe. The profile page is `noindex`.
- **Nothing else on the page.** A name and the linked polls that are registered and neither delisted nor hidden; no picture, text, link, dates or counts about the account, and nothing about answering (T-10).
- **Names are bounded.** NFC, 1 to 60 code points, no control, format, private-use, unassigned or separator characters but the space; at most one rename a day. Not unique and not verified, and the page says so.
- **Names are moderated** under [[moderation-policy]]'s grounds and public log, with appeals, before any name is shown (P2-27 part 1c); a hidden profile shows a notice instead of its name and its polls show no "Asked by".
- **A random handle.** 80 bits from the CSPRNG, never derived from the account, never reissued; a fresh profile links nothing from before.

Not mitigated: "Asked by" is what our database says, so an insider can change it and only the author would notice; confusable characters aren't checked, so only `impersonation` moderation answers a look-alike name; and copies of a poll page made while it was linked (search caches, link previews, archives) keep the name after it is unlinked.

## History

- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1a): added with [[0040-creator-profiles]].
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1b): built in `packages/core` (`src/profiles.ts`, migration 0033): one profile per account with a verified phone; `profileName` refuses control, format, private-use, unassigned, surrogate, default-ignorable (Hangul fillers, the combining grapheme joiner, variation selectors) and separator characters but the space, the blank Braille pattern and a name with nothing visible, collapses spaces, trims and composes to NFC, and the table checks the length, NFC, spaces and controls again; a name is set at most once a UTC day, making a profile included, and the day outlives a deleted profile, so deleting and remaking can't flip a name faster; handles are 80 CSPRNG bits kept for good in `profile_handles`, which the app role may add to and never change or remove, so core never issues one twice. A profile shows only chosen polls with a stored definition and no delist or hide standing; `askedBy` says nothing for a hidden poll. Not yet shown anywhere: moderation of profiles (part 1c) comes first. Residual: the database role can still point a new profile row at a retired handle by hand, which core never does; that is the insider case already stated.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1c-1): moderation of profiles in `packages/core` (`src/profile-moderation.ts`, migration 0034): a profile is hidden or restored under [[moderation-policy]]'s grounds in an append-only log numbered from the poll log's own sequence, reported per handle and ground, and appealed once per hide by its author before a different moderator who doesn't hold it. While a hide stands, the profile's public read gives only the ground and no poll says "Asked by" it; the polls stay listed. A hidden profile can't be renamed (checked under the moderation lock), so a restore shows the name a moderator judged. Residual: deleting a hidden profile and making a new one sheds the hide (a new handle, at most one name a day, every poll's link chosen again); a rename after a restore isn't reviewed unless reported.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1c-2a): decisions about profiles are public: `GET /moderation/log` and the website's log page name a profile by its handle and never its name, so a name hidden for `personal-data` or `harassment` is never repeated by the log that hides it. Still nothing shows a profile itself until part 1d.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1c-2b): the operator's commands in `apps/admin-cli` (`moderation profile-queue`, `profile-review`, `profile-decide`, `profile-status`, `profile-appeals`, `profile-appeal-decide`) call core's profile moderation and name a profile only by its handle; `moderation log` prints the whole log, a profile by handle, never its name, account or moderator. No mitigation changes.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1c-2c): profiles are reportable and appealable from outside: anyone reports a profile by handle and ground on the website's origin, signed in or not, with nothing kept about the reporter, as a poll's report on the API; the author sees their own profile's state, newest decision and appeals, never a moderator or reviewer, and files the one appeal per hide. No mitigation changes; nothing shows a profile itself until part 1d.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1d-1): the author's own profile routes on the website's origin (`POST /profiles/own`, `make`, `rename`, `delete`, `show`) call core's `readOwnProfile`, `makeProfile`, `renameProfile`, `deleteProfile` and `showOnProfile` for the session's account only, so the name rules, one name a UTC day, the verified phone and the hidden-profile rename refusal hold from outside exactly as in core. No mitigation changes; no page calls them or shows a profile until part 1d-2.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1d-2): the account page's profile settings: the author makes, renames, deletes and appeals their profile through the part 1d-1 routes, never sending a handle; the name is shown isolated in `<bdi>` and typed in a `dir="auto"` field, so a right-to-left name can't reorder the page; the page says names aren't checked or unique, that the name changes once a UTC day, and that copies may outlive deletion.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1d-3): the author chooses, poll by poll, whether their profile shows it: a box under each of their polls on the account page and on the poll form (a new poll's choice is sent right after its first save gives it an ID), each sending only the poll's ID and the choice to `POST /profiles/show`, never a handle; the server takes only the author's own polls. The profile's name beside the box is isolated in `<bdi>`. Nothing is shown publicly yet: `/by/<handle>` and "Asked by" come next.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1d-4): profiles are public at `/by/<handle>`: the name, isolated in `<bdi>`, and the polls the profile shows that are registered and neither delisted nor hidden, each the stream's checked card, ten a page. The page says the name is unchecked and needn't be unique, and that which polls it shows is this website's word, not part of any poll. A hidden profile shows only its ground. Anyone can report it from the page, under the policy's removal grounds. The profile's address on the account page and its handle in the moderation log now link to it. "Asked by" on poll pages and cards is next.
- 2026-10-22 (P2-27 part 1d-5): "Asked by" is public: a poll's page and its card in the stream name the profile its author chose to show it on, the name isolated in `<bdi>` and a link to `/by/<handle>`, and the poll's page says who asked is the website's word, not part of the poll. Read by the poll's public ID alone through the front's reader (core's `askedBy`), never the API; none for a hidden poll (dropped by the loader too, so the page's data carries no name) or a hidden profile, and none where the read fails or the handle isn't one core issues. A profile's own page doesn't repeat it. The link preview and share image don't name the asker, so cached previews never outlive an unlinking with a name in them.
