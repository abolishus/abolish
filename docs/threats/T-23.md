# T-23: Board tampering

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: entries added since the last anchor can be dropped or reordered undetectably by anyone but the voter holding a receipt, for up to an hour.

## Threat

An insider alters, drops or reorders board entries.

## Mitigation

Entries are hash-chained (P1-16); the app's DB role has no UPDATE or DELETE, enforced by grants and triggers (P2-1); roots are anchored hourly to L1 (P4-1) and mirrored to IPFS (P4-3); the verifier recomputes the chain (P1-19).
