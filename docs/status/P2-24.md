# P2-24: Sharing

- State: todo
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

Every way a poll travels, as [[sharing]] designs them: the share sheet, link previews (done in [[P2-18]]), QR codes, the printable flyer, the embed, native share on phones, a result card once results exist, and the voter's own "I answered" card. From `share-*.tsx` in `apps/web/src/mockups/`.

- The voter's own card is made on their device and shared only by them; the server never learns that it was made or what it says.
- Every surface carries the poll's short code and seal, and nothing that identifies a voter.

Rules for every part, as [[P2-19]]'s: everyday register by default ([[language]]); no hard-coded strings (`messages.ts`); CSP-safe (no inline `style`); server-rendered where the page is the website's; a fixed analytics template per route, never a path or poll ID (T-67); each component a story with a play function passing axe at desktop width and 360px; SSR and serve tests per route; the mockup's story deleted in the PR whose page matches it. Privacy holds everywhere: nothing an account does on the website (following, reacting, sharing, notifications) is ever stored, logged or keyed with a ballot, nullifier, receipt or the fact of having answered, and the T-10 no-join tests cover every new table, queue and log line ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Identity).

## Plan

1. The share sheet on the poll page and after answering: copy link, native share, QR.
2. The flyer (`/p/<code>/flyer`), printable on A4 and Letter.
3. The embed (`/embed/p/<code>`), script-free, with `frame-ancestors` relaxed on `/embed/*` only ([[sharing]], The embed decision).
4. The result card and its preview image once results exist ([[P2-25]]).
5. The voter's "I answered" card, made in the ballot client.
