# P2-29: Design iterations of the everyday app

- State: in progress (the system pages' iteration 1 merged in #615; their iteration 2 in progress, then the other surfaces in the Scope's order)
- Branch: `claude/p2-29`
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

The gate before any official work: every everyday surface goes through at least three design iterations for UX quality ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Priority).

Surfaces: home, the stream and its card, topic and place pages, search, the poll page (open, live, sealed, closed), answering in the ballot client (arrive, questions, check, sending, done, receipt, change your answer, recovery), creating a poll, the author's list, sharing surfaces, the account and profile pages, notifications, and the system pages.

Each iteration is one PR per surface (or a small group):

1. **Review**: screenshots at 360px and desktop width, light and dark, of every state; a written critique against [[language]], the brief's design direction and the Center for Civic Design rules for the ballot; tap counts and copy read aloud; what a first-time user would misread.
2. **Fix**: the changes the review calls for, with the stories and tests updated.
3. **Record**: the before and after screenshots and what changed, in this file.

A surface is done after its third iteration finds nothing it would change. The everyday app is complete when every surface is done and [[P2-23]] to [[P2-28]] are done; then official work may start.

## Iterations

Screenshots are taken from Storybook (`vp exec storybook dev` in the app, then Playwright at 360px and 1280px, light and dark) and are not committed: the stories are the reproducible record, and the repository holds no binary images. Each record below says what the screenshots showed.

### System pages: iteration 1

Not found, Something went wrong and You're offline (`apps/web/src/components/site-layout.tsx`; stories `Components/Site layout`).

Review:

- **The offline page's "Try again" drew at 12px** where the error page's drew at 16px. A link styled with `buttonVariants()` keeps both the base's `text-xs` and the size's `text-base`, and unmerged, the stylesheet's order picks `text-xs`. Every link or plain button styled that way had it: the home page's "Browse polls", the account's new-poll link, the stream's start link, the poll register's filter button and the verification page's action. Only `Button` merged its classes.
- **The offline page offered other places to go** that all fail offline the same way, so each one would bring back this page.
- **Not found spoke the civic register**: "board entries, definitions and results are permanent" and "paste its 64-character ID into the form on the front page". Someone who followed a broken link to a friend's poll has a link, not an ID, and none of those words.
- **Other places to go left out Discover**, the everyday way to find a poll again, and its three links left an empty cell in the two-column grid at desktop width.
- Kept: the titles say what happened in plain words; the error page says nothing about the error (T-67) and tells a voter their ballot is safe in the ballot app; both themes pass axe; one action per page, in `primary`.
- For a later iteration (the frame, not these pages): at 360px the site's links wrap to a second row with "Moderation log" alone on it.

Fix:

- `packages/ui`'s `buttonVariants` returns merged classes (`cn`), so a link drawn as a button takes its size's text; the `AsLink` button story checks the computed size.
- The offline page has no "Other places to go"; not found and error list Discover ("Search the questions people are asking"), Polls, Moderation log and Home, four for the grid.
- Not found: "The link may be mistyped or cut short. Polls and their results are never deleted, so a poll's own link keeps working." and "Looking for a poll someone shared? Ask them to send the link again, or search for its question on Discover."
- The site-layout stories check the four places, no civic wording on not found, and on the offline page no places and a 16px "Try again".

Record: before, the offline page's button label was visibly smaller than its body copy, and the desktop grid ended in a blank cell; after, the button matches the error page's, the grid closes at four, and the offline page ends at its button.
