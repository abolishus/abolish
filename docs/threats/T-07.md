# T-07: Setup compromise of a proving system

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-1
- Status: **Accepted.** Sound if any one participant in each ceremony was honest.

## Threat

A cheating participant in Semaphore's Groth16 ceremony could forge Semaphore proofs; a compromised universal KZG SRS (used by bb's UltraHonk for our Noir circuits) could forge Noir proofs.

## Mitigation

Semaphore's multi-party ceremony is the one accepted exception to "no per-circuit trusted setup" (brief, Chain). Our Noir circuits need no per-circuit setup, but they rest on the universal SRS bb uses, itself from a multi-party ceremony. Membership and nullifiers are verified with Semaphore v4's Groth16 keys from its ceremony ([[0009-membership-proof]]): a forged membership proof needs every ceremony participant to have cheated. Privacy doesn't rest on the ceremony: P1-17b part 2 checks the pinned proving key's well-formedness once, publicly; `membership_vk_hash` binds that key's sha256, so the prover can check it uses exactly that key (P2-8, P5-4). Until part 2 is published, zero knowledge against a subverted ceremony is not established.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): settled: Semaphore's Groth16 ceremony for membership ([[0009-membership-proof]]); bb's universal SRS applies only to later custom Noir circuits.
- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): Tier 2 proofs ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]], zkPassport) use UltraHonk under Aztec's universal SRS, with no per-circuit setup.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-17b): the depth-32 verification key is pinned from Semaphore's published keys and the proving key by sha256, both bound into `membership_vk_hash`; the reference-string check is part 2.
