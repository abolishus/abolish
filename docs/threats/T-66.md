# T-66: Database loss or corruption

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-7
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

Database loss or corruption.

## Mitigation

Postgres isn't authoritative: every result is rebuilt from chain + IPFS, and the reference election wipes and rebuilds (P1-20, P2-1).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P2-6 part 1): [[0017-identity-recovery-backup]] stores voters' recovery envelopes in Postgres. They affect no result, so nothing here changes for results, but they can't be rebuilt: a wipe or loss of the table loses every backup, and a voter who then loses their device loses their voting identity.
- 2026-10-11 (P2-4 part 1): the worker's scheduled board audit detects stored columns that disagree with the stored payloads (corruption, rows written around `appendEntry`). It can't detect a restore of an older but consistent snapshot, a truncated tail, or a consistent rewrite with the triggers off: it remembers nothing between runs, and only anchored checkpoints (P4-1) catch those. Detection only: the board is rebuilt from chain and IPFS (P4-6). An audit reads a whole board into memory at once.
