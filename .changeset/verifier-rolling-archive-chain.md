`verifyBundle` builds a rolling poll's chain from the archive's replay of the group and election registries, under the registries the release pins (`PINNED_REGISTRIES`, none yet, or a third argument), once each registry's replay reproduces an evidence record past the close and every `root_l2_block` (`archivedChain`). With it, 2.3 reads a fitting rolling profile as passing, the board checks run and the ballots are judged against their step roots; a rejection that rests on the chain leaves 5.1 to 5.3 unverifiable, never failed, while the archive's output roots aren't checked on L1. Delivery, the counted set, the spoiled-ballot and tally openings and a live rolling poll's releases aren't reached yet. Without the chain a rolling poll still stops at 2.3.
