# T-14: Trustee collusion

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Needs decision** (P1-4, P1-7). Residual: k colluding trustees break secrecy for every ballot of that election. Automated trustee nodes add one more path: a malicious node release reaches every share at once (T-61, [[0006-trustees]] rule 6). That holds for as long as they, or anyone who seizes them, keep their keys and stored shares; destruction after the tally is required but can't be proven, and posted shares are permanent. k colluding trustees can also publish a ballot's shares, which anyone can check against the public VSS commitments, making that ballot permanently and verifiably public (as with a published decryption under ElGamal).

## Threat

k or more trustees combine shares and decrypt individual ballots.

## Mitigation

k-of-n threshold with independent named trustees (P1-7, owner decision; [[0006-trustees]] proposes 4-of-7 with at most k − 1 trustees per jurisdiction and per hosting provider, and at most one seat for us), Each voter splits the ballot's opening k-of-n among the trustees with Pedersen VSS, so no one ever holds a key that opens ballots, and no DKG is needed for plurality and approval (P1-12, P1-14, [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]). Honest trustees only ever send summed shares to each other, and publish openings, for cells of the partition registered in the election definition ([[0015-tally-publication]]) (tiers only until P1-15 specifies proven regional cells; verifier 2.8, which their nodes run before releasing a share), which open aggregates (P1-15, [[0003-tally-scheme]]); per-election keys and stored shares are destroyed after the tally.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-5): summed shares limited to cells of the registered partition ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision; verifier 2.8, 7.4).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): trustees exchange summed shares ([[0015-tally-publication]], option C), so each trustee that receives `k` of them learns a cell's summed polynomials at every seat. Wherever posted shares fall into the pattern in 0015's Context (shaped by a relay or by trustees' downtime), one corrupt trustee with a later break of their classical encryption can complete a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares; it can also disclose the summed shares, which can't be attributed to it, and a trustee seized before destruction yields the same ([[tally]], Residuals). Not mitigated; re-randomising the summed polynomials (0015 option B) would close it only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1`, since `k − 1` trustees know the summed polynomials everywhere under every option.
