# P2-14: Multi-question ballots

- State: in progress (part 1a, the definition and display-text versions in the spec with vectors, under way)
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

Several questions on one ballot, cast once with one nullifier per poll and tier (or cell), one membership proof and one receipt, each question tallied on its own and never cross-tabulated, as the owner decided on 2026-10-12 ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Election types). The design is [[0026-multi-question-ballots]] (option B, the owner's; the flat option vector, the limits and the checks are the agent's under rule A): election-definition version 5 with a list of questions (method, option count, minimum and maximum selections each), display-text version 2 with a title and per-question text, ballot version 3 with one range proof per question beside the per-option proofs, the spoiled-ballot opening for its cores, `tally-question-range`, `MAX_QUESTIONS = 32`, `MAX_DISPLAY_TEXT` and at most `MAX_OPTIONS` options over the whole poll. Definition versions 1, 3 and 4 and ballot versions 1 and 2 are unchanged; a one-question poll under version 5 is the one-question case. Ranked-choice questions wait on the mixnet ADR ([[0003-tally-scheme]], [[P5-1]]).

## Parts

In order, each one PR. Parts 1 to 5 change `docs/spec/`, `packages/crypto`, `packages/verifier` or `packages/contracts`, so they are inside the `crypto-review` CI gate; parts 6 to 8 are outside it, but `packages/core`, `packages/sdk` and `apps/ballot` still get the in-session `crypto-review` subagent review (`AGENTS.md`, Before opening any PR, step 5).

1. **Spec and vectors** (gate). [[election-definition]] version 5 and its well-formedness; [[display-text]] version 2 and its cap; [[ballot-validity]] version 3 (question range proofs, the version-3 statement, ballot and core hashes, shape and admission); [[challenge]]'s opening version for version-3 cores, with `opening-vote-outside` per question, and a note on whether the same layout serves version-2 cores; [[tally]]'s `m` as the total option count and `tally-question-range`; `MAX_QUESTIONS` and `MAX_DISPLAY_TEXT` in [[parameters]]; `abolish/v1/fs/ballot-validity/question-sum` in [[domain-separation]]; the new versions in [[versioning]]; [[verifier]] 2.1, 2.3, 2.7, 5.4, 6.2, 7.2 and 8.1. Vectors (`election-definition-v5.json`, `display-text-v2.json`, `ballot-v3.json`, the opening's and the tally's per-question cases) from an independent Python generator reusing the existing ones. Settles the open point on skippable pick-exactly-N questions before anything freezes. May split into 1a (definition and display text) and 1b (ballot, opening, tally) if review needs it.
2. **`packages/crypto`: definition and display text** (gate). Codecs and well-formedness for definition version 5 and display-text version 2, the whole-record cap checked before decoding, `checkElectionToShow` for version 5, flat-index helpers. Vectors from part 1, fast-check over the codecs.
3. **`packages/crypto`: ballot version 3** (gate). The committed vote's codec, question range proofs (prove and verify), the version-3 hashes, the intake shape and validity checks trustees run, the version-3 opening (`openChallenge`, `checkOpening`). Waits on [[P1-15]]'s verifier reading definition version 3 (cells), on which version 5 builds.
4. **Tally** (gate). `packages/crypto`'s aggregates, summed shares and opening checks over a version-5 definition, `tally-question-range`, and per-question results sliced from the opening; the trustee node ([[P2-13]]) calls them unchanged in form.
5. **Verifier and contracts** (gate). `packages/verifier`: 2.3's combinations, 2.7, 5.4, 6.2, 7.2 and per-question results in 8.1, with the reference election ([[P1-20]]) gaining a multi-question poll (pick one, pick any, pick exactly N) with a re-vote and a challenge. `packages/contracts`: direct submit's cheap checks for ballot version 3 (the per-question range-proof lengths in `validity-shape`) and calldata at the new worst case, about 47 KB; waits on [[P1-18]]'s direct submit and may go in a PR of its own.
6. **`packages/core`, `packages/api-contract` and `apps/api`** (outside the gate). Polls with questions in the Drizzle schema (with its migration), creation and listing in the contract (`zod/mini`, strict objects), ballot admission for version 3 through `packages/crypto`, results per question and tier; `packages/sdk` and `apps/admin-cli` simulations with several questions. Waits on [[P2-3]]'s ballot admission.
7. **`apps/ballot`** (outside the gate). One question per screen, each instruction rendered from its limits, the limits enforced before the next step, a review of every answer before the tracking code and the cast-or-challenge choice, the checker showing every question in every translation, and a re-vote that asks every question again with no earlier answers kept. Storybook stories with interaction and axe tests. Waits on [[P2-8]] part 3.
8. **`apps/web`** (outside the gate). Poll creation with several questions and the four methods (pick one, pick any, pick up to N, pick exactly N), results per question with the tier switcher, no joint view across questions. Storybook stories with interaction and axe tests. Waits on [[P2-9]]'s results pages.

## Notes

- Threats: T-28, T-30, T-31, T-32, T-34, T-41, T-42, T-45, T-47, T-16, T-17, T-52, T-29, T-36 ([[0026-multi-question-ballots]]).
- Known-weak, from the ADR: a small cell reveals every answer of its voters at once; a pick-exactly-N question can't be skipped unless part 1 allows a blank; blank counts are derivable only for questions with a maximum of one; more than 64 options in a poll needs new versions of every per-option record; ranked-choice questions aren't available.
