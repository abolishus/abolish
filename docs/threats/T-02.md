# T-02: Sybil rings at Tier 1

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-1, A-9
- Status: **Planned**, weak by design ([[0014-tier1-vouching]]): a ring of `c ≥ 3` colluding Tier 2 members can admit at most `4c` fake Tier 1 identities, a person with two disjoint groups of three vouchers gets two, and vouchers can't be held to account.

## Threat

Colluding vouchers vouch for fake identities.

## Mitigation

Vouching rules and limits (P3-3). Reported per tier.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P3-3): [[0014-tier1-vouching]]: only Tier 2 (document-verified) members vouch, and only anonymously, with two Semaphore proofs per vouch. A Tier 1 addition needs vouches from 3 distinct Tier 2 members, and each Tier 2 member has 12 vouches, ever. So collusion is bounded at 4 fake identities per colluding document, ever (`c·L/k`), and every Tier 2 residual (T-03) carries over, multiplied by up to 4. A transitive web of trust was rejected, because fake identities could vouch for more. Not mitigated: double admission through two disjoint groups of vouchers, and voucher accountability (the vouchers are anonymous).
