# T-45: Observed coercion

- Area: Coercion and vote buying (G-11)
- Adversary: A-4
- Status: **Partial.** Defeated by a coercer who watches until the poll closes; who votes last with the voter's secret; who has the voter make ballots in advance while watching once and posts them later, unless the re-vote rule binds something fresh, such as a recent L1 block, into each ballot ([[verifier]] check 6.1, P1-13); or who demands the voter abstain (forced abstention is visible through the nullifier to anyone holding the secret). Re-voting also gives no deniability against anyone who has seen one of the voter's ballots or receipts, since later ballots under that nullifier are public ([[verifier]] check 6.1). Where ballots are decrypted individually (a ranked-choice mixnet, P1-4), re-voting doesn't stop a pattern attack (T-70).

## Threat

A coercer watches the voter vote, or demands the receipt.

## Mitigation

Re-voting where the last ballot counts (P1-13), protected against replay by T-38; receipts prove inclusion, not content.
