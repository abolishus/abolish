# T-15: Harvest now, decrypt later

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-11
- Status: **Partial by design.** The public board itself is perfectly hiding, provided the tally publishes only each cell's opening ([[0015-tally-publication]]); published per-trustee summed shares would let a later break of posted shares' encryption complete ballots with fewer than `k` posted shares. Residual: share ciphertexts harvested in transit are only post-quantum conjectured (Module-LWE), or classical if ML-KEM can't be used unaudited; shares posted publicly (offline or refusing trustees, direct submit) have only classical (Shor-breakable) privacy, and each trustee offline during voting lowers the harvest threshold for the ballots it missed.

## Threat

ElGamal ciphertexts on the public board are only computationally hiding; a future quantum computer or discrete-log break reveals every historic vote.

## Mitigation

[[0002-everlasting-privacy]] (P1-3, accepted: option B): perfectly hiding Pedersen and VSS commitments on the board, with each trustee's shares sent off the board under a hybrid ML-KEM + Diffie–Hellman KEM, so nothing the protocol normally publishes reveals a vote to any future adversary. The tally publishes only each cell's opening, never a trustee's summed shares, which would fix the summed polynomials at every seat and, with posted shares, complete ballots for a future adversary ([[0015-tally-publication]], [[tally]]). Remaining: a corrupt trustee, which sees the summed shares, has that power ([[tally]], Residuals).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P1-11): `h = hash_to_ristretto255("", DST = "abolish/v1/generator-h")`, published with its intermediate bytes in `docs/spec/vectors/hash.json` so anyone can check no one chose it ([[group]]).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-12, part 1): the public part is specified ([[ballot-validity]]): each VSS commitment has a uniform, independent `h` exponent, so it is perfectly hiding, and honest validity proofs simulate their false branches, so they are witness-indistinguishable against an unbounded adversary; both are within about 2⁻²⁵⁰ of ideal, from reducing 64 CSPRNG bytes per scalar. `packages/crypto` proves only a sharing it made itself, so no caller can choose a commitment's randomness. Share encryption (the computational part) is part 2.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): `crypto-review` on #55 found that publishing each trustee's summed shares, as ADR 0002 had it, fixes the summed polynomials at every seat, so a future adversary (A-11) who breaks the classical encryption of posted shares can complete a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares (with a relay, A-2, shaping the postings, or by accident). [[0015-tally-publication]] (option C) publishes only each cell's opening; summed shares go only between trustees under the hybrid KEM. Residual: a corrupt trustee sees the summed shares and has the same power ([[tally]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): verifier 8.2 reports, per cell, the counted ballots with passing postings at fewer than `k` distinct seats: a future adversary reads every other ballot, and the opening gives the sum of these ([[tally]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-12, part 2): share encryption specified and built ([[share-encryption]], [[0016-share-kem-dh-group]]): ML-KEM-768 combined with Diffie–Hellman in ristretto255, X-Wing style, so a share in transit falls only to an adversary who breaks both. Each ciphertext proves knowledge of its DH ephemeral, so no one can copy a victim's ephemeral into a posting of their own and make a trustee's complaint reveal the victim's DH value (found by the in-session review). The AEAD's associated data doesn't bind the nullifier, so the ciphertexts can be made before it. `@noble/post-quantum` is self-audited only; the owner allowed it on testnets in a hybrid with X25519, and on 2026-10-12 confirmed that this ristretto255 hybrid counts ([[0016-share-kem-dh-group]]). Residuals unchanged: a recorded ciphertext falls to both breaks, a posted one to discrete logs alone.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15, part 2b): the summed-share message is specified and built ([[tally]], Summed-share message; record type `0x000b`): a trustee's summed shares for one cell travel to one other trustee under the same hybrid KEM (ML-KEM-768 with Diffie–Hellman in ristretto255) with their own tags, so a recorded message falls only to an adversary who breaks both. The board's envelope refuses the record type, so it never reaches the board, IPFS or the archive. Residuals unchanged: a corrupt trustee or a seized one still holds every seat's sums ([[tally]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-12 part 3b, [[delivery]]): the client never posts a seat whose share receipt passes, so an operator that refuses receipts can't push a ballot's shares onto the permanent board with `m_i`; a seat that isn't receipted is posted only at the voter's explicit choice, after the client says it lowers that seat's long-term privacy, and only through the contract path. The owner accepted this voter-chosen posting as an extension of [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]'s fallback on 2026-10-12. The board path refuses unfounded complaints, whose `D` and the posted `m_i` would open a share at once; the contract path can't, and such a complaint is attributed to its seat.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 1, [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]]): a Diffie–Hellman OPRF leaves discrete-log relations on the key (every answer `sk_i · B`, and the `sk · H1(x)` each registrant unblinds), so a discrete-log break recovers the Tier 2 nullifier key from one harvested or self-made registration; for that reason the keyed nullifier never goes on the board, and requests travel under the hybrid KEM to registration KEM keys of their own, answers to a fresh client key, with the proofs deleted after attestation. A stolen attester list remains exposed to a discrete-log break plus chip data.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-1, [[keyed-nullifier]]): the Tier 2 key's second generator `J` is derived by counter from a registered tag, so no one knows `log_G J` and the share commitments bind; a seat's answer carries a proof of knowledge of its commitment's opening. Each answer is a static Diffie–Hellman value: Cheon's attack would cut about 14 bits from a share with about 2²⁹ chained queries (`l − 1`'s small factors), and only the query proof (part 2b-3), which shows the client knows `r`, stops chaining, so a seat checks it before answering.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-2a, [[nullifier-key]]): the Tier 2 key's transcript holds only perfectly hiding commitments, the hashes of the dealt-share messages and reveals of corrupt dealers' points or corrupt complainers' own shares, so nothing in it fixes the key even to an unbounded adversary; the dealt shares travel under the hybrid KEM and are never archived. A missing dealt share is never a complaint, so withholding one can't make an honest dealer publish an honest seat's share.
- 2026-10-13 (P3-4 part 2b-2b, [[nullifier-reshare]]): a reshare's transcript holds only perfectly hiding commitments (the constant ones are the predecessor's public `C_i`), hashes of the reshared-share messages and reveals of corrupt dealers' points or corrupt complainers' own shares, so nothing in it fixes the key even to an unbounded adversary; reshared shares travel under the hybrid KEM and are never archived.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-16 part 2b-3a, `packages/crypto`): share encryption of version-4 ballots (`encryptSharesV4`, `openSharesV4`, `checkPostingV4`, `proveComplaintV4`, `checkComplaintV4`) draws `m`, `e` and `w` from the CSPRNG for every seat of both panels and keeps the proof of knowledge of `e` under version 4's `t_e`, so a complaint about an author seat's posting reveals only the DH value its poster already knows; `share-encryption-v4.json`, from an independent generator, re-makes each ciphertext byte for byte.
