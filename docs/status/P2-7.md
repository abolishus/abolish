## Scope

`packages/ui`: shadcn/ui + Tailwind, i18n, and Storybook stories with interaction and a11y tests for every component.

## Part 1

- Tailwind 4 theme in `src/styles.css`: the brief's palette as tokens, a dark theme (system preference, or `data-theme` on any element), and IBM Plex Sans, Mono and Serif bundled from `@fontsource` (no CDN).
- i18n on `Intl` alone: `formatMessage` (`{name}` placeholders, single pass, plural categories from `Intl.PluralRules`, locale number formatting, a missing parameter throws), and `I18nProvider`, `useI18n` and `useTranslate` (no default locale; a locale without a catalog throws).
- Components: `Button` (three variants, 48px targets), `Hash` (Plex Mono, grouped visually only so a copy is exact, `translate="no"`), `Count` (tabular figures, `<data>` with the exact value, bigint support).
- shadcn/ui is followed as a model, not a dependency: anything `apps/ballot` imports carries no third-party runtime code (AGENTS.md), which rules out Radix, `class-variance-authority`, `clsx` and `tailwind-merge`. Not a one-way door, so no ADR.
- The story-coverage check is now recursive (the non-blocking `claude-review` finding on #51).

Tested: unit tests for the palette (every text colour at 4.5:1 on the ground in both themes, and paper on the accent; the two dark blocks agree; the light palette is the brief's), message formatting (fast-check: a parameter's value is never re-read as a placeholder; templates without placeholders are unchanged), and the provider (server rendering). Storybook: every story's play function and axe, including dark-theme stories. Checked by mutation: a low-contrast dark `pencil` fails the dark `Hash` story's axe check. A Storybook build contains the fonts as local files and no external URL.

Known-weak:

- No translated catalog ships yet; the apps own their catalogs. Which languages to ship first is open (Spanish is the obvious second for the US).
- axe only judges what a story renders; reading order, wording and the Center for Civic Design's ballot guidance need review of the ballot screens themselves (P2-8).
- The fonts ship every Unicode subset fontsource publishes for Plex (about 27 woff2 files), loaded by the browser only when a page uses those characters.

Both were caught by the stories' axe checks.
- Variant names follow shadcn: `apps/web` moves `secondary` to `outline` and `quiet` to `link`.

Tested: 23 stories (play functions plus axe in headless Chromium), light and dark for every variant; the target size of the default and icon sizes is measured (at least 48×48) and `lg` at least 56 tall; keyboard activation, disabled (no click reaches the handler) and `type="button"` by default are kept from part 1. `apps/web`'s tests and type checks pass.

Known-weak:

- The focus ring uses the preset's `--ring` (zinc 400 in light), about 2.6:1 against white. WCAG 2.2 AA asks only that focus be visible (2.4.7); 2.4.13's 3:1 is AAA. A darker ring is a token change for all components, left to the part that next touches the tokens.
- Hover states aren't contrast-checked; axe sees only the resting state.

## Part 2b

- `Fingerprint` (`src/components/fingerprint.tsx`): the visual fingerprint, a deterministic, left-right symmetric 8×8 glyph from a 32-byte hash. The left half's 32 cells each show 2 of the hash's first 64 bits (empty, square, dot or corner triangle), and the right half mirrors it. A hash output is already uniform, so the bits are used as they are. The SVG is an image named by the caller, who includes the hash's first digits, since a screen reader can't compare shapes (T-24, T-41, T-53).
- `ReceiptStub` (`src/components/receipt-stub.tsx`): the ticket stub, on shadcn's `Card` (added with `vp exec shadcn add card`, unchanged), with the fingerprint and `Hash` above a perforated divider and the board entry (`Count`) and time below. The time is formatted for the locale in UTC by default, the board's zone, so everyone comparing reads the same; `<time dateTime>` carries the instant. It shows nothing about the vote (T-47).
- `StatusTrail` (`src/components/status-trail.tsx`): an ordered list of steps (complete, pending or upcoming) in the status tokens, each with its own Lucide icon and its state in words for screen readers (WCAG 1.4.1), `aria-current="step"` on the pending one. It refuses a trail that isn't complete, then at most one pending, then upcoming, which would claim more than the board shows (T-53).
- The contrast test now also checks `muted-foreground` on `card`.

Tested: unit tests for the fingerprint (fast-check: the grid is 8×8 and mirrored, it shows exactly the hash's first 64 bits, flipping any of them changes the glyph; the mapping is pinned by a fixed vector; malformed input is refused) and for the trail's ordering (fast-check over every sequence of states up to 6: exactly the well-ordered ones render). Storybook: 14 new stories with play functions and axe, light and dark: the glyph's mirror symmetry measured from the rendered shapes, the stub's hash, entry (`<data value>`), UTC and local time, French grouping, and the trail's states in words and `aria-current`.

Known-weak:

- A matching fingerprint is weaker than its 64 bits. People compare shapes roughly, so someone who can grind hashes (an operator varying its own fields) can find a glyph that looks alike far below 2⁶⁴ work. A different glyph is conclusive; a match is a quick check, the hash is shown beside it, and the verifier compares bytes (T-24, T-53).
- The fingerprint's mapping is specified only in this package's README and test, not in `docs/spec/`. A third-party board that draws its own glyphs needs it there; it moves to the spec when the board view (P2-9) shows fingerprints.
- The stub's perforation notches are drawn in the page's ground, so on a page whose ground differs from `background` they show as discs.

## Part 2c

- `Table` and `Tabs` added with `vp exec shadcn add table tabs` and adapted, each with a comment. `Table` is a ruled grid: every cell bordered, a 2px rule under the header, tabular figures and the caption on top as the ledger's heading (brief, "the board"). `Tabs` gets 48px targets, `text-sm`, and inactive tabs at 70% of the foreground.
- `TierSwitcher` (`src/components/tier-switcher.tsx`): results by verification tier on the line tabs, one tab per tier with its name and scope, each panel only that tier's results (brief, Keep: tiers as a first-class layer, never blended). It has no "all tiers" tab and says callers must not add one (T-01, T-13, T-53). It refuses no tiers, repeated values and a default that isn't a tier.
- `StatusTrail` now joins a step and its state for screen readers with the caller's translated `stepTemplate` (`{step}, {state}`) instead of a hard-coded `, ` (the non-blocking `claude-review` finding on #106), and refuses a template that drops either placeholder (the non-blocking finding on #107).

Tested: Storybook stories with play functions and axe, light and dark, for `Table` (every cell has a solid border, tabular figures, row headers), `Tabs` and `TierSwitcher` (one tab per tier with its scope, the first selected, switching by click and by arrow key, a chosen default, every tab at least 48px tall), and `StatusTrail`'s template in another locale's punctuation. Unit tests for `TierSwitcher`'s refusals and `StatusTrail`'s template check. The tab stories read the panel the selected tab controls, since Base UI keeps the outgoing panel mounted during its exit transition (CI saw two panels once).

Known-weak:

- `TierSwitcher` can't stop a caller passing a blended total as if it were a tier; the rule is in its doc comment and README, and P2-9's results page must keep it.
- The ruled grid's lines are at 30% of the foreground, decorative under WCAG 1.4.11; the header rule is full strength.
