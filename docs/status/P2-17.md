- State: in progress (part 1, definition version 7 with rolling tiers, the rolling tier tree, the root clock and the rolling scope in the spec with vectors, merged in #207; part 2a, ballot version 5 with the step, `root-step-range` and the window, with vectors, merged in #256; part 2b-1, what a rolling tier reports (counts at opening, close, smallest step used and live releases, and growth), with vectors, merged in #265; part 2b-2, the rolling direct submission's supplied root and the board carrying the groups' events through close, in progress)

## Scope

Rolling electorates for everyday polls, as the owner decided on 2026-10-12 and [[0028-rolling-electorates]] designs (option B): people who register after a poll opens can answer it. A rolling tier's tree holds its listed groups' additions in chain order, and its roots are taken at the boundaries of a protocol-wide root clock (`ROOT_PERIOD`, proposed 600 s). Each ballot names its step: the client uses the newest settled step, and the verifier accepts only steps within `ROOT_WINDOW` (proposed 2 h) before the ballot's L1 reference. The nullifier scope is `H(DS("abolish/v1/rolling-nullifier-scope", definition_hash ‖ u8(t)))`, so one secret has one nullifier per tier and poll whatever root it proves against. A rolling tier is one cell. Results report the member count at opening, at close, at the smallest step any counted ballot used and at each live release. Direct submission supplies the root it proved against, and the verifier checks it. Civic-record polls are never rolling (`rolling-class`), which the verifier, the election registry and the ballot client all check. Fixed polls are unchanged.

## Known-weak (part 2b-1)

- Nothing computes the counts in production code yet: the verifier's rolling 8.1 is part 4.
- Growth places L2 timestamps in the anchoring clock's periods, which are L1 time; the two clocks agree only to within the L2's drift.
- The counts show a Tier 0 Sybil flood or a registrars' stuffing run as a shape, never tell it from a genuine surge (T-01, T-06 stay Not mitigated at Tier 0).
- The eligible count includes removed members who were live at an accepted step, as it must, so it can overstate who a removal was meant to exclude ([[membership]], Removals don't stop a ballot in an open poll).
