# T-29: Tally or decryption manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A trustee or coordinator publishes a wrong decryption share or combines shares wrongly.

## Mitigation

Each trustee's summed shares are checked against the product of the public VSS commitments, so a wrong sum is attributed to its trustee, and the combined aggregate must open the product of the public commitments ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); the verifier recombines and recomputes the tally (P1-15, P1-19).
