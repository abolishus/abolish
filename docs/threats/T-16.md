# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result. Regional cells, which [[0002-everlasting-privacy]] allows where results are regional, would increase this exposure; verifier 2.8 rejects them until P1-15 specifies them.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires. The tally partition is fixed in the election definition registered before voting opens and is the tiers only until P1-15 specifies regional cells, whose region must then come from a membership proof, never a voter-chosen, grindable or per-ballot field, with a protocol-wide floor on each cell's admitted electorate ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision; verifier 2.8). Trustee nodes and the ballot client check the partition before releasing a share or casting. Openings are checked per registered cell (7.4), small tiers are flagged (8.2) and the result pages and API flag them too (P3-6). Tiers are never withheld or merged; every tier is opened and flagged. Regions below the floor are folded into their tier's remainder cell, decided from the registered electorate before voting opens ([[0006-trustees]], Small cells).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-5): mitigation updated for the registered partition (tiers only until P1-15 specifies proven regional cells with a size floor), checked before any share is released or ballot cast, per-cell openings (verifier 2.8, 7.4) and per-tier flags (8.2). This closes two paths `crypto-review` found in a looser draft: a per-ballot partition that opened every ballot, and a voter-ground regional cell that made a vote-sale receipt.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): [[tally]] opens one aggregate per cell and derives coarser totals by adding cells' counts, never by a second opening, and states that an opening over one ballot reveals it. Only the opening is published ([[0015-tally-publication]]): per-trustee summed shares on the board would, with posted shares, let a future adversary isolate sub-aggregates down to one ballot (T-15).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): verifier 8.2 also flags a cell whose counted ballots with passing postings at fewer than `k` distinct seats are few, since a future adversary reads the rest and the opening gives their sum.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 2b follow-up, [[tally]], Summed-share message): `packages/crypto` seals and opens summed shares only for cells of the definition's registered partition, so a node bug or a coordinator asking an honest trustee to sum over a subset of a counted set as another cell can't make it disclose a difference of sums that isolates a ballot. Checking that the set summed is the anchored counted set, once per cell, stays the node's.
- 2026-10-12 (owner, [[0006-trustees]]): small cells: regions below the floor merge into a per-tier remainder cell; tiers are never merged or withheld.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4a, [[0019-regional-cells]]): regional cells come from registered region groups, folded by the owner's rule at a floor of `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` = 1,000 registered members, each proven against one cell tree, so a ballot's region is fixed at registration and the remainder cell hides which small region a ballot came from. Not mitigated: the floor counts registrations, so at Tier 0 (and Tier 1 up to its collusion bound) padding a target's region with known-vote identities still isolates the target; two elections whose remainders differ by one region can difference it; and a creator can list one small region as an unsplit tier.
