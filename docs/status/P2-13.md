## Scope

The trustee node [[0006-trustees]] (Consequences) calls for, added after the owner decided the panel's structure on 2026-10-12: the deployable each trustee runs on infrastructure it controls (an app, or a mode of `apps/node`), since open polls close too often for trustees who act by hand. It runs only what the spec already defines, calling `packages/crypto` and `packages/verifier` and never reimplementing them:

- **Ceremony** ([[ceremony]]): the stub checks, the four rounds with fresh keys and challenge secrets per run, approval of at most one transcript per `stub_hash` with at least `max(k, n − 1)` qualified seats (rule 7), and the ceremony registration signature ([[trustee-identity]]).
- **Intake** ([[share-encryption]], Intake and receipts; [[delivery]]): receipts during voting, complaints within the complaint window, and the contract-path receipt obligation, bounded as [[P1-12c]] specifies.
- **Tally** ([[tally]]): summed shares only for cells of the registered partition over the anchored counted set, checked against the L1 anchor first; the summed-share message; openings.
- **Destruction** (P1-15 part 3): the KEM secret after the tally, the signing key after its last statement.
- **Partitions** ([[0006-trustees]], Consequences): never a share for a partition the spec doesn't require; every tier is decrypted, and small tiers carry T-16's warning, until the owner answers the tier-floor question.

## Notes

- These rules bind honest nodes only: a trustee can change its own node, and `k` trustees together can decrypt anything (T-14).
- Rule 6 of [[0006-trustees]] applies, since whoever controls the node software reaches every share at once (T-61): no auto-update, each trustee rebuilds the signed, reproducible release (P0-3) and publishes which one it runs, releases roll out one trustee at a time, and a second implementation should exist before Phase 5.
- Phase 1's reference election (P1-20) and simulations (P1-21) simulate seven trustees, 4-of-7, with two offline; this node replaces the simulation in Phase 2's reference election against staging (P2-12).
