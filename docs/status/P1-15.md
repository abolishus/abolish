# P1-15: Summed shares, tally and destruction

- State: in progress (part 1 merged in #55; part 2a, the opening record and the checks over a board's openings, in review; parts 2b, 3 and 4 to follow)
- Branch: `claude/p1-15`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Per-trustee summed shares checked against the public VSS commitments, tally combination and the opening of the aggregate commitment (only over the cells of one partition), the tally transcript, and signed destruction of keys and stored shares after the tally.

## Plan

The item is built in parts, each its own PR on this branch's name:

1. **Summed shares and cell openings** (this part): [[tally]] and [[0015-tally-publication]]. The cell aggregates, each trustee's summed shares and their check, the combination of the `k` lowest passing seats, the published opening and its checks, and coarser totals. Needs only P1-12 part 1's committed vote and shares.
2. **The opening record and the messages**, in two PRs:
   - **2a, the opening record** (this part): the tally opening (`0x0006`, version 1), one cell's opening signed by the trustee that combined it under `abolish/v1/sig/tally-opening`; which openings are a cell's and which are stray; attribution by the registered signing keys; the per-cell checks over several openings (`tally-conflict` included); the tally window; [[verifier]] 7.3 and 7.4; retiring `abolish/v1/fs/tally-share` and `abolish/v1/tally-transcript`.
   - **2b, the summed-share message**: the private message carrying a trustee's summed shares to each other trustee, encrypted under P1-12 part 2's hybrid KEM to the recipient's registered KEM key and signed with the sender's per-election key.
3. **Destruction**: each trustee's signed statement, on the board, that it destroyed its per-election KEM key and stored share ciphertexts once the tally is anchored ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], Consequences; T-14, T-69). Waits on P1-14 part 2 and P1-12 part 2.
4. **Regional cells** ([[verifier]] 2.8, T-16): per-region roots, the size floor in [[parameters]] and a later definition version. Waits on the owner's answer to the small-cell question in [[0006-trustees]]; until then the partition is the tiers only.

## What was built (part 1)

- [[0015-tally-publication]] (`Accepted (agent) — owner may veto`): option C, the tally publishes only each cell's opening; trustees exchange summed shares privately. Rejected: publishing summed shares (A, ADR 0002 as written: leaks ballots with fewer than `k` posted shares to a future adversary; narrowing ADR 0002 is the owner's) and re-randomising them with zero-sharings (B: keeps public attribution, but adds an interactive round with its own agreement and liveness problems).
- [[tally]], a new spec page: aggregates `Ê_{c,j,t}` (identity for an empty cell); summed shares `(V_{c,i,j}, R_{c,i,j})`, sent only to other trustees; the trustees' summed-share check; `Q` = the `k` lowest passing seats and Lagrange at 0 (`tally-quorum` while fewer than `k` pass); the published opening and the verifier's checks `tally-missing`, `tally-opening` and `tally-range`, which fail the election; coarser totals derived by adding cells, never opened; residuals.
- [[verifier]] 7.1, 7.2, 7.4 and 8.4 follow it; the spec and vectors READMEs list it; [[STATUS]] lists the ADR under Decisions to review.
- `packages/crypto/src/tally.ts`: `aggregateCell`, `sumShares`, `checkSummedShares`, `lagrangeAtZero`, `openCell` (a trustee's combination) and `checkOpening` (the verifier's check), `deriveTotal`.
- Threat files T-14, T-15, T-16, T-29, T-54.

## Tested (part 1)

- `docs/spec/vectors/tally.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`packages/crypto/scripts/tally-vectors.py`, ballots from `ballot-validity-vectors.py`; openings found by Gaussian elimination over every coefficient and cross-checked against the Lagrange coefficients): 11 cells (honest plurality; approval with two excluded trustees; a quorum failure with a sum missing a ballot, swapped options and an absent seat; high seats only; empty; one ballot; `MAX_TRUSTEES`; `MAX_OPTIONS`; a vote of 2 failing `tally-range`; two tiers of one election), 16 published openings checked by the verifier (every valid cell's opening; a count or randomness off by one and swapped options, `tally-opening`; short, missing and a count encoded as `V + ℓ`, `tally-missing`; the vote-of-2 cell's, `tally-range`) and a derived overall total. TypeScript reproduces every aggregate, summed share, verdict, combined set, Lagrange coefficient, opening and opening verdict.
- fast-check properties (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): honest sums from any `k` or more seats open each cell to its counts, and any other `k` seats interpolate to the same opening; any changed summed share fails and is excluded; a sum over the cell minus any one ballot fails for every seat.
- Mutation checks by hand: dropping the summed-share check or the range check fails tests. An honest combination always passes `tally-opening` (an identity in the exponent), so the combiner's copy of that check is reached only by the verifier's vectors.

## Known-weak (part 1)

- **Nothing sends, posts or signs summed shares or openings yet** (part 2), so 7.3 and 7.4 and signer attribution aren't implemented.
- **A corrupt trustee sees every seat's summed share** ([[tally]], Residuals; T-14, T-15): wherever posted shares fall into the pattern (a relay or trustees' downtime) and with a later break of their classical encryption, it can complete a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares, or disclose the sums unattributably; a seized trustee yields the same. Not mitigated; [[0015-tally-publication]] option B would close it only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1`.
- **Ballots readable after a break shrink a cell's anonymity set**: [[verifier]] 8.2 now reports the number of counted ballots with fewer than `k` valid posted shares per cell, for P1-19 to implement.
- **Several openings per cell** are specified (`tally-conflict`), but `checkOpening` takes one; part 2 implements the rule with the transcript.
- **A wrong summed share isn't publicly attributable**, only known to the trustees; a wrong opening is (T-29).
- **No published vectors exist for this composition.** The independent implementation and the reviews carry the weight (T-36).
- **Scalar arithmetic isn't constant time.** Summing and interpolation use JavaScript `bigint` on a trustee's machine; per-ballot shares are summed, not branched on.

## What was built (part 2a)

- [[tally]], Opening record: the tally opening (record type `0x0006`, version 1: `definition_hash`, `cell`, `aggregate_hash`, `signer`, `opening`, `signature`), one record per cell opening; the registry entry is renamed from "tally transcript" to "tally opening", and an election's tally transcript is every tally opening on its board. `aggregate_hash` (new tag `abolish/v1/tally-aggregate`) names the counted set the opening is over, so an opening over another counted set is reported `tally-aggregate`, not as a wrong sum. The signature is the signer's per-election signing key (registered by the ceremony) under the new tag `abolish/v1/sig/tally-opening`, over the record's fields without header or signature. An opening is attributed only if `signer` is a registered seat and the signature verifies under its key; anything else is unattributed, counts if it passes and is blamed on no one if it fails. Openings naming another definition or a cell the partition doesn't have are stray. The operator boards only passing openings attributed to a registered seat, one per seat and cell, so the board carries no chosen bytes beyond a trustee's ECDSA nonce and no disguised summed shares (T-17, T-15); the verifier still reads anything boarded.
- [[tally]], Opening checks: several openings per cell, in the order `tally-missing`, `tally-opening` (each failing opening reported with its attribution), `tally-conflict`, `tally-range`.
- [[tally]], Tally window: 7 days, starting when the checkpoint anchoring the counted set is due (after every window that precedes the counted set: delivery deadline, complaint window, P1-18's post-finality window, each a constant of its pinned version or contract), a constant of tally-opening version 1 and the tally's term in the seal's `W`.
- [[verifier]] 7.3, 7.4 and 8.4; [[versioning]] and [[board]] rows; [[domain-separation]]: the new tag, `abolish/v1/fs/tally-share` and `abolish/v1/tally-transcript` retired (never used), and the `retired` status defined; [[trustee-identity]] points at the new statement; `circuit.json` gains the new tag's field element.
- `packages/crypto/src/tally-opening.ts`: `encodeTallyOpening`, `decodeTallyOpening`, `tallyOpeningStatement`, `signTallyOpening` and `checkTally` (verifier 7.2 to 7.4: per cell, with stray openings and attributions). `RECORD_TYPES.tallyTranscript` is renamed `tallyOpening`.
- Threat files T-17, T-29 and T-54; [[STATUS]] Known-weak: the contract path for tally openings that P1-18 must provide.

## Tested (part 2a)

- `docs/spec/vectors/tally-opening.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`packages/crypto/scripts/tally-opening-vectors.py`: its own strict decoder and per-cell checks, cells and openings from `tally-vectors.py`, `trustee-vectors.py`'s P-256 ECDSA, which reproduces RFC 6979 A.2.5 first): 4 signed openings (an empty cell's and one at `MAX_OPTIONS` among them) re-made byte for byte by `signTallyOpening`; 12 decode rejections (empty, header only, another record type, an unpinned version, truncation in the list and the signature, a trailing byte, `V + ℓ`, `R = ℓ`, `MAX_OPTIONS + 1` entries, high `s`, `r = 0`); 16 elections, each checked whole by `checkTally` from the raw payloads: all signed, a failing opening beside a passing one (attributed and unattributed), only failing, a missing cell, a short opening, passing openings by an unregistered seat and by another seat's key, a replay into another cell (unattributed, so no framing), stray openings (a cell outside the partition, another definition signed with this election's key, and one with a key this election never registered), identical openings by three signers, an empty cell, `tally-range`, the first failure in cell order, two failing openings (the first in board order reported), a short opening beside a failing one (`tally-opening`, not `tally-missing`), and an unpinned-version payload and a truncated one beside honest openings (rejected entries; the election passes).
- fast-check (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): honest signed openings of random cells pass, attributed; any changed byte of a signed opening either fails decoding or leaves it unattributed; random bytes are rejected or re-encode to themselves; records round-trip.
- `tally-conflict` can't have a vector (two passing openings need `log_g h`); `tests/tally-conflict.test.ts` replaces `h` with `g^x` for a known `x` and checks two different passing openings fail the cell in either board order. Mutations that drop the signature check, the definition-hash check, the length check, the range check or the conflict check each fail the tests.

## Known-weak (part 2a)

- **The summed-share message isn't specified yet** (part 2b), so trustees have no interoperable way to exchange summed shares.
- **The windows the tally window waits for aren't specified yet** (P1-12 part 3, P1-13, P1-18), so `t_S` and the seal can't be computed until they are; the tally window itself no longer depends on their length.
- **A missed tally still fails the election permanently** (T-54); the window only fixes how long trustees have.
- **No vector for `tally-conflict`**, which needs a break of binding to construct; a test with a substituted `h` covers the branch.
- **The operator can withhold every opening** ([[tally]], Residuals; T-54): openings have no direct-submit path yet, so an operator that boards none before the seal fails the election, unattributed. [[tally]] now requires a contract path for tally openings (events under the event accumulator) before any election registry is deployed, which is P1-18's; recorded under Known-weak in [[STATUS]].
- **`tally-late` is specified, not built** ([[verifier]] 5.6, 7.1; T-25): a late entry that would change a cell's counted set fails the election against the operator, but checking it needs the verifier's re-vote selection (P1-19); the `late-ballot` vector records the expected verdict. Recorded under Known-weak in [[STATUS]].

## Independent review (part 2a)

A fresh in-session subagent reviewed the diff against `.github/review/crypto-review.md`, reran the generators (identical values) and the package's tests. Findings and dispositions:

- **Blocking, fixed:** verifier 2.3 still counted tally transcripts among records the election's authors write, where an unpinned version fails the election, and [[tally]] repeated it, while 7.3 and [[board]] made any undecodable opening a rejected entry. Since anyone can post a tally opening, a relay could have vetoed the result with one `00 06 02 …` payload under one reading. Tally openings are now in 2.3's "anyone can post" class, every undecodable one (an unpinned version included) is a rejected entry, `checkTally` takes the raw payloads and reports `rejected`, and a vector puts a version-2 copy and a truncated payload beside honest openings with the election passing.
- **Non-blocking, fixed:** verifier 7.2 still said an opening is signed by its combiner and that an unsigned failure is attributed to its relayer; it now defers to 7.3. Which option a cell with several failing openings reports is stated (the first failing opening in board order) and has a vector, as does a short opening beside a failing one. The operator's power to withhold every opening is stated in [[tally]], Residuals, and T-54. `TALLY_WINDOW` is listed in [[parameters]].
- Re-review of the fixes: **pass, nothing blocking.** Its two nits are applied: [[parameters]] says windows change only with a new version and points at the tally window's rationale, and verifier 2.3 calls an unpinned tally opening a rejected entry of 7.3 rather than an admission rejection.
- In-session review of those follow-ups: one blocking finding, fixed ([[parameters]] still started `TALLY_WINDOW` at close, which would give a third party an earlier seal); non-blocking, fixed: the contract path for openings accepts only openings signed by a registered seat, one per seat and cell, so it can't reopen the chosen-bytes channel, with the remaining per-seat residual stated; a `tally-aggregate` opening is attributed to its signer as an opening over a set other than the anchored one, never excused; `aggregate_hash` names the aggregate; T-54's wording on reposts; 7.2 lists `tally-aggregate`.
- `crypto-review` on #87, second run: **blocking, fixed.** Nothing pinned the counted set to the checkpoint due at `t_S`, so an operator could hold back one valid direct-submitted ballot until after the trustees combined, board it before the seal, and make every honest opening `tally-aggregate`, failing the election and blaming the trustees. The counted set is now computed from the counted extent alone (entries with `segment ≤ s_S`), nothing later changes it, the items that own late-boardable records must make late boarding the operator's failure, and the `late-ballot` vector shows the cell still passing with no trustee blamed.
- `claude-review` on #87: pass; one nit (a duplicated heading here), fixed.
- `crypto-review` on #87: pass, with four non-blocking findings and two nits, all applied before merging because the vectors freeze version 1: (1) a failing or stray opening could carry about 4 KB of chosen bytes into the archive, or disguised summed shares, so the operator now boards only passing, attributed openings, one per seat and cell (T-17, T-15); (2) the operator's withholding is now a MUST for P1-18 (a contract path for tally openings before any registry is deployed); (3) the window now starts when the counted set's anchoring checkpoint is due, after every earlier window including P1-18's post-finality window, and the record binds `aggregate_hash`, so an honest trustee that opened another counted set is reported `tally-aggregate`, never as a wrong sum; (4) a test reaches `tally-conflict` with a substituted `h`. ## `crypto-review` on #55

- Blocking, fixed: the residual described the wrong leak, and the report flag (then 8.2a) missed the real one. Publishing summed shares fixes the summed polynomials at every seat, so a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares can be completed at a seat where it is the only unposted ballot (with `k = 4`, `n = 6`: three posted shares plus one completed point), after a break of the posted shares' classical encryption alone; it happens with or without a relay arranging it, and the flag failed for any panel with `n ≤ 2k − 2`. Fixed at the root: [[0015-tally-publication]] publishes only openings, so no per-seat value is public; 8.2a is removed; the remaining corrupt-trustee residual is stated in [[tally]], T-14 and T-15. T-15's Status and Mitigation lines are updated (also `claude-review`'s non-blocking finding), and the adversary is A-11, not A-6.
- Non-blocking, fixed: `S_c` now names the share-delivery deadline and proven-complaint exclusions of [[0002-everlasting-privacy]].
- Non-blocking, moot: `Q` and the 8.4 report counted passing but unsigned summed shares, which anyone could compute; with [[0015-tally-publication]] no summed share is on the board, and 8.4 names the signer of each opening.

## Independent review of the fix

A fresh subagent reviewed the ADR 0015 fix against `.github/review/crypto-review.md`. It confirmed that publishing only `(F(0), G(0))` leaks nothing beyond the count, even to an adversary with discrete logs and every posted share (each posted share adds one independent equation; fewer than `k` leave `g_b(0)` free), and that the ADR's leak condition and example are right. Findings:

- Blocking, fixed: [[verifier]] 7.4 attributed a summed share found on the board to its signer, but every recipient holds the signed message and summed shares need no signature to be used, so an honest trustee could be framed. A disclosure is now reported against its sender or any recipient, never its signer alone, and stated as unattributable in [[tally]] and T-14.
- Non-blocking, fixed: option B closes the corrupt-trustee residual only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1` (and not at all for `k = 2`); several openings for one cell are defined (`tally-conflict`, any passing opening counts, all must agree); summed shares never go through the board, IPFS or the archive and are destroyed with the KEM key (part 3); [[0015-tally-publication]] lists every statement in ADRs 0002 and 0003 and T-14 it supersedes, and T-14's Mitigation is updated; ballots readable after a break shrink a cell's anonymity set, now reported by [[verifier]] 8.2.
- Nits, fixed: `checkSummedShares` itself rejects non-canonical scalars (and the Python generator's combination does too); the vectors say `received` and "sends", not "posted"; the residual names trustees' downtime and seizure as well as a relay; a vector for an opening count encoded as `V + ℓ` (`tally-missing`).
- Third pass: all ten findings resolved; its five new non-blocking points fixed: an opening's signed message binds the definition hash, the cell and `m`, and a failing opening is attributed only then (no replay framing); openings count within the sealed extent, signed or not, with 8.4 reporting an unsigned one; the per-cell check order is explicit and `tally-conflict` has no vector because it needs `log_g h`; the 8.2 flag counts postings that pass the public check at distinct seats, with T-15 and T-16 history lines; the list of who may have disclosed summed shares includes seizure and harvest. Left as is: `checkSummedShares` doesn't know `n` (it can't, and `openCell` checks seats against it).
