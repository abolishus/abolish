# T-30: Weak Fiat–Shamir binding ("Frozen Heart")

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-9, A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A challenge that omits part of the statement lets a prover forge proofs or transplant them across elections.

## Mitigation

Challenges hash the entire statement: domain tag, election ID, public key, every commitment and ciphertext, poll ID, chain ID and contract address (`crypto-review` skill; P1-8, P1-12). The challenge is a uniform scalar from RFC 9497's `HashToScalar` construction with the tag as its `DST` ([[0007-group-and-hash]]), never a reduced 32-byte digest.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

- 2026-10-10 (P1-11): challenges derived with RFC 9497's `HashToScalar` construction, the tag as `DST` ([[0007-group-and-hash]]), instead of reducing a 32-byte digest.
