# T-06: Group stuffing by the operator

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned** for Tier 2 ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]). It holds only with the verifier pinning the inner-circuit set and accepting only US-issued documents chained to pinned US CSCA certificates (an operator-published certificate list, or a foreign CSCA, could otherwise mint members), and only if Tier 2 proofs are published (the ADR's zero-knowledge gate; its fallback is trustee-attested additions). For Tier 1, **planned** ([[0014-tier1-vouching]]): an addition counts only with vouches from 3 distinct Tier 2 members within their budgets, checked from the board, so Tier 1 stuffing is bounded by Tier 2's. For Tier 0, the operator can add members with no external check: **not mitigated** there beyond public counts.

## Threat

Whoever adds commitments to a Semaphore group adds fake members.

## Mitigation

Tier 2 additions carry a verifiable proof of the document (P3-1 ADR must require that the proof, or its verification, be public and re-checkable). Group sizes are public.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): an operator who adds one commitment adds at most one voting identity: membership is proven against a complete depth-32 tier tree that verifiers rebuild from the board, never a LeanIMT root, in which one added value could hide a private tree of identities ([[membership]], Tier tree).
- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]: each Tier 2 addition carries a zkPassport proof that the verifier re-checks from the board. The verifier pins the inner-circuit set and the US CSCA anchors, so neither our operator nor zkPassport's oracle can admit a circuit or a CSCA of their own. Publishing the proofs waits on P3-4 showing they are statistically zero-knowledge; if not, additions are attested by a trustee threshold, and Tier 2 stuffing is then checkable only by trusting the attesters.
- 2026-10-11 (P3-3): [[0014-tier1-vouching]]: Tier 1 additions are authorised by `k = 3` anonymous vouches from distinct Tier 2 members, each with a lifetime budget of `L = 12`, which the verifier checks from the board. This replaces our Safe's signature for Tier 1 ([[verifier]] 2.4 changes in P3-3 part 2). The operator can still refuse to post vouches; direct submit covers that (T-25).
