# P2-30: Account-gated Tier 0 registration

- State: in progress (part 1, [[0039-tier0-token-issuance]] and the issuance spec [[tier0-token]] with vectors, merged in #527; part 1b, the review follow-ups (re-issuance after a key's natural expiry, recording a redemption before sending it, the stall guard as a precondition), merged in #530; part 2, `packages/crypto`, in progress)
- Phase: Phase 2: Open polls end to end at Tier 0

## Scope

The owner's decision of 2026-10-21 ([[0024-tier0-registration]], Owner decision): voting needs an account, and an account registers at most one Tier 0 voting identity per group, through blind-issued tokens, so the server never learns which identity belongs to which account.

## Rules

- Inside the `crypto-review` gate where it touches the token protocol, `packages/crypto`, `packages/core`'s checks or the ballot client.
- The ballot client blinds and unblinds; `apps/web` only relays with the account's session. Nothing stores or logs an account next to a token's unblinded value, a commitment or its timing; redemptions are delayed and batched so timing can't join them (T-10, T-11).
- The issuer key is rotated by period and its compromise is a new threat entry (it mints Tier 0 identities).

## Plan

1. Spec and vectors: VOPRF issuance and redemption (RFC 9497, as Privacy Pass, RFC 9578) bound to the group and period, with independent vectors.
2. `packages/crypto`: blind, evaluate, unblind, verify.
3. The issuer in `packages/core` and `apps/api`: one token per account (for the group of its region, [[0039-tier0-token-issuance]]), only to an account verified by email and phone ([[P2-31]]); key, rotation, rate limits.
4. `apps/web`'s relay and the ballot client's blinding, the cross-origin handoff, and redemption on the registration request in place of the Turnstile solve.
5. The signing service checks a redeemed token, with delay and batching; the T-10 and T-11 tests.
6. Migration of the registration screens ([[P2-8]]) from Turnstile-only to account-gated.

- **Part 2 must:** implement the steps on `ristretto255` and `ristretto255_hasher` with RFC 9497's DSTs (the group test bars `@noble/curves`' OPRF bundle from `src/`, so either compose the steps as `tests/vectors/rfc.test.ts` does or amend that test with a reason), and replace the vectors test's `@noble/curves` checks with the package's own functions.
- **Part 3 must:** class the signing service's refusal list (abandoned commitments, no account, time or order) in the no-join tests and build re-issuance's cross-side step as a board-side check-and-mark keyed on the commitment alone (T-10); build re-issuance for a token whose key no longer redeems (retired early or past `q + 1`), against the dead token and its unregistered commitment, shown only by a client that never sent its redemption (and, in parts 4 and 5, that finds `C` in no Tier 0 group addition on chain first, under a cross-tab lock around the sent-record), refusing a commitment ever added to any Tier 0 group (removed leaves included, with a test), with the signing service's list check and queueing serialised with it, and batches counted in flight until final on L2 (with a reorg test) (tests at the natural-expiry boundary too), bound to the account by its stored `B` and `blind`, and replacing the account's record atomically with the queue, with tests for a retirement crossing a redemption in flight, another account's dead token refused and one dead token shown twice refused ([[tier0-token]], Keys); add the issuer keys to the deployment record (a new version of [[deployment]]), decide how many periods ahead a release lists, keep each period's key the same across releases with the verifier's report checking it against the L1 release registry, make the issuer's record an atomic insert-if-absent per account, define moving region, and count issuances and redemptions per key and group (T-74).
- **Part 4 must:** in the ballot client, re-issue only a margin past `q + 1` after a natural expiry (at once after a listed early retirement), write the sent-record durably before sending and atomically with `C` and `blind`, hold one lock across tabs around it and re-issuance, read the chain for `C` by light client or several independent RPC endpoints (never Abolish's API), reading every group's addition history in full and never querying one commitment, rebuild a retried re-issuance under the current key after a period change, durably record that a `C` was shown before sending its re-issuance (so it is never redeemed afterwards, whatever storage survives), and, once a re-issuance may have been accepted but its answer was lost, retry with the new request's `key_id'`, `C'` and `blind'` rather than the old dead token's, which no longer match the record ([[tier0-token]], Keys and Redemption; review notes on #530).
- **Part 5 must:** specify and test the client's guard against the issuer's active stall of a quiet region, with a way out of the deadlock a plain join floor causes, bounded inside the key's redemption window ([[tier0-token]], Security notes; a precondition in [[0039-tier0-token-issuance]]); seal the redemption's body to a signing-service key with a hybrid KEM (as [[nullifier-transport]]), so the proxy and platform never see a commitment before its batch and can't shut a one-token account out with a self-paid copy, leaving [[registration]]'s drop rule as it is ([[tier0-token]], Redemption); and test that no record keeps the time of issuance, and that redemptions are spread over `TOKEN_REDEEM_DELAY`.
- **Known-weak:** a crash after a redemption is recorded as sent but before it is sent, followed by an absence past `q + 1`, strands the token for good (it counts as sent, so it can't be re-issued): the price of the safe default. Also: one identity per account for good (an identity lost after redemption isn't replaced; a token lost before redemption is replaced only by re-issuance once its key stops redeeming, and only if the client kept `blind` and `C`); tokens can be sold; a new commitment's anonymity set is the region's accounts that took a token in the day before it joined; POPRF rests on 3HashSDHI; the network-level timing of issuance and redemption stays T-11's.
