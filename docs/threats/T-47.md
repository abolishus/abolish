# T-47: Proof of vote

- Area: Coercion and vote buying (G-11)
- Adversary: A-4
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: a published Benaloh opening (opt-in, sent after close, canonically ordered) can still be linked to its voter through network metadata (T-11), and usually shows the voter's real choice ([[verifier]] check 6.2).

## Threat

The Benaloh challenge or receipt is abused to prove a vote to a buyer.

## Mitigation

A challenged ballot is spoiled and never counted; the cast ballot's receipt reveals nothing about its plaintext. Challenged ballots never reach the board during voting, and an opening is boarded only after the counted extent, sorted within its period ([[challenge]], Publication).

- 2026-10-11 (P1-13): a ballot's L1 block publishes its making time to about 12 seconds, which challenged-entry placement must not let link to the cast ballot ([[revoting]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-13 (P1-19 part 4g): the verifier checks every opening on the copy and reports the publication rules it can decide before `s_S` is known: `opening-early` for an opening at or below `period(closes_at) + 1`, the floor of `s_S`, `opening-cast` for one opening a commitment of a ballot admitted at or below it, and `opening-repeat`. Until P1-18 fixes the contract-event window, an opening boarded above the floor but within the counted extent, or after its window, goes unreported, and 6.2 stays unverifiable while any opening isn't settled ([[verifier]], Spoiled-ballot openings).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P1-16): the canonical order is specified and checked: within each anchoring segment, openings come last, sorted by their bytes ([[board]], Spoiled-ballot openings). This hides when an opening was sent, not which challenged ballot it opens; keeping that challenged ballot's board entry from being tied to the cast ballot is P1-13's.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3a, [[0021-challenged-ballots]]): challenged ballots never reach the board during voting; an opening carries its own core and is published only after close, at the voter's request. An opening whose core is a cast ballot's never changes what counts, and the operator must refuse it. Residual: an opening gives the core's randomness, so a voter can turn a challenge into a receipt by casting the opened core under their own nullifier with their own tooling, and anyone given an opening during voting can cast it; re-voting still overrides a sold ballot unless the buyer watches until close (T-45).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3b): [[challenge]] specifies publication: openings only through the board path, in segments after the counted extent's last (`s_S`) and up to `t_S + OPENING_WINDOW`, so no ballot that lands later can count; `opening-cast` looks for any ballot commitment the opening opens (an opened `C_j`, or `h^{r_j}` or `g · h^{r_j}` under an opened `r_j`, same `definition_hash`, at any option), never tracking codes or whole `vss`, since an opened core's commitments or randomness can be cast again under fresh share hashes, another tier or re-blinded coefficients; an opened `C_j` cast in another election, and a commitment derived from an opened one by a chosen relation, are residuals nothing reports; `opening-cast`, `opening-early`, `opening-late` and `opening-repeat` are reported against the board operator, never changing what counts. The residual above stands ([[challenge]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3b follow-up): an opening reveals every coefficient of every `f_j` and `g_j`, every seat's shares and each seat's `e_i` and `w_i`, not only `r_j`; `opening-cast` now matches a ballot commitment `h^s` or `g · h^s` for any of them, but never for a vote `v_j`, which is public and would let a ballot carrying `g` or `h` block every opening. Randomness derived from revealed scalars by a chosen relation stays a residual ([[challenge]], Opened commitments).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-13 part 3b, second follow-up): the scalars 0 and 1 are left out of what an opening opens, rather than each vote: an out-of-rules vote is any scalar a ballot could use as randomness, so it is matched; `openedCommitments` builds the set once per opening, so a verifier's `opening-cast` costs no group operation per ballot (T-52).
- 2026-10-13 (P2-16 part 2a, [[challenge]] Opening version 3): `opening-cast` matches a version-4 half against every VSS commitment of the opening, but a half that reuses a revealed scalar as randomness for a fresh value has no public test; publishing the opening then strips that half's blinding, so the other panel alone reads the vote (T-14).
- 2026-10-13 (P2-16 part 2b-4, `packages/crypto`): `openedCommitmentsV3` and `opensBallotCommitmentV4` implement Opening version 3's rule: a version-4 ballot's `C'` against each `C_j` and `h^s`, `g · h^s` for every scalar the opening reveals (both halves' coefficients and seat shares, both panels' `e_i` and `w_i`, each `r_j` and `v_j`, but 0 and 1), and either half's constant term against every VSS commitment and `C_j` of the opening. A half reusing a revealed scalar for a fresh value stays the residual [[challenge]] names.
