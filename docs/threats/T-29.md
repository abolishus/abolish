# T-29: Tally or decryption manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A trustee or coordinator publishes a wrong decryption share or combines shares wrongly.

## Mitigation

Each trustee's summed shares are checked by the other trustees against the product of the public VSS commitments, and the published opening of each cell must open the product of the public commitments, which every verifier recomputes from the board ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], [[0015-tally-publication]], [[tally]]; P1-15, P1-19). A wrong opening is attributed to its signer; a wrong summed share is known only to the trustees, because summed shares aren't published (T-15).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): the tally is specified ([[tally]], [[0015-tally-publication]] option C): per cell, the product of the counted ballots' VSS commitments; trustees exchange summed shares privately and check each against it, so a wrong sum or a sum over another set of ballots is excluded; any trustee holding `k` passing sums publishes the cell's opening, which everyone checks against the aggregate commitment (`tally-opening`) with a count of at most the cell's ballots (`tally-range`). A wrong result can't pass; a wrong opening is attributed to its signer, but a wrong summed share only among the trustees, since publishing per-seat sums would leak ballots (T-15). `packages/crypto` implements it (`openCell`, `checkOpening`), with vectors from an independent implementation.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15, part 2a, [[tally]], Opening record): an opening is published as a tally opening (record type `0x0006`), signed with the combining seat's per-election signing key over the definition hash, the cell, the counted set (`aggregate_hash`) and the opening, so a failing opening is attributed only to a registered seat whose signature verifies over this election and cell; a replayed, relabelled or re-signed opening is unattributed and never frames its signer. A passing opening counts whoever signed it, so stripping signatures can't stop the tally. `checkTally` implements verifier 7.2 to 7.4, with vectors from an independent implementation.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15, part 2b, [[tally]], Summed-share message): summed shares travel signed by the sender's per-election key, bound to the definition, cell, counted set and both seats, so a recipient knows which seat sent a failing sum; `aggregate_hash` names the sender's counted set by its constant-term aggregates, and the summed-share check against the recipient's own aggregate is what shows the sums are over the same set. The signature gives no formal public proof of a wrong sum (checking a decryption needs the recipient's ML-KEM secret), though a recipient that publishes a message's AEAD key gives convincing evidence of what the sender sent; the protocol defines no such complaint, since it would publish per-seat values (ADR 0015), so a wrong summed share stays attributed only among the trustees.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-12 part 3b, [[delivery]]): a trustee's complaint is signed with its per-election key over the definition, ballot hash, seat and proof, so an unfounded complaint is attributed only to a registered seat whose signature verifies over this election, ballot and seat; a proven complaint excludes the ballot whoever signed it. A share receipt contradicted by a proven complaint about the same seat and ballot is reported against that seat.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-1, [[keyed-nullifier]]): a seat's keyed-nullifier answer is signed with its identity key and checked signature first, so an answer failing only its proof is a wrong answer attributed to its seat; anything failing earlier is attributed to no one.
