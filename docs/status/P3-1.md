# P3-1: ADR: Tier 2 identity provider

- State: in review ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]: zkPassport, accepted by the agent, owner may veto)
- Branch: `claude/p3-1`
- Phase: Phase 3: Tier 2 ZK identity, Semaphore groups, indexer, per-tier results

## Scope

ADR: zkPassport vs Self vs longfellow-zk. Must confirm proofs verify off-chain or on the chosen L2, with no third-chain dependency. Rule A applies (an identity-scheme decision is the agent's, with an owner veto); only the owner-only question below gets `needs-decision`.

## Outcome

[[0010-tier2-identity-provider]] chooses zkPassport:

- **No third chain:** it verifies off-chain in TypeScript with bb.js, or on Base.
- **Proving:** client-side, on the device.
- **Uniqueness:** a per-document nullifier scoped to Abolish.
- **Binding:** arbitrary bound data, so the proof commits to our identity commitment.
- **Coverage:** US passports.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0 for the circuits and the app.

The other two were rejected:

- **Self** depends on Celo state, and proves registration in a TEE that receives the document data.
- **longfellow-zk** has no uniqueness nullifier.

## Hand-offs

To P3-4, with P3-2 and [[membership]]:

- Verify locally with the pinned bb.js against vendored, provenance-checked zkPassport verification keys. Never use the hosted verifier or zkPassport's upgradeable contracts for anything that affects a result. Allowing the publisher needs a `ci:` PR first.
- Confirm that zkPassport's bb version matches the toolchain pins, or move all four pins together.
- The election definition fixes zkPassport's certificate and circuit roots at an L2 block. The verifier recomputes them from mirrored, archived certificate lists.
- The bound data is the identity commitment, the tier group, the chain ID and the registry, under an Abolish domain tag. The verifier attributes each addition by what the proof binds.
- Use a per-deployment scope and the non-salted nullifier. The verifier counts one addition per scoped nullifier.
- Mock documents only in the reference election. MOCK nullifiers are rejected off local chains.
- **Zero-knowledge gate:** before any real document is registered on any public chain, establish that the published proof is statistically zero-knowledge. If that can't be established, label the question `needs-decision` for the owner: publish the proofs anyway (a computational reading of "No PII on-chain"), or keep them off the board and use attested additions (T-06). Tier 2 stays on mock documents until the owner answers.

## Known-weak

The full list is in the ADR's Residuals. The main points:

- passive authentication only;
- zkPassport's circuits have no public audit yet;
- zkPassport's oracle chooses which certificates its roots admit;
- an issuing state can tell which of its documents registered;
- voters run zkPassport's app until P5-3 and P5-4 ship ours.

The facts were read from each project's docs and repositories on 2026-10-11. P3-4 re-checks them before building.
