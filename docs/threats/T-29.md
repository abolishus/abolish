# T-29: Tally or decryption manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A trustee or coordinator publishes a wrong decryption share or combines shares wrongly.

## Mitigation

Each trustee's summed shares are checked by the other trustees against the product of the public VSS commitments, and the published opening of each cell must open the product of the public commitments, which every verifier recomputes from the board ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], [[0015-tally-publication]], [[tally]]; P1-15, P1-19). A wrong opening is attributed to its signer; a wrong summed share is known only to the trustees, because summed shares aren't published (T-15).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): the tally is specified ([[tally]], [[0015-tally-publication]] option C): per cell, the product of the counted ballots' VSS commitments; trustees exchange summed shares privately and check each against it, so a wrong sum or a sum over another set of ballots is excluded; any trustee holding `k` passing sums publishes the cell's opening, which everyone checks against the aggregate commitment (`tally-opening`) with a count of at most the cell's ballots (`tally-range`). A wrong result can't pass; a wrong opening is attributed to its signer, but a wrong summed share only among the trustees, since publishing per-seat sums would leak ballots (T-15). `packages/crypto` implements it (`openCell`, `checkOpening`), with vectors from an independent implementation.
