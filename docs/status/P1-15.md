# P1-15: Summed shares, tally and destruction

- State: in progress (part 1 merged in #55; part 2, the summed-share messages and the tally transcript, in progress; parts 3 and 4 to follow)
- Branch: `claude/p1-15`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Per-trustee summed shares checked against the public VSS commitments, tally combination and the opening of the aggregate commitment (only over the cells of one partition), the tally transcript, and signed destruction of keys and stored shares after the tally.

## Plan

The item is built in parts, each its own PR on this branch's name:

1. **Summed shares and cell openings** (this part): [[tally]] and [[0015-tally-publication]]. The cell aggregates, each trustee's summed shares and their check, the combination of the `k` lowest passing seats, the published opening and its checks, and coarser totals. Needs only P1-12 part 1's committed vote and shares.
2. **The messages and the tally transcript** (`0x0006`, `abolish/v1/tally-transcript`): the private message carrying a trustee's summed shares to the other trustees (encrypted under P1-12 part 2's hybrid KEM, signed with the sender's per-election key from P1-14 part 2), and the transcript carrying each cell's opening, signed by the trustee that combined it; its window before the seal ([[election-definition]], The seal); [[verifier]] 7.3 and 7.4; retiring `abolish/v1/fs/tally-share` (no proof is needed beyond the VSS check). Several openings may be posted for one cell: the cell passes if any of them passes, and an unsigned failure is never attributed to a trustee, so a relay can't crowd out an honest opening. Waits on P1-14 part 2 and P1-12 part 2.
3. **Destruction**: each trustee's signed statement, on the board, that it destroyed its per-election KEM key and stored share ciphertexts once the tally is anchored ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], Consequences; T-14, T-69). Waits on P1-14 part 2 and P1-12 part 2.
4. **Regional cells** ([[verifier]] 2.8, T-16): per-region roots, the size floor in [[parameters]] and a later definition version. Waits on the owner's answer to the small-cell question in [[0006-trustees]]; until then the partition is the tiers only.

## What was built (part 1)

- [[0015-tally-publication]] (`Accepted (agent) — owner may veto`): option C, the tally publishes only each cell's opening; trustees exchange summed shares privately. Rejected: publishing summed shares (A, ADR 0002 as written: leaks ballots with fewer than `k` posted shares to a future adversary; narrowing ADR 0002 is the owner's) and re-randomising them with zero-sharings (B: keeps public attribution, but adds an interactive round with its own agreement and liveness problems).
- [[tally]], a new spec page: aggregates `Ê_{c,j,t}` (identity for an empty cell); summed shares `(V_{c,i,j}, R_{c,i,j})`, sent only to other trustees; the trustees' summed-share check; `Q` = the `k` lowest passing seats and Lagrange at 0 (`tally-quorum` while fewer than `k` pass); the published opening and the verifier's checks `tally-missing`, `tally-opening` and `tally-range`, which fail the election; coarser totals derived by adding cells, never opened; residuals.
- [[verifier]] 7.1, 7.2, 7.4 and 8.4 follow it; the spec and vectors READMEs list it; [[STATUS]] lists the ADR under Decisions to review.
- `packages/crypto/src/tally.ts`: `aggregateCell`, `sumShares`, `checkSummedShares`, `lagrangeAtZero`, `openCell` (a trustee's combination) and `checkOpening` (the verifier's check), `deriveTotal`.
- Threat files T-14, T-15, T-16, T-29, T-54.

## Tested (part 1)

- `docs/spec/vectors/tally.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`packages/crypto/scripts/tally-vectors.py`, ballots from `ballot-validity-vectors.py`; openings found by Gaussian elimination over every coefficient and cross-checked against the Lagrange coefficients): 11 cells (honest plurality; approval with two excluded trustees; a quorum failure with a sum missing a ballot, swapped options and an absent seat; high seats only; empty; one ballot; `MAX_TRUSTEES`; `MAX_OPTIONS`; a vote of 2 failing `tally-range`; two tiers of one election), 16 published openings checked by the verifier (every valid cell's opening; a count or randomness off by one and swapped options, `tally-opening`; short, missing and a count encoded as `V + ℓ`, `tally-missing`; the vote-of-2 cell's, `tally-range`) and a derived overall total. TypeScript reproduces every aggregate, summed share, verdict, combined set, Lagrange coefficient, opening and opening verdict.
- fast-check properties (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): honest sums from any `k` or more seats open each cell to its counts, and any other `k` seats interpolate to the same opening; any changed summed share fails and is excluded; a sum over the cell minus any one ballot fails for every seat.
- Mutation checks by hand: dropping the summed-share check or the range check fails tests. An honest combination always passes `tally-opening` (an identity in the exponent), so the combiner's copy of that check is reached only by the verifier's vectors.

## Known-weak (part 1)

- **Nothing sends, posts or signs summed shares or openings yet** (part 2), so 7.3 and 7.4 and signer attribution aren't implemented.
- **A corrupt trustee sees every seat's summed share** ([[tally]], Residuals; T-14, T-15): wherever posted shares fall into the pattern (a relay or trustees' downtime) and with a later break of their classical encryption, it can complete a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares, or disclose the sums unattributably; a seized trustee yields the same. Not mitigated; [[0015-tally-publication]] option B would close it only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1`.
- **Ballots readable after a break shrink a cell's anonymity set**: [[verifier]] 8.2 now reports the number of counted ballots with fewer than `k` valid posted shares per cell, for P1-19 to implement.
- **Several openings per cell** are specified (`tally-conflict`), but `checkOpening` takes one; part 2 implements the rule with the transcript.
- **A wrong summed share isn't publicly attributable**, only known to the trustees; a wrong opening is (T-29).
- **No published vectors exist for this composition.** The independent implementation and the reviews carry the weight (T-36).
- **Scalar arithmetic isn't constant time.** Summing and interpolation use JavaScript `bigint` on a trustee's machine; per-ballot shares are summed, not branched on.

## `crypto-review` on #55

- Blocking, fixed: the residual described the wrong leak, and the report flag (then 8.2a) missed the real one. Publishing summed shares fixes the summed polynomials at every seat, so a ballot with fewer than `k` posted shares can be completed at a seat where it is the only unposted ballot (with `k = 4`, `n = 6`: three posted shares plus one completed point), after a break of the posted shares' classical encryption alone; it happens with or without a relay arranging it, and the flag failed for any panel with `n ≤ 2k − 2`. Fixed at the root: [[0015-tally-publication]] publishes only openings, so no per-seat value is public; 8.2a is removed; the remaining corrupt-trustee residual is stated in [[tally]], T-14 and T-15. T-15's Status and Mitigation lines are updated (also `claude-review`'s non-blocking finding), and the adversary is A-11, not A-6.
- Non-blocking, fixed: `S_c` now names the share-delivery deadline and proven-complaint exclusions of [[0002-everlasting-privacy]].
- Non-blocking, moot: `Q` and the 8.4 report counted passing but unsigned summed shares, which anyone could compute; with [[0015-tally-publication]] no summed share is on the board, and 8.4 names the signer of each opening.

## Independent review of the fix

A fresh subagent reviewed the ADR 0015 fix against `.github/review/crypto-review.md`. It confirmed that publishing only `(F(0), G(0))` leaks nothing beyond the count, even to an adversary with discrete logs and every posted share (each posted share adds one independent equation; fewer than `k` leave `g_b(0)` free), and that the ADR's leak condition and example are right. Findings:

- Blocking, fixed: [[verifier]] 7.4 attributed a summed share found on the board to its signer, but every recipient holds the signed message and summed shares need no signature to be used, so an honest trustee could be framed. A disclosure is now reported against its sender or any recipient, never its signer alone, and stated as unattributable in [[tally]] and T-14.
- Non-blocking, fixed: option B closes the corrupt-trustee residual only against coalitions of fewer than `k − 1` (and not at all for `k = 2`); several openings for one cell are defined (`tally-conflict`, any passing opening counts, all must agree); summed shares never go through the board, IPFS or the archive and are destroyed with the KEM key (part 3); [[0015-tally-publication]] lists every statement in ADRs 0002 and 0003 and T-14 it supersedes, and T-14's Mitigation is updated; ballots readable after a break shrink a cell's anonymity set, now reported by [[verifier]] 8.2.
- Nits, fixed: `checkSummedShares` itself rejects non-canonical scalars (and the Python generator's combination does too); the vectors say `received` and "sends", not "posted"; the residual names trustees' downtime and seizure as well as a relay; a vector for an opening count encoded as `V + ℓ` (`tally-missing`).
- Third pass: all ten findings resolved; its five new non-blocking points fixed: an opening's signed message binds the definition hash, the cell and `m`, and a failing opening is attributed only then (no replay framing); openings count within the sealed extent, signed or not, with 8.4 reporting an unsigned one; the per-cell check order is explicit and `tally-conflict` has no vector because it needs `log_g h`; the 8.2 flag counts postings that pass the public check at distinct seats, with T-15 and T-16 history lines; the list of who may have disclosed summed shares includes seizure and harvest. Left as is: `checkSummedShares` doesn't know `n` (it can't, and `openCell` checks seats against it).
