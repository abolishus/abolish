# T-06: Group stuffing by the operator

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned** for Tier 2 ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]), subject to its zero-knowledge gate. For Tier 0 and Tier 1, the operator can add members with no external check: **not mitigated** there beyond public counts.

## Threat

Whoever adds commitments to a Semaphore group adds fake members.

## Mitigation

Tier 2 additions carry a verifiable proof of the document (P3-1 ADR must require that the proof, or its verification, be public and re-checkable). Group sizes are public.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): an operator who adds one commitment adds at most one voting identity: membership is proven against a complete depth-32 tier tree that verifiers rebuild from the board, never a LeanIMT root, in which one added value could hide a private tree of identities ([[membership]], Tier tree).
- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]: each Tier 2 addition carries a zkPassport proof that the verifier re-checks from the board. Publishing those proofs waits on P3-4 establishing that they are statistically zero-knowledge; if that fails, the owner chooses between publishing them anyway and attested additions, under which Tier 2 stuffing is checkable only by trusting the attesters.
