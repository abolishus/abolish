# T-63: Contract bug

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-9, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: an immutable contract can't be patched; the fix is a new versioned deployment and an election-definition reference to it.

## Threat

An immutable contract has a flaw (reentrancy, unchecked input, a broken invariant).

## Mitigation

Foundry unit, fuzz and invariant tests with a deeper `ci` profile; `crypto-review`; testnet only until the owner signs off (P1-18).
