# T-26: Unprovable drops and false complaints

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A voter whose ballot is missing can't prove to anyone else that they submitted it, and a false complaint can't be refuted, so a drop becomes a he-said-she-said.

## Mitigation

Receipts are meant to be attributable: either the operator's signature on the board it showed the voter, with the voter's entry last, checked for inclusion at cast ([[receipts]], [[0013-ballot-receipts]]), or the voter's own L2 inclusion via direct submit (P4-2). What a receipt is evidence of later is specified in [[receipts]], Later checks ([[0023-receipt-evidence]]): verdicts a third party reaches from the definition, L1 and the board, bounded by a dispute end that salted L1 commitments outlast. Until the receipt keys are bound in the definition and registered on L1 (P1-13b part 2b) the keys that judge a receipt are the client's pinned set, so it is evidence to its voter but not yet attributable to anyone else. The dispute procedure goes in a runbook.
