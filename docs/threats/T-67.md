# T-67: Analytics and error-tracking leakage

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: `apps/web` analytics can still reveal which poll pages an account viewed; T-10 and T-11 apply to that data.

## Threat

PostHog or logs capture poll participation, ballot data or identifiers.

## Mitigation

PostHog only in `apps/web`, proxied through our domain; never in `apps/ballot` (bundle test, P2-8); no secret, ballot or voting identity in logs (dev skill).
