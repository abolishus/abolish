- State: in progress (part 1a, the root record, its leaves, leaf availability, the delivery check and capacity in the spec with vectors, merged in #287; part 1b-1, the board's root call and contract-event version 5, merged in #295; part 1b-2, the registry's rule 9, verifier 2.3 and every profile on version 5, merged in #300; part 2, `packages/crypto` reading roots, merged in #304; part 3, the verifier reading roots and the trustee node's roots and leaf publication, next)

## Scope

Build [[0031-national-delivery-obligation]] (option C, accepted by the owner on 2026-10-16). Needed before the first civic poll, not before launch: at launch every trustee is synthetic (the transitional panel, every seat ours), so a root adds nothing checkable against us until independent trustees hold seats. at `c_I` each trustee seat commits what it owes as one signed Merkle root through the contract path, its leaves (receipts, then complaints over a second root) published under [[0029-board-data-availability]]'s availability rule, and the delivery check reads a seat as delivered for a ballot from a leaf with its inclusion proof. Per-record batches ([[P1-12d]], [[P1-12e]]) stay the path for small elections and clients.

- [[delivery]]: the root record and its signed tag, its leaves' layout and content address, what makes a root's leaves unavailable and the report code against the seat, and the capacity check reduced to a constant per seat for elections that use roots.
- [[contracts]]: the board call and its event, in a new contract-event version (5), which rule 9 then pins and 2.3 requires; decided before any registry is deployed.
- `packages/crypto` with an independent Python model and vectors; `packages/contracts`; `packages/verifier` (coordinate with [[P1-19]] and [[P4-3]]).

## What was built (part 1a)

- [[delivery]], Obligation root: record type `0x001a`, version 1 (fixed by ballot versions 1 to 3; versions 4 and 5 fix none yet). `definition_hash`, `seat`, `kind` (1 receipts, 2 complaints), `count`, `root`, `leaves_cid` and one `p256-sig` under `abolish/v1/sig/obligation-root` over the 106 bytes before it; 173 bytes. Leaves are batches' pairs and entries, strictly ascending by ballot hash; the leaf object is their concatenation, named by its canonical `objectCid`; the obligation tree is RFC 9162 under `abolish/v1/obligation/leaf` and `/node`, with RFC 9162 inclusion proofs. At most one root per seat and kind fills its slot.
- [[delivery]], Leaf availability: the seat serves its leaves from submission; each counted-extent attester names, by `H(DS("abolish/v1/obligation-root", record))`, every root in time whose leaves it holds and has checked; a root is available with `q − k + 1` of the default panel's seats naming it, and no seat sums before it holds every available root's leaves, otherwise withheld (`root-withheld`, against its seat, with who named it and who attested without). Consistency (`root-inconsistent`), the bound `2M` (`root-count`), the fetch cap, and unverifiable rather than failed when an available root's leaves can't be fetched.
- [[delivery]], Delivery check: a root is read once, with `delivery-stray`, `delivery-seat`, `root-signature`, `root-repeat`, `delivery-late`, `root-count`, `root-withheld` and `root-inconsistent` in that order, then leaf by leaf as a batch's pairs and entries are, reports carrying `leaf`.
- [[delivery]], What a node owes and Capacity: an election commits roots when, alone, its batched obligation doesn't fit; its seats then send one receipt root by `c_D` and one complaint root by `c_K`, each interval carrying `s · ROOT_CALL_GAS` (200,000, provisional until part 1b measures it). Until the call, the code and P4-3 part 2c exist, `forcedPathFits` counts every election with batches, so nodes refuse those elections.
- Client rules (a client may check its leaf's inclusion as evidence, and never stands down on it), Decoding, Boarding, Direct submit, Residuals (the availability committee, the fetch, a root binding the seat's view) and Test vectors; [[verifier]] 5.9, [[versioning]], [[domain-separation]], the spec index, `RECORD_TYPES` and `TAG_REGISTRY`.
- `docs/spec/vectors/delivery-root.json` from `packages/crypto/scripts/delivery-root-vectors.py`, which reuses the delivery, batch, board and obligation generators and writes its own dag-pb CIDs, checked against all 15 CIDs of `content-addressing.json` (Kubo's and ipfs-unixfs-importer's) before printing.

## Known-weak (part 2)

- **Nothing passes roots yet.** The verifier still holds 5.9 and the tally unverifiable at a root's entry (part 1b-2) until part 3 fetches leaf objects and [[P4-3]] part 2c brings the attestations; the trustee node doesn't build or send roots.
- **Leaf objects are read whole, in memory.** A national root's object is up to `2M · 128` bytes; part 3's fetch reads it block by block under the cap `64·c` or `128·c` and must hand the check what it needs without holding a source's excess. Bytes that don't give the CID are a failed fetch, so a dag-pb object whose blocks all hash but whose DAG isn't the canonical layout reads as unverifiable here, not `root-inconsistent` as the spec says; part 3, which sees the blocks, must report it against the seat.
- **The availability input is the caller's.** `DeliveryRoots.attestations` must be the attestations that count (recorded by `t_A'`, through the contract path); their record is [[P4-3]] part 2c's, and the check only refuses seats outside the key set and repeats.
- **An undecided result has no ballots.** When `unverifiable` lists a root, `ballots` and `records` are empty; a caller that reads `ballots` without checking `unverifiable` would take every ballot as uncounted. No caller passes roots yet (the verifier holds 5.9 at a root before calling, the sdk's simulation sends none); part 3 makes the verifier read `unverifiable` as its own unverifiable row.
