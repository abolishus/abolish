# T-28: Invalid ballots

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-9
- Status: **Planned** (P1-4 accepted by the agent, owner may veto: homomorphic tally with validity proofs for plurality and approval, built by P1-12, which is blocked by the three owner questions in [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]). For the ranked-choice mixnet, validity is checked on decrypted ballots instead, and the threat changes shape.

## Threat

A ballot encrypts an over-vote, a negative count or a value outside the allowed options, corrupting a homomorphic tally.

## Mitigation

Disjunctive Chaum–Pedersen (CDS) validity proofs over the Pedersen commitments, per option and for the sum (P1-4, P1-12, [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); trustees also check each share against the public VSS commitments, and a proven complaint excludes a ballot with an invalid share; the verifier rejects ballots without valid proofs.
