# T-50: L2 censorship, reorg or failure

- Area: Availability and censorship resistance (G-10)
- Adversary: A-1, A-7
- Status: **Planned** (P1-5 accepted by the agent for development and testnet, owner may veto: Base; mainnet waits for the owner). Residual: forced inclusion takes up to 12 hours on Base (24 on Arbitrum One) and costs L1 gas; voters who can't use it lose ballots censored near close. The sequencer is one US company on either chain. Censoring or pricing out the board's anchor transaction on L1 for longer than the anchoring deadline fails a running election permanently ([[board]], Timing).

## Threat

The sequencer censors registrations or ballots, the L2 halts, or a ballot accepted on a soft confirmation is reorged out.

## Mitigation

The L2 is L2BEAT Stage ≥ 1 at decision time; [[0004-l2-choice]] recommends Base for its 12-hour forced-inclusion bound (P1-5); a live Base sequencer can delay a deposit only by its drift bound (30 minutes today). The close rule must accept ballots forced through L1 before close (P1-18). Receipts and the verifier count only L2 ballots derived from finalized L1 data, not soft-confirmed ones (P1-13, P1-19). Hourly L1 anchoring protects the board's history, not L2 inclusion.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-16): censoring or pricing out the anchor transaction on L1 for longer than the board's anchoring deadline fails the running election permanently ([[board]], Timing). P4-1 sets the deadline against this.
- 2026-10-12 (P4-1 part 1): the anchoring deadline is 12 hours, so L1 censorship or fee spikes shorter than that can't make an election miss an anchor; the closing archive locator is a separate call that doesn't share the deadline ([[contracts]], Board anchor).
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-1, [[0030-close-rule]]): decided, not built. A ballot forced through our L1 relay counts by its L1 stamp, so one sent before close counts however late the sequencer includes it. A batcher outage at close never drops an ordinary ballot that is in a block by close, but one longer than δ fails the election if a late-batched ballot would change its result (`close-contested`).
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-2a, [[close]]): specified. A ballot in an L2 block by `closes_at` is never dropped for a slow batch; if its block isn't derivable from L1 by `closes_at + δ` and it would change a result, the election fails `close-contested`, unattributed. A client that sees its ballot's block not yet derivable from L1 well before close may relay a copy through L1, which the check keeps.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-2b-1): built in the election registry. It refuses a definition whose δ is below `MIN_INCLUSION_BOUND`, ten minutes, over three times the longest gap between Base Sepolia's batch posts measured over six hours (180 seconds; [[parameters]]), so an honest batcher's normal rhythm doesn't fail an election `close-contested`. An outage longer than δ around close still can.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-2b-2a): built in the election registry. It refuses a definition assuming a `sequencing_window` above `MAX_SEQUENCING_WINDOW`, 3,600 L1 blocks ([[parameters]]), so no registered election lets [[verifier]] 2.6 accept a chain whose forced inclusion takes longer than the 13 hours the post-close windows allow for it. If Base raises its window, polls open meanwhile fail 2.6 until a new registry is deployed.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-2b-2b-2a): specified, not built. The L1 relay ([[contracts]], L1 relay) records each forced ballot with the L1 block's number and timestamp and deposits it to the board through the portal, so a censored voter's ballot reaches the board within the sequencing window and is judged by its L1 stamp. A record of up to `MAX_RELAY_RECORD` bytes fits the portal's data cap, which every ballot version holds. L2 governance can still censor deposits through the portal.
- 2026-10-14 (P1-18 part 4c-2b-2b-2b): specified, not built. The board's relayed-ballot call ([[contracts]], Board, Relayed ballots) accepts a deposit only from the relay's alias, checks its ballot as an ordinary one and its stamp against the poll's window, and emits `RelayedBallotSubmitted` for every deposit it accepts, a byte-identical repeat included, so a sequencer that copies a forced ballot into an ordinary block and withholds it can't turn the forced copy into a no-op. Contract-event version 3 archives the event.
