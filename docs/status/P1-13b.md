# P1-13b: Receipt evidence model

- State: in progress (part 1, [[0023-receipt-evidence]], merged in #145; part 2a, the later checks in the spec, merged in #147; part 2b, the receipt keys in the definition and the L1 registry, next, with the definition version after #141's; part 3, the code and vectors, after it)
- Branch: `claude/p1-13b`

## Scope

The receipt's later checks ([[receipts]], Later checks): the checks after the segment's deadline against the checkpoints anchored on L1, the check with a copy of the anchored board, and what makes a receipt evidence a third party can check, with [[board]]'s client rule 2. Split out of P1-13 part 2 (#48) at the owner's request in the session of 2026-10-12.

## Requirements

From #48's reviews (its draft of these checks and the review threads are in #48's history):

- **Time.** A receipt carries no time, so a key obtained after an election could forge evidence against it for good. The evidence must be bounded in time (the draft used 30 days of L1 time after the seal), and a voter's evidence of a real drop must survive the bound (the draft used salted L1 commitments carried by any transaction whose input is a list of 32-byte commitments, judged at `min(latest, dispute_end)`, never at the commitment's block).
- **Withheld boards.** An operator can anchor a fork that no checkpoint contradicts from L1 alone, and withhold the board until any bound has passed; clients must keep their evidence (the draft committed every receipt in doubt).
- **Keys.** The receipt key set that judges an election is fixed before voting starts (in the election definition, or recorded per election from the release current when it opens), so a later release can't void receipts, and a key not bound to an election can't forge against it.
- **Presence.** A dropped ballot is shown only by a valid ballot that no admitted entry of the anchored board carries by `(tier, ν, M)`, whatever its selection outcome; a rejected or re-randomised copy proves nothing. Needs P1-12's ballot record and admission.
- **Privacy.** Commitments are salted and posted through relayers; proofs are fetched for every segment, never for the voter's own alone (T-45, T-11).
- **Admission and selection.** The voter's later check shows the ballot's admission verdict and whether it is the selected ballot of its tier and nullifier ([[revoting]], Residuals; T-41, T-42).
- **The voter's own view.** After any bound, the voter's client still shows its own result, marked as no longer evidence (G-7).

## Part 1: the ADR

- [[0023-receipt-evidence]], option A: receipt keys listed in the election definition (`receipt_keys`, in the next unfrozen definition version, coordinated with P1-15 part 4b's version 2) and registered beforehand on L1 in an append-only registry only the anchoring authority writes (never on L2, where governance can forge any sender, T-71), so evidence is attributed to it alone; a dispute end `t_E` (the seal's anchoring window plus the pinned receipt version's 30 days), after which a receipt is evidence only with a salted commitment in L1 calldata from before `t_E` (opened with a beacon-state proof of its block), made by the client when the receipt is in doubt; the client's later check from every segment's entry-hash list and a whole verifier report, fetched at a time independent of its segment; verdicts `not-evidence`, `board-failed`, `pending`, `unanchored`, `forked`, `included`, `unresolved`, and a drop as an `unanchored` or `forked` receipt for an admissible ballot that no admitted entry carries by `(tier, ν, M)`. Rejected: keys from the release history with no time bound (B), and every receipt committed at cast on L2 (C).
- Independent review (in-session): four blocking findings, all fixed: a receipt for a segment outside the election could frame an honest operator (now `not-evidence`, and the operator must refuse such entries); attribution rested on a release-pinned key list the operator controls (now an L1 registry the anchoring authority writes); the dispute window followed the presented receipt's version (now the definition's pinned version); a dropped entry in an unanchored segment escaped every verdict (now `unanchored`). Also taken: a canonicality proof so evidence survives L1 history expiry, the inclusion path in `not-evidence`, fetch timing independent of the segment, the tie escape exempted from the selection alarm, the close rule in the drop rule, and a T-69 history line
- Threat files T-26, T-25, T-69 and T-71 record the change.
- CI review (`claude-review`): the first version put the registry on L2, trusting its sender, which L2 governance can forge (T-71); moved to L1. A seized key could never be retired, so a hostile creator could list it in any later election; the registry gains an append-only retirement record, and a definition listing a key retired by its `opens_at` fails. Its non-blocking note is taken too: `pending` and `board-failed` aren't doubt, so no commitment follows a cast closely. Its non-blocking note (recent blocks aren't yet in `historical_summaries`) is taken: the opening may use `block_roots`.
- Tested: documentation only; `vp check` formats it.

Known-weak:

- Before `t_E`, whoever holds a receipt key (a leak, a seizure, a compelled hand-over) can forge evidence against the anchoring authority for elections that opened before the key was retired, each until its `t_E` (T-69, T-53).
- A client that never checks before `t_E` and didn't commit at cast loses its evidence.
- A withheld board leaves committed evidence `unresolved` and the election unverifiable until P4-3 makes non-publication attributable.
- The whole-board hash list is 32 bytes per entry, heavy on weak connections; admission and selection come from a verifier report the client trusts.

## Part 2a: the later checks in the spec

- [[receipts]], Later checks: the dispute end `t_E`; the commitment `C = H(DS("abolish/v1/receipt-commitment", salt ‖ encode(receipt)))` as an aligned word of any L1 transaction's calldata; its opening (the transaction-trie proof, the execution header, the beacon block header, the payload branch at generalized index 412 for Capella and 812 for Deneb to Fulu, and the 13-hash branch to the period's `block_summary_root`, or `block_roots` while the period is incomplete) with six ordered checks and codes; entry-hash lists; the verdicts in order (`not-evidence` with reasons, `board-failed`, `pending`, `unanchored`, `forked`, `included`, `unresolved`); the drop rule; and five client rules (following the board, admission and selection from a whole report, alarms, committing in doubt, after `t_E`). Residuals extended.
- [[receipts]]: a receipt is decoded under the version the profile pins (which fixes its window); the operator never appends or signs an entry outside `f` to `lastSegment`, and check at cast 5 accepts only that range.
- [[board]]: client rule 2 replaced by the later checks; the entry-hash list as a derived, content-addressed object beside each segment object.
- [[parameters]]: `DISPUTE_WINDOW` (2,592,000 s); [[domain-separation]]: `abolish/v1/receipt-commitment`.
- Threat files T-24, T-25 and T-26 record the change.
- Not in this part: `receipt_keys`. [[0023-receipt-evidence]] puts it in the next unfrozen definition version, and #141 (P1-15 part 4b-1) is writing version 3 while this part is written; adding the field to its draft from here would edit another open PR's files. Until part 2b, the keys that judge a receipt are the client's pinned set, so the verdicts are the voter's own and not yet attributable by a third party, which [[receipts]] says.
- Independent review (in-session): no blocking findings; nine non-blocking, all taken: the interim key set is the one pinned when the receipt was accepted, stored with it, so a later release can't silence an alarm; every attribution is qualified until part 2b; a light-client judge proves the state fields itself (the opening can't carry them); an unknown fork in the judge's state, not only at the block, is `opening-fork-unknown`; pre-Capella blocks fail `opening-payload`; proof lengths are exact (8 or 9, and 0 or 13); the client's fetch schedule and its commitment time never depend on its segment; the trusted state is on `l1_chain_id`'s chain.
- Tested: documentation only; `vp check` formats it.
