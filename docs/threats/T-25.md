# T-25: Ballot or registration censorship

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1, A-7
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: direct submit needs gas; funding an address can itself link a voter (T-11-like), and voters are told so. Under P1-3 (option B), a ballot also needs a receipt from, or a public posting to, each trustee's intake by the delivery deadline; trustees can't block it (posting needs no cooperation), but a posted share has only classical privacy.

## Threat

Our servers refuse, delay or silently drop a valid submission.

## Mitigation

Receipts let the voter detect a drop: at cast only against the operator's own signed claim ([[receipts]]); a drop after the receipt, or a receipt over a made-up tree, only by the later checks ([[P1-13b]]). The direct-submit path lets anyone post a registration or ballot straight to the contracts (P1-18, P4-2); the indexer and verifier include direct-submitted ballots (P3-5), and each contract's event accumulator ([[contracts]]) lets the verifier prove that no direct-submitted ballot was left off the board, or boarded only after the counted extent the tally combines over ([[verifier]] 4.4, 5.6); a late entry that would change a counted set fails the election against the operator (`tally-late`, 7.1). The re-vote rule must not let the operator void a direct-submitted re-vote by withholding or reordering the voter's earlier ballots (requirement R3 in [[verifier]] check 6.1, P1-13).

## History

- 2026-10-12 (P1-15): the tally combines over the counted extent (entries with `segment ≤ s_S`), and a late entry is never ignored: a direct-submitted ballot first boarded after that extent fails 5.6, and any late entry that would change a cell's counted set fails the election as `tally-late`, both attributed to the operator ([[tally]], Tally window; [[verifier]] 5.6, 7.1). So holding back a re-vote past the counted extent fails loudly instead of keeping the earlier ballot counted. `s_S` is at least the period after close's, so client rule 1's tolerance can't place a receipted ballot outside it.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-18): the event accumulator that 4.4 and 5.6 rely on is specified and built ([[contracts]]); the direct-submit path itself is still to come.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): a registrant can't fail later elections by registering a forbidden commitment: it gets leaf 0 and never fails the definition, and group contracts must revert it ([[membership]], Commitments and removals). Direct-submit contracts must verify membership proofs against the registered tier root, so non-members can't post at all.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-13, receipts): [[board]]'s client rule 1 refuses an envelope with the wrong segment at cast without alarming: nothing in the refusal is checkable by anyone else, since the operator hasn't signed anything the client accepted. The client keeps the ballot and resubmits it or submits it directly ([[receipts]], Checks at cast).
