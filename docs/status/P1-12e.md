## Scope

With receipts batched ([[P1-12d]]), complaints bound the forced path's cap about as much as receipts: at about 157,000 gas each and two per member, an election alone fits 23,589 members with 3 seats at most, and elections whose complaint intervals overlap share the target almost wholly ([[delivery]], Capacity; STATUS, Known-weak; T-25, T-52, T-54).

- [[delivery]]: a share-complaint batch: one seat's complaints for one election under one signature, each with its own proof (the board never checks proofs), so one signature verification, storage key and event per batch. Its event needs a contract-event version 5, which the registry's rule 9 then pins and 2.3 requires; it must be decided before any registry is deployed.
- An ADR: a national electorate needs more than any record per receipt or complaint on L1. Options include committing a node's obligation as a Merkle root on L1, its leaves made available by [[P4-3]]'s data availability, with the delivery check reading inclusion proofs; or sizing the complaint obligation below two per member.
