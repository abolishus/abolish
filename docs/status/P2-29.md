## Scope

The gate before any official work: every everyday surface goes through at least three design iterations for UX quality ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Priority).

Surfaces: home, the stream and its card, topic and place pages, search, the poll page (open, live, sealed, closed), answering in the ballot client (arrive, questions, check, sending, done, receipt, change your answer, recovery), creating a poll, the author's list, sharing surfaces, the account and profile pages, notifications, and the system pages.

Each iteration is one PR per surface (or a small group):

1. **Review**: screenshots at 360px and desktop width, light and dark, of every state; a written critique against [[language]], the brief's design direction and the Center for Civic Design rules for the ballot; tap counts and copy read aloud; what a first-time user would misread.
2. **Fix**: the changes the review calls for, with the stories and tests updated.
3. **Record**: the before and after screenshots and what changed, in this file.

A surface is done after its third iteration finds nothing it would change. The everyday app is complete when every surface is done and [[P2-23]] to [[P2-28]] are done; then official work may start.

### System pages: iteration 1

Not found, Something went wrong and You're offline (`apps/web/src/components/site-layout.tsx`; stories `Components/Site layout`).

Review:

- **The offline page's "Try again" drew at 12px** where the error page's drew at 16px.
