# T-62: Contract admin abuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A single key upgrades or pauses a contract, or rewrites a group.

## Mitigation

Bulletin-board contracts are immutable; anything upgradeable sits behind a Safe multisig plus timelock; no single-key admin anywhere (P1-18).
