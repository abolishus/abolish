# P1-15: Summed shares, tally and destruction

- State: in progress (part 1: summed shares and cell openings, in review)
- Branch: `claude/p1-15`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Per-trustee summed shares checked against the public VSS commitments, tally combination and the opening of the aggregate commitment (only over the cells of one partition), the tally transcript, and signed destruction of keys and stored shares after the tally.

## Plan

The item is built in parts, each its own PR on this branch's name:

1. **Summed shares and cell openings** (this part): [[tally]]. The cell aggregates, each trustee's summed shares and their check, the combination of the `k` lowest passing seats and the opening checks, and coarser totals. Needs only P1-12 part 1's committed vote and shares.
2. **The tally transcript** (`0x0006`, `abolish/v1/tally-transcript`): the record a trustee posts with its summed shares per cell, signed with its per-election signing key (P1-14 part 2), its window before the seal ([[election-definition]], The seal), [[verifier]] 7.3 and 7.4, and retiring `abolish/v1/fs/tally-share` (no proof is needed beyond the VSS check). Waits on P1-14 part 2 for the per-election key.
3. **Destruction**: each trustee's signed statement, on the board, that it destroyed its per-election KEM key and stored share ciphertexts once the tally is anchored ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], Consequences; T-14, T-69). Waits on P1-14 part 2 and P1-12 part 2.

## What was built (part 1)

- [[tally]], a new spec page: aggregates `Ê_{c,j,t}` as the product of the counted ballots' VSS commitments (identity for an empty cell); summed shares `(V_{c,i,j}, R_{c,i,j})`; the summed-share check (Pedersen's equation against the aggregates; a failure is `tally-share`, excluded and reported, never an election failure); `Q` = the `k` lowest passing seats, Lagrange at 0, and the checks `tally-quorum`, `tally-opening` and `tally-range`, which fail the election; coarser totals derived by adding cells, never opened; residuals.
- [[verifier]] 7.1 and 7.2 point at it; the spec and vectors READMEs list it.
- `packages/crypto/src/tally.ts`: `aggregateCell`, `sumShares`, `checkSummedShares`, `lagrangeAtZero`, `openCell`, `deriveTotal`.
- Threat files T-16, T-29, T-54.

## Tested (part 1)

- `docs/spec/vectors/tally.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`packages/crypto/scripts/tally-vectors.py`, ballots from `ballot-validity-vectors.py`; the opening found by Gaussian elimination over every coefficient and cross-checked against the Lagrange coefficients): 11 cells (honest plurality; approval with two excluded trustees; a quorum failure with a sum missing a ballot, swapped options and an absent seat; high seats only; empty; one ballot; `MAX_TRUSTEES`; `MAX_OPTIONS`; a vote of 2 failing `tally-range`; two tiers of one election) and a derived overall total. TypeScript reproduces every aggregate, summed share, verdict, combined set, Lagrange coefficient and opening.
- fast-check properties (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): honest sums from any `k` or more seats open each cell to its counts, and any other `k` seats interpolate to the same opening; any changed summed share fails and is excluded; a sum over the cell minus any one ballot fails for every seat.
- Mutation checks by hand: dropping the summed-share check or the range check fails tests. `tally-opening` can't fail when the summed-share checks pass (it follows algebraically), so no test reaches it; it stays as the check the result rests on.

## Known-weak (part 1)

- **Nothing posts or signs summed shares yet** (part 2), so 7.3 and 7.4 and the attribution of a failing sum to a signer aren't implemented.
- **No published vectors exist for this composition.** The independent implementation and the review carry the weight (T-36).
- **Scalar arithmetic isn't constant time.** Summing and interpolation use JavaScript `bigint`; a trustee's summed shares are published, so only its per-ballot shares are secret, and those are summed, not branched on.
