## Scope

Live results as [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]] (Part 2) decides: an author may choose running totals, decrypted at scheduled anchors during voting, instead of sealed-until-close; civic referendums are always sealed. Running sets hold each nullifier's one ballot while it has exactly one and it is receipted by every qualified seat; re-voters are held out until the final tally. A tier's running totals are released only when every cell has changed by at least `B` nullifiers against every earlier release, and by `B` again after the released snapshot (the one-batch lag), with `MIN_LIVE_BATCH = 20` and a default of 50. The final tally is unchanged.

## Notes

- Threats: T-16 (differencing), T-45 (re-votes held out), T-01 (padding at Tier 0, not mitigated), T-29, T-54.
- Known-weak from the ADR: padding by an adversary who can cast `B − 1` known ballots into a quiet interval; combinations of three or more releases until part 1 settles them; a final tally can differ from a release by fewer than `B` after late ties or exclusions (flagged, never withheld).
- Part 1a found that [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]]'s running set doesn't work with its pairwise rule: when ballots leave running sets (re-voters, posted ballots), a single difference can have one voter alone on a side, three releases can give one ballot exactly, one re-vote freezes a tier's releases, and one seat's trustee can force departures by posting its share of any ballot. So running sets only grow (first delivered ballot, kept until the final tally), and [[tally]] proves that the smallest non-empty side of any combination of totals is a least positive directed cut, which with grow-only sets the pairwise batch bounds; the verifier computes it exactly for the final tally against the releases. The lag counts entries. All listed under Decisions to review. Two in-session independent review rounds and the first CI `crypto-review` found these step by step (the sign problem, the freeze, the final tally's other side, trustee-forced postings), and the rolling-ballot, lag-counting, deferral and receipt-withholding points, all addressed in the spec or stated as residuals.
- Tested (part 1a): `live-release.json`, printed by `packages/crypto/scripts/live-release-vectors.py`, which computes every cut two ways and checks the theorem by exhaustive search on small graphs; `packages/crypto` consumes it in part 2. The model takes admission per ballot as given, so the pending rules get ballot-level cases in part 1c.
- Known-weak (part 1a): the final tally can disclose a lone released re-voter's released ballot (flagged, never withheld); a zero count in a side of `B` discloses (`(1 − p)^B`), which also weakens re-voting against coercion in live polls (a higher floor needs a new definition version); an operator deferring one period's ballots, or withholding one seat's receipts, makes an interval quiet unseen by the other seats ([[P2-13]] must close both before any live poll with an author panel or an independent default panel); releases can include posted shares; padding at Tier 0.
