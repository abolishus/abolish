## Scope

[[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]], Parts 1 and 3:

- **Author-added trustees.** A ballot's opening is split additively in two; the default half is shared to the default panel and the author half to the author's panel, each with its own threshold, so a ballot opens only with both thresholds and the author panel can never lower secrecy below the default panel's. The definition binds the author panel's keys, threshold and ceremony transcript; the verifier checks them off chain. If fewer than `k_A` author seats take part at the close, the poll can never be counted, and the UI says so plainly.
- **The transitional panel.** At launch the default panel is ours alone (4-of-7, seats spread so no machine, provider or keyholder holds 4). Every page and receipt says "During launch, Abolish alone holds the key for this poll"; only everyday polls run under it, checked through a panel kind in the release pins and the trustee registry and a poll class in the definition; shares are destroyed after each final count; seats hand over to named independent trustees before the first civic-record poll.

## Plan

Each part is one PR on this branch's name. "Gate" means the CI `crypto-review` check (`docs/spec`, `packages/crypto`, `packages/verifier`, `packages/contracts`); "in-session" means the in-session `crypto-review` subagent review AGENTS.md requires for `core`, `sdk` and `apps/ballot`.

1. **Definition, panel kind and poll class** (gate), in two PRs: (a) spec and vectors (#193); (b) `packages/crypto`'s codec and rules for version 6 and the author panel identifier, checked against (a)'s labels. A new [[election-definition]] version carrying every field ADR 0027 adds: the poll class (everyday, organisational, civic referendum), the results mode with `live_every` and `B` (for [[P2-15]]), and the author panel (`author_threshold`, `author_keys`, `author_transcript_hash`); the author panel identifier under `abolish/v1/author-panel` and its validity (`1 ≤ k_A ≤ n_A ≤ MAX_TRUSTEES`, keys distinct from the default panel's) in [[trustee-identity]]; the panel kind and per-seat holder in the release pins; [[verifier]] 2.5's `panel-kind` (a civic-record class needs an `independent` default panel) and well-formedness (a civic referendum is sealed); `packages/crypto` codec and rules; vectors.
2. **Ballot and delivery** (gate), in two PRs: (a) spec: the ballot version with both sharings, the public check `E^D_{j,0} · E^A_{j,0} = C_j`, the validity statement binding both, [[share-encryption]]'s associated data and [[delivery]]'s receipt, posting and complaint versions binding the panel, the delivery check over every qualified seat of both panels, and Benaloh openings of both halves ([[challenge]]); (b) `packages/crypto` and vectors from an independent implementation.
3. **Ceremony and tally** (gate): [[ceremony]] for the author panel (`panel_id` from the definition, its stub checks, rule 7 per panel, approvals on the board); [[tally]]'s per-panel summed shares and partial openings, the combined range check, `tally-missing` when either panel's opening is absent, and destruction for both panels; `packages/crypto`; vectors.
4. **Verifier** (gate): 2.5, 3.1 and 3.2 per panel, the ballot and tally checks of parts 2 and 3, and 8.4's report of both panels, the panel kind, our seats and the transitional label.
5. **Contracts** (gate; before P1-18 deploys anything): the panel kind in the trustee registry's deployment and `PanelPinned`, and the election registry refusing a civic-record definition whose default panel isn't `independent`.
6. **Trustee node and transitional operations** (in-session where it touches protocol code): the [[P2-13]] node's author-panel mode, installable and runnable by an author's trustee; a runbook in `docs/runbooks/` for our transitional seats (machines, providers, keyholders, destruction procedure, the published layout and the commitment statement); the release that pins the transitional panel with its kind and seat holders.
7. **UI** (`apps/web`, `apps/ballot`, `packages/ui`; in-session for `apps/ballot`): the transitional label on every page and receipt (and its author-panel variant); adding trustees at creation (their `k`-of-`n`, key fingerprints, the liveness warning confirmed through the shared confirm dialog, offered during launch as protection from us); the poll page's one line for an author panel; the "can't be counted" result; the evidence view of both panels. Storybook stories with interaction and a11y tests.
8. **Handover** (waits on the owner, [[P1-7]]): a new trustee registry deployment and a release pinning the independent panel (or an intermediate one, labelled with our seat count), before the first civic-record poll.
9. **Reference election** (outside the gate): a poll with an author panel counted by both, one whose author panel fails at the close (`tally-missing`, with the plain result), and the transitional label in the report.

## What was built (part 1a, #193)

- [[election-definition]], Layout (version 6): version 5's fields, then `poll_class` (everyday, organisational vote, civic referendum), `results_mode` (sealed, live), `live_every`, `live_batch`, `author_threshold`, `author_keys` and `author_transcript_hash`, appended after `receipt_keys`, so version 6's bytes are version 5's followed by the new fields. Sections on the poll class and results (classes 2 and 3 on the civic record; versions 1 to 5 are everyday polls; release parameters fixed in the stub; the running opening's version fixed by the pinned tally-opening version, never pinned) and on the author panel. The stub zeroes both transcript hashes. Well-formedness `results`, `civic-sealed` and `author-panel`, after version 5's rules. Notes on the partition and pairing, civic elections and the panel checks before any share exists; the chosen-bytes residual of author keys (T-17), closed by part 3's default-seat check.
- [[trustee-identity]], Author panel identifier: `H(DS("abolish/v1/author-panel", u8(k_A) ‖ u8(n_A) ‖ Q_1 ‖ … ‖ Q_{n_A}))`, never stored; validity rules `size`, `threshold` (`1 ≤ k_A ≤ n_A`), `key`, `duplicate-key` and, against the default panel, `default-seat`.
- [[verifier]]: Pinned trust roots gain each panel's kind (`independent` or `transitional`) and per-seat holders; 2.1 reports version 6's rules; 2.3 refuses every version-6 profile until part 2's ballot version exists (and a live one until P2-15's tally-opening version); 2.5 adds `panel-kind` and `default-seat`; the Definition procedure says how a release that reads version 6 reports.
- [[parameters]] (`MIN_LIVE_BATCH = 20`; `MAX_TRUSTEES` bounds `n_A`), [[domain-separation]] (`abolish/v1/author-panel`), [[versioning]]'s registry, the vectors README, T-14, T-16, T-17, T-37 and T-54 history. `packages/crypto`: the tag in `TAG_REGISTRY` and `MIN_LIVE_BATCH` in `PARAMETERS`, which tests tie to the spec; `circuit.json` gains the new tag's field element.
- `docs/spec/vectors/election-definition-v6.json` (34 definition vectors, 13 author panels) from `packages/crypto/scripts/definition-v6-vectors.py`, written from the spec with the standard library, reusing the version-5 generator; its encoder is checked against version 5's.

## Tested (part 1a)

- `packages/crypto/tests/definition-v6-vectors.test.ts` decodes every vector with codecs built from the file's own type descriptors beside version 5's (so mislabelled versions read the other layout), re-encodes it, recomputes the definition hash, the stub hash with both transcript hashes zeroed and the author panel identifier, checks each decode error and that the file labels every version-6 rule; and reads each author panel's validity and identifier from [[trustee-identity]] directly with `@noble/curves`, checking it never equals a default panel's identifier over the same keys.
- Crypto suite passes; `vp check` and `vp run -r check` clean.

## Known-weak (part 1a)

- No production codec or rule check reads version 6 yet (part 1b); until then its labels are checked only by the generator's rules.
- No version-6 election can be registered until part 2 specifies the ballot version with both halves (2.3).
- Up to 16 author keys are chosen bytes nothing on chain checks until part 3 has default seats refuse a registration without the author panel's approved transcript on the board (T-17).
- The author trustees' display names have no display-text field yet (part 7).
- The election registry doesn't yet refuse a civic-record definition under a transitional panel (part 5), and nothing reads the panel kind until releases pin it (parts 4 and 6).
