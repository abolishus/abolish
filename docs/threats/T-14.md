# T-14: Trustee collusion

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Needs decision** (P1-4, P1-7). Residual: k colluding trustees break secrecy for every ballot of that election. Automated trustee nodes add one more path: a malicious node release reaches every share at once (T-61, [[0006-trustees]] rule 6). That holds for as long as they, or anyone who seizes them, keep their keys and stored shares; destruction after the tally is required but can't be proven, and posted shares are permanent. k colluding trustees can also publish a ballot's shares, which anyone can check against the public VSS commitments, making that ballot permanently and verifiably public (as with a published decryption under ElGamal).

## Threat

k or more trustees combine shares and decrypt individual ballots.

## Mitigation

k-of-n threshold with independent named trustees (P1-7, owner decision; [[0006-trustees]] proposes 4-of-7 with at most k − 1 trustees per jurisdiction and per hosting provider, and at most one seat for us), Each voter splits the ballot's opening k-of-n among the trustees with Pedersen VSS, so no one ever holds a key that opens ballots, and no DKG is needed for plurality and approval (P1-12, P1-14, [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]). Honest trustees only ever publish summed shares for cells of the partition registered in the election definition, which open aggregates (P1-15, [[0003-tally-scheme]]); per-election keys and stored shares are destroyed after the tally.
