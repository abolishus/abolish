# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result. Regional cells (election-definition version 3, [[0019-regional-cells]]) increase this exposure, to be bounded by the `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` floor on every cell of a split tier, which counts registrations, not people: specified, with ballot version 2 naming its cell, and enforced once the verifier, trustee nodes and the ballot client check it. The verifier reads version 3 since P1-15 part 4b-2b and checks the stated cells against the fold of the stated region counts (`cells`); recomputing the counts and cell trees from the board (2.4, 2.8) is P1-19's.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires. The tally partition is fixed in the election definition registered before voting opens and is the tiers only until P1-15 specifies regional cells, whose region must then come from a membership proof, never a voter-chosen, grindable or per-ballot field, with a protocol-wide floor on each cell's admitted electorate ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision; verifier 2.8). Trustee nodes and the ballot client check the partition before releasing a share or casting. Openings are checked per registered cell (7.4), small tiers are flagged (8.2) and the result pages and API flag them too (P3-6). Tiers are never withheld or merged; every tier is opened and flagged. Regions below the floor are folded into their tier's remainder cell, decided from the registered electorate before voting opens ([[0006-trustees]], Small cells).

- 2026-10-16 (P3-2 part 4d-1, [[registration]]): the queue holds each commitment for the group it was first queued for, set atomically with `HSETNX` in one script, so a request resending a queued commitment for another region changes nothing; tested. Only region 0 is served until the group registry records regions.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-5): mitigation updated for the registered partition (tiers only until P1-15 specifies proven regional cells with a size floor), checked before any share is released or ballot cast, per-cell openings (verifier 2.8, 7.4) and per-tier flags (8.2). This closes two paths `crypto-review` found in a looser draft: a per-ballot partition that opened every ballot, and a voter-ground regional cell that made a vote-sale receipt.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): [[tally]] opens one aggregate per cell and derives coarser totals by adding cells' counts, never by a second opening, and states that an opening over one ballot reveals it. Only the opening is published ([[0015-tally-publication]]): per-trustee summed shares on the board would, with posted shares, let a future adversary isolate sub-aggregates down to one ballot (T-15).
- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): verifier 8.2 also flags a cell whose counted ballots with passing postings at fewer than `k` distinct seats are few, since a future adversary reads the rest and the opening gives their sum.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 2b follow-up, [[tally]], Summed-share message): `packages/crypto` seals and opens summed shares only for cells of the definition's registered partition, so a node bug or a coordinator asking an honest trustee to sum over a subset of a counted set as another cell can't make it disclose a difference of sums that isolates a ballot. Checking that the set summed is the anchored counted set, once per cell, stays the node's.
- 2026-10-12 (owner, [[0006-trustees]]): small cells: regions below the floor merge into a per-tier remainder cell; tiers are never merged or withheld.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4a, [[0019-regional-cells]]): regional cells come from registered region groups, folded by the owner's rule at a floor of `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` = 1,000 registered members, each proven against one cell tree, so a ballot's region is fixed at registration and the remainder cell hides which small region a ballot came from. Not mitigated: the floor counts registrations, so at Tier 0 (and Tier 1 up to its collusion bound) padding a target's region with known-vote identities still isolates the target; two elections whose remainders differ by one region can difference it; and a creator can list one small region as an unsplit tier.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b, part 1): [[election-definition]] version 3 and [[membership]] (Cell tree) specify regional cells: per tier one root block, its groups each in one region, the stated region counts and the cells the fold derives from them (the `cells` rule), one cell tree per cell with each commitment counted once per tier (first occurrence), and `MIN_CELL_ELECTORATE` = 1,000 in [[parameters]]. `packages/crypto` decodes and checks version 3 and recomputes region counts and cells from the groups' leaves; vectors from an independent implementation. At most 256 cells per definition (the tally opening's `u8` cell). No ballot names a cell yet, so 2.3 refuses version 3 with any ballot version until P1-12 adds one; the verifier and clients don't read version 3 yet (part 2).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-2 part 4a, [[0024-tier0-registration]]): the planned self-paid Tier 0 path (P3-2 part 5) would let anyone, not only registrars breaking the no-repeat rule, move a voter to another regional cell by copying their commitment into a lower-numbered self-paid group. Part 5 must first change [[membership]]'s first-occurrence rule so that signed groups win over self-paid ones, and among self-paid groups the earliest addition in chain order wins. Residuals: a self-paid copy landing before a self-paid registration (front-running) moves that registrant; and since a signed copy always wins, the registrars or a compromised signing service (T-06, T-69) can move any self-paid registrant, even with a later copy. An honest service checks self-paid groups against the latest chain state when a request arrives and again before signing, so it never signs such a copy. That second check has its own residual: a party that sees a B request before its batch is signed (Cloudflare, Railway's logs, a compelled deploy; T-11, T-69) can self-add the queued commitment into a self-paid group of its chosen region, the check then drops it from the batch, and the registrant lands only in the copier's cell. The client detects it by checking where its commitment landed; part 4d decides whether to sign anyway a commitment copied after its request was accepted.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b-2a): [[ballot-validity]] (Ballot record (version 2)) specifies the ballot that names its cell: the cell's position in the partition where version 1 names a tier, bound by the statement and ballot hashes; the verifier takes the cell's tier, root and scope from the definition (`ballot-cell` refuses any other cell), so no voter chooses a cell by choosing a root, and a commitment repeated in another cell's group can't prove from its zeroed leaf. Definition versions 3 and 4 pair with ballot version 2 only (2.3). `packages/crypto` decodes and admits version 2; vectors from an independent implementation with snarkjs proofs against cell trees. The verifier doesn't read version 3 yet (part 4b-2b).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15 part 4b-2b): `packages/verifier` reads definition versions 3 and 4: each decodes and is checked under its own layout and rules (`cells` and `receipt-keys` reported on 2.1), its hash and the stub hash the key ceremony binds are taken over its own encoding, and 2.3 runs over it. The per-cell recomputation from the board's group additions (2.4, 2.8) and the ballot checks against each cell's root (5.1 to 5.3) are still unverifiable, with the same checks for version 1 (P1-19), so the floor is checked only against the stated counts until then.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-16 part 1a, [[election-definition]] Layout (version 6)): a live poll's schedule (`live_every`) and minimum batch (`live_batch`, at least `MIN_LIVE_BATCH` = 20) are fixed in the stub before opening, and civic referendums are always sealed (`results`, `civic-sealed`); the running set and batch rule are P2-15's. Padding a batch at Tier 0 stays unmitigated ([[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-13 (P3-2 part 4b, [[registration]]): the signing service queues a commitment for at most one Tier 0 group at a time and forms each boundary's batches one group after another against that queue and every in-flight batch, so a party that sees a registration request can't resend it for another region and have the service sign it into two groups, which would move the registrant by the first-occurrence rule.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-17 part 1, [[membership]], Rolling tier tree): a rolling tier's member count is its distinct live commitments, so a repeated commitment never inflates a reported anonymity set; a rolling tier is one cell, with no floor, and 8.2 flags it when small. Live-results padding can now use identities registered during the poll (P2-17 part 2 reports the counts).
- 2026-10-13 (P1-19 part 4i): the verifier admits version-2 ballots under definition versions 3 and 4 against the root and scope of the cell each names, read from the definition and never from the ballot, so a proof made in another cell, of the same tier or another, or from a leaf first occurrence zeroed, is rejected (`membership-proof`), and a cell past the partition is `ballot-cell` (verifier 5.1 to 5.3). Recomputing the cells' trees from the board (2.4, 2.8) is still P1-19's.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-19 part 4j): the verifier sorts tally openings under definition versions 3 and 4 into the cells the definition states, numbered across the tiers as ballots name them, so an opening is reported against a cell, never a tier, and one naming a cell past the partition is stray (verifier 7.4). The partition is taken as stated until 2.8 recomputes it.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-14 part 4, `packages/crypto`): a multi-question poll's results are each question's slice of a cell's opening (`questionResults`) and coarser totals sum them per question and option; nothing in the tally relates two questions, so no joint figure across questions is computed or published ([[0026-multi-question-ballots]], No cross-tabulation). A small cell still reveals every answer of its voters at once (the ADR's Known-weak).
- 2026-10-14 (P2-17 part 2b-1, [[membership]], Reported counts; [[verifier]] 8.1, 8.2; [[tally]], Rolling tiers): a rolling tier reports no single size but its member counts at opening, at close (the last boundary's, whether or not accepted, so removals show) and at the smallest step any admitted ballot named, which 8.2 flags with the close count by the small-count rule; each live release carries the count at its newest step, which the verifier recomputes. Padding during the poll is still Not mitigated; the counts make the electorate it pads visible.
- 2026-10-14 (P2-15 part 1a, [[tally]], Live results): the running set, the schedule and the release choice are specified. Running sets only grow: a nullifier enters with its first fully receipted ballot and keeps it whatever follows, because under [[0027-trustee-panels-and-live-results]]'s rule, where re-voters and posted ballots leave, one voter leaving as 19 enter, or three releases, disclose a single ballot, and one seat's trustee, which can post any ballot's share, could force such departures. The spec proves that the smallest non-empty side of any combination of totals is the least positive directed cut of a per-cell graph, so with grow-only sets the pairwise batch bounds every combination of releases (checked by search in the vectors); the lag counts entries. Not mitigated: the final tally against the releases has every released re-voter's, tie's or excluded voter's released ballot on one side, so a cell with one such voter shows that ballot at a `−1` (about 11% at `B = 20` over ten 10% options; the verifier computes the exact cut and flags it, never withholds); a zero count in a side of `B` discloses that no one in it chose that option (`(1 − p)^B`); padding at Tier 0; an operator withholding ballots or one seat's receipts to quiet an interval, which [[P2-13]]'s withholding check must close; until then live polls run only with no author panel and where we hold at least `k` default seats, enforced by [[verifier]] 2.3 (a single compromised board host can still quiet an interval there); and one seat's trustee keeping ballots out of running sets by not receipting them.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-15 part 1b, [[tally]], Running opening record): a release is published as running openings (record type `0x0018`), one per cell, each over the cell's running set at a snapshot the release choice chose and naming it. The operator boards one only if it is a chosen release's, passes against that release's aggregate and is attributed to a registered seat, so a passing opening over any other set (another snapshot, or two sets one ballot apart) never reaches the board from an honest operator, and a signed one is evidence against its signer, since an honest seat sums only chosen releases. Mitigated as far as the batch rule reaches; an operator and `k` seats together can still publish anything (A-2 with A-3 at threshold).
- 2026-10-16 (P2-16 part 3b, [[tally]], Residuals of author panels): with an author panel, a future adversary's anonymity set is set per panel, since each panel's sum is public; 8.2 reports and flags per panel the counted ballots whose half isn't readable from postings.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-15 part 2a, `packages/crypto` `running-opening.ts`): the running opening and its boarding rule are implemented. `boardRunningOpenings` refuses an opening outside the schedule (an unchosen snapshot among them), one over another aggregate, a failing or unattributed one and a second per seat, release and cell, so a passing opening over a set the release choice never chose never reaches the board through an honest operator; checked against `running-opening.json` from an independent implementation.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-15 part 2b, `packages/crypto` `live-release.ts`): the schedule, running sets, change counts, the release graph's least positive directed cut (exact, by maximum flow) and the release choice with its lag are implemented, and `liveReleases` builds the schedule that reading and boarding running openings take from the fixed snapshots' running sets, so the chosen releases never come from a caller's list. Board-entry version 6 adds the running opening to version 5's types, and 2.3 refuses it under every sealed definition, so a sealed poll's board never carries one. Checked against `live-release.json` from an independent implementation, and by fast-check: every pair of chosen releases differs by at least `B` in every cell and their least directed cut is at least `B`; the cut equals the least over every vertex set and, on small graphs, the smallest non-empty side of any combination.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-15 part 3a, `packages/verifier` `releases.ts`): the verifier's side of releases is implemented as a library, checked against `live-verifier.json` from an independent implementation: each snapshot's L1 head from the run of finalized headers (`l1-blocks-short` where the run doesn't fix it), how each ballot copy stands at a snapshot (a pending or rejected copy counting towards the one-ballot rule, a broken copy ahead of a voter's real one changing nothing), the running sets and the release choice from the board alone, an unscheduled opening that opens its snapshot's running set reported as a disclosure, and 8.2's `live-final-batch` by the closed form, equal to the least positive directed cut by property test. Not wired yet: the verifier reads no definition version 6 or 7 until [[P2-16]] part 4, so 7.5 and 7.6 stay `not-implemented` and 2.3 still fails every live definition (part 3b).
- 2026-10-17 (P2-16 part 3c-2, `packages/crypto` `tally-opening.ts`): tally-opening version 3 is implemented, checked against `panel-tally.json`: each cell's count is the sum of its two panels' passing partial openings, range-checked only as a sum, and no partial opening is masked (agent, owner may veto; [[tally]], Residuals of author panels).
