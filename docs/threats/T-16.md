# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result and every regional cell. Regional cells, which [[0002-everlasting-privacy]] allows where results are regional, increase this exposure.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires, and per regional cell only where results are regional. The tally partition is fixed in the election definition registered before voting opens, so no one can choose a finer or targeted split after seeing the counted set ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision); the verifier checks it (2.8), checks openings per registered cell (7.4) and flags small counts per cell (8.2), and the result pages and API flag them too (P3-6). Whether a cell below the minimum may be withheld or merged is the owner's question in [[0006-trustees]]; until then every cell is opened and flagged.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].
