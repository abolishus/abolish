## What was decided (part 1)

[[0014-tier1-vouching]], `Accepted (agent) — owner may veto`. It picks option C:

- **Who vouches:** Tier 2 members, anonymously.
- **Each vouch** is two proofs under Semaphore v4's unchanged circuit and keys:
  - one with a per-vouchee scope, so the vouchers are distinct;
  - one with a per-slot scope, so each voucher has a lifetime budget.

  Each proof's message binds the vouchee and the other proof's nullifier.

- **Admission:** a commitment with vouches from `k = 3` distinct members, each member having `L = 12` vouches, ever.

It replaces the Safe's signature for Tier 1 additions, so the verifier checks the rule from the board.

The rejected options:

- an operator-held graph: unverifiable, and it stores the graph;
- a public signed web of trust: it publishes the graph;
- a transitive variant: fake identities could vouch for more, without bound.

Threat files T-02, T-06, T-22 and T-27 are updated.

## What was built (part 2b-2)

- `countTier1Additions` (`packages/crypto/src/vouch-count.ts`): every Tier 1 addition of a group registry decided in one replay over the registry's events in log order. Each Tier 2 group is replayed strictly first (`applyGroupRecords` under the pinned registrars); the earliest event breaking a Tier 2 history (a record the replay refuses, a members record naming a group with no earlier `GroupCreated`, or a repeated `GroupCreated`, each judged from earlier events alone) stops the count at its block, leaving every addition at or after it undecided. Then the checks in the spec's order, each over the vouches in record order: checks 1 to 6 of one addition, `group`, `root-grid`, `root-window`, `tier2-group`, `root-mismatch`, `slot-spent`, `member`, `proof`. Each Tier 2 group a vouch names is replayed once with an incremental tier tree (`TierTreeBuilder`, internal), its root taken at every block named. Counted additions append a leaf and consume their slot nullifiers; refused ones consume nothing. `tier1Leaves` gives a group's leaves at a block, or `group-history` at or past the break.
- `verifyDecodedMembershipProofs` (`membership-proof.ts`): the small-exponent batch test over strictly decoded proofs, coefficients uniform in `[1, 2^128)` from the CSPRNG and never supplied by a caller, `N + 3` pairings for `N` proofs, in batches of at most 64; a failed batch is split in halves, each batched again, and parts below 33 that fail are checked proof by proof, so each verdict is the individual one. Every proof of a replay is checked this way before the walk, for additions that pass the checks needing no counting state.
- `checkTier1Addition` now runs on `precheckTier1Addition` (checks 1 to 6 and the `2k` proof checks), which the counting shares.
- Measured costs in [[vouching]], Batched verification: batching cuts honest proof checks to about a sixth (about 8 ms a proof); one invalid proof in every 64 leaves about 85% of the one-at-a-time cost, and all junk about 1.35 times it.
- [[vouching]]: the two new break triggers, the costs, the vectors and `countTier1Additions`; Known-weak gains the held vouch that links two vouchees (#288's non-blocking finding, stated rather than closed). Threat files T-22, T-52 and T-71.

## Known-weak

- **Tier 1 rests on Tier 2.** No real person is admitted before Tier 2 admits real documents ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]'s owner questions).
- **Collusion is bounded, not prevented:** at most 4 fake identities per colluding document.
- **Double admission:** a person with two disjoint groups of three vouchers gets two identities.
- **No accountability:** anonymous vouchers can't be held to account.
- **Metadata:** timing and network metadata around an addition remain (T-11, T-22).
- **No addition counts yet:** the counting rule has code and vectors (part 2b-2) and the registry's call is specified (part 3a), but the call is built only in part 3b, every vouch names a Tier 2 group, which waits for P3-4, and no verifier calls the rule until [[verifier]] 2.4 reads Tier 1 groups.
- **Junk Tier 1 additions stay loggable** (part 3a): additions naming made-up Tier 2 roots pass the registry's checks without bound, at about 1.3 million gas each (T-52).
- **No Tier 1 removals** (part 3a): a lost or stolen Tier 1 secret stays a member for the registry's life (T-69).
- **A held vouch can link two vouchees** (part 2b-2): a vouch held past its window frees its slot in the voucher's client, and if the slot is spent again, the stale vouch shows the same slot nullifier beside another vouchee (T-22). Stated, not closed; since part 3a the registry refuses to log it, but its vouchee can still publish it elsewhere.
- **Batching relieves only honest load** (part 2b-2): junk additions that reach `proof`, about one in ten of a replay's, take most of its gain away, and a flood of junk costs about 1.35 times checking it one at a time (T-52).
- **The grid and window follow L2 block time** (part 2b-1): sized for Base's 2-second blocks; a shorter block time shrinks them in seconds.
- **Replay cost** (part 2b-1): rebuilding any Tier 1 group replays every Tier 1 addition of the registry up to its root block, junk groups included (T-52).
- **Unchecked prior art:** the prior-art facts cited in the ADR weren't re-checked in this session. Part 2 re-checks any it relies on.
