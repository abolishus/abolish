# T-33: Re-vote resolution manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Specified** (P1-13, [[0011-revote-rule]]): the rule of [[revoting]] meets R1 to R5 of [[verifier]] check 6.1, and R6 once P1-18's close rule accounts for its wait; `packages/crypto` implements its admission check on the L1 block (`checkL1Reference`) and its selection (`selectBallots`). Reading finalized L1 is P1-19's.

## Threat

The server or a verifier picks a ballot other than the voter's last one.

## Mitigation

Re-vote resolution is a deterministic rule that meets the requirements in [[verifier]] check 6.1, including independence from the order of unanchored entries, specified in `docs/spec/` and implemented once in `packages/crypto` or `packages/verifier` (P1-8, P1-13). Depends on T-38 holding.

The rule ([[revoting]]): each ballot's membership proof binds a recent L1 block by number and hash; for each tier and nullifier, the admitted ballot naming the highest block counts, and two distinct ballots naming that block count nothing. No board position, segment or arrival time enters it, so the operator can't choose the counted ballot by reordering or withholding (R3).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-13): the rule is specified ([[revoting]], [[0011-revote-rule]]) and its selection implemented in `packages/crypto`.
- 2026-10-14 (P1-19 part 4n): the verifier decides 6.1 by `selectBallots` over the ballots every admission check admitted from the counted extent, and only once that set is fixed: every admission row settled (5.8 included, so format 5 only), the copy authenticated by 4.1 and 4.2, the seal's window closed, no ordinary ballot whose `l2_timestamp` 4.4 hasn't authenticated, and every admitted ballot at or below `s_F`, the floor of `s_S`. Short of any of these, 6.1 is unverifiable, so no withheld, late or re-timed entry can make a pass over a set that later changes (R3, R4). The report lists `selected` and `ties`. Residual: until 4.4 replays the accumulators and `s_S` is known, a board with ordinary ballots, or with an admitted one after `s_F`, never decides 6.1.
