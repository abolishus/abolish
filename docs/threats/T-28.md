# T-28: Invalid ballots

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-9
- Status: **Planned** (P1-4 accepted by the agent, owner may veto: homomorphic tally with validity proofs for plurality and approval, specified in [[ballot-validity]] and implemented in `packages/crypto` by P1-12 part 1; the ballot record that carries it, with 5.4 among its admission checks, by part 4 (`checkBallot`); the verifier's use of it is P1-19's). For the ranked-choice mixnet, validity is checked on decrypted ballots instead, and the threat changes shape.

## Threat

A ballot encrypts an over-vote, a negative count or a value outside the allowed options, corrupting a homomorphic tally.

## Mitigation

Disjunctive Chaum–Pedersen (CDS) validity proofs over the Pedersen commitments, per option and for the sum (P1-4, P1-12, [[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); trustees also check each share against the public VSS commitments, and a proven complaint excludes a ballot with an invalid share; the verifier rejects ballots without valid proofs.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-4): status moved from Needs decision to Planned: [[0003-tally-scheme]] is accepted under rule A.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-12, part 1): [[ballot-validity]] specifies the per-option proofs (each commitment opens to 0 or 1) and the sum proof (the sum lies from `min_selections` to `max_selections`), as CDS disjunctions of Schnorr proofs over the Pedersen commitments, and the trustees' share check against the VSS commitments. `packages/crypto` implements both; vectors from an independent implementation include a vote of 2, a vote of −1 that would cancel another option, an over-vote, a ballot over its approval limit and one under its minimum, each rejected.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-12 part 4): the ballot record (`0x0002`, version 1) carries the committed vote, and its admission checks run the validity proofs after the record's definition, tier, shape, unqualified seats and membership proof ([[ballot-validity]], Ballot record); a ballot failing any of them is rejected at admission, never counted.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-14 part 1a, [[election-definition]], Layout (version 5)): a multi-question poll's definition gives each question its own limits, `min_selections ≤ max_selections ≤ m_q` with `max_selections ≥ 1`, and one option budget of `MAX_OPTIONS` for the whole poll, checked as well-formedness before anything is cast. The ballot's per-question range proofs that enforce them are part 1b's ([[ballot-validity]], ballot version 3).
