# T-07: Setup compromise of a proving system

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-1
- Status: **Accepted.** Sound if any one participant in each ceremony was honest.

## Threat

A cheating participant in Semaphore's Groth16 ceremony could forge Semaphore proofs; a compromised universal KZG SRS (used by bb's UltraHonk for our Noir circuits) could forge Noir proofs.

## Mitigation

Semaphore's multi-party ceremony is the one accepted exception to "no per-circuit trusted setup" (brief, Chain). Our Noir circuits need no per-circuit setup, but they rest on the universal SRS bb uses, itself from a multi-party ceremony. Membership and nullifiers are verified with Semaphore v4's Groth16 keys from its ceremony ([[0009-membership-proof]]): a forged membership proof needs every ceremony participant to have cheated. Privacy doesn't rest on the ceremony: P1-17b checks each pinned proving key's well-formedness once, publicly, and the prover uses exactly that key (P2-8, P5-4).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): settled: Semaphore's Groth16 ceremony for membership ([[0009-membership-proof]]); bb's universal SRS applies only to later custom Noir circuits.
