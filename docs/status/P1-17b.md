## Scope

From [[0009-membership-proof]] and [[membership]]:

- Vendor Semaphore v4's depth-32 Groth16 verification key (the only depth Abolish uses, [[membership]], Tree depth) from `semaphore-protocol/semaphore` at the tag and commit [[membership]] cites, with source, commit and sha256 re-fetched in CI. Allowing that publisher needs a `ci:` PR first (rule B).
- Specify `abolish/v1/membership-vk`: the hash of the pinned key set that the election definition's `membership_vk_hash` carries (P1-10b), and the proof encoding a ballot carries.
- Check the pinned depth-32 proving key's reference string for well-formedness once, publicly (Fuchsbauer, PKC 2018), and bind the checked key into `abolish/v1/membership-vk`: without it, zero knowledge against a subverted ceremony isn't established ([[membership]], Security notes).
- Groth16 verification over BN254 in the gated packages, on `@noble/curves`' BN254 pairing: the published verification equation, no new primitive ([[0009-membership-proof]], Consequences). Strict decoding of proof points (canonical coordinates, on the curve, in the prime-order subgroup, G2 included); every public input below `r`; the key's `IC` exactly 5 points; only pinned keys.
- The tier tree ([[membership]], Tier tree): rebuild a tier's root from its additions, as verifier 2.4 needs. It needs circomlib's `Poseidon2` over BN254, from `@noble/curves`' Poseidon with circomlib's published parameters vendored and checked against circomlib's own test vectors.
- Real Semaphore proofs as vectors (generated with Semaphore's own tooling outside the gated packages, recorded with their source), plus tampered proofs, wrong public signals, a proof under another depth's key, wrong scope (including another tier's scope over the same root) a proof against root 0 (proof length above the depth), a hidden-subtree proof (a registered value that is the root of a private tree, proven with a path longer than its leaf's), and removals by commitment, all rejected; and a tier tree with a forbidden addition (`0`, `1`, `Poseidon2(0, 1)`, non-canonical) whose leaf is 0 and whose definition passes, and a tier whose only additions are forbidden (root `Z_32`), whose definition fails; and removals of an out-of-range or already-removed index, which change nothing. The real proofs' scope and message inputs, made by Semaphore's own `hash`, also tie `F` in `membership.json` to Semaphore's output, which P1-17 checked only against Keccak known answers.

## Notes

- Groth16 proofs are malleable; replay detection never compares proof bytes ([[verifier]] 5.7, 6.1 R1).

## Part 1 (#111)

Built:

- [[membership]], Verification key: Semaphore's depth-32 key (vendored `verification-keys.json`, entry 31) and its proving key `semaphore-32.zkey` by sha256; `membership_vk_hash = H(DS("abolish/v1/membership-vk", u8(32) ‖ vk ‖ zkey_sha256))`, so the hash binds the proving key the reference-string check (part 2) covers.
- [[membership]], Proof encoding: `membership_proof`, 256 bytes, A ‖ B ‖ C in EIP-196/197's encoding (Semaphore's packed order), with every point canonical, on its curve, in the order-`r` subgroup and not the identity. The ballot record (P1-12) can now carry it.
- [[membership]], Verification: Groth16's equation on `@noble/curves`' BN254 pairing; signals never reduced; root 0 refused before pairing.
- `Poseidon2`: `@noble/curves`' permutation with constants from its Grain generator, equal to circomlibjs's published constants (t = 3); no constant table in the package.
- The tier tree: root, paths and the zero chain, and `replayTierGroup`, the lenient replay of Commitments and removals. The group registry's groups replay strictly through `applyGroupRecords`, whose leaves `tierTreeRoot` takes.
- `abolish/v1/membership-vk` is specified ([[domain-separation]]); `circuit.json` already listed its field element.

Tested:

- Vendored and re-fetched in CI: `semaphore/verification-keys.json` (v4.14.3), circomlibjs `poseidon_constants.json` and `test/poseidon.js` (v0.1.7), circomlib `test/poseidoncircuit.js` (v2.0.5). - `docs/spec/vectors/membership-proof.json`, made outside the workspace by `scripts/membership-proof-vectors.mjs` with Semaphore 4.14.3's `generateProof` (and snarkjs's `fullProve` for witnesses `generateProof` refuses), every verdict from snarkjs 0.7.5: two honest proofs over tier trees (one sparse, after zero leaves), a re-randomised proof (valid, other bytes), tampered A and C, A and C swapped, a wrong nullifier, message, root and scope (tier 1's scope over tier 0's root), swapped signals, signals plus `r`, a proof under the depth-1 key, a root-0 proof (which snarkjs accepts and Abolish rejects) and a hidden-subtree proof; 12 rejected encodings (lengths, coordinates at or above `p`, off-curve, identity, B's component order, B outside the subgroup). - fast-check: any changed byte of an honest proof or any changed signal is rejected; random 256-byte strings are rejected or re-encode to themselves; paths climb to the root and other leaves' don't; the root changes with any leaf and not with trailing empty leaves; removals and stray removals.
- Mutations: removing the root-0 check or the signal-range check fails the tests.
- `vp check` clean; `packages/crypto` (1700+ tests) and `packages/verifier` pass; CI's `gated` and `vectors-provenance` steps pass locally.

Known-weak:

## Part 2

The public check of `semaphore-32.zkey`'s reference string ([[membership]], Reference-string check; T-07, T-12).

Built:

- `packages/crypto/scripts/membership-crs-check.ts`: parses snarkjs's zkey and ptau and iden3's R1CS strictly (sections cover the file exactly, canonical Montgomery coordinates, every point on its curve and in its order-`r` group) and checks that the key is exactly an honest Groth16 key for the R1CS: 18 checks covering the circuit's A and B matrices, the powers of tau as powers of one `τ` (so the ptau isn't trusted), `α`, `β`, `γ`, `δ` nonzero and consistent across groups, `τ^N ≠ 1`, and the A, B (both groups), IC, L and H points against their formulas. Each family is one random linear combination with CSPRNG scalars; every group, MSM and pairing operation is `@noble/curves`'. H is tested up to the one direction a proof never uses (the ptau has no `τ^{2N−1}`).
- Inputs pinned by sha256: the zkey (`MEMBERSHIP_ZKEY_SHA256`), `ppot_0080_14.ptau` from the PSE Perpetual Powers of Tau bucket, and `semaphore-32.r1cs`, compiled by circom 2.1.9 `--O2` from the pinned `semaphore.circom` with Semaphore's npm-pinned `circomlib` 2.0.5 and `binary-merkle-root.circom` 2.0.0.
- [[membership]] gains Reference-string check (inputs, recipe, the 18 checks, the argument and the result); Security notes now state zero knowledge holds against a subverted ceremony. [[T-07]] and [[T-12]] record it.

Result (2026-10-12): well formed; all 18 checks pass in about 4 minutes. snarkjs 0.7.5's `zkey verify` on the same three files independently reports `ZKey Ok!` (320 phase-2 contributions and a beacon).

Tested:

- An honest key for a toy circuit (`y = x³`), built from a known trapdoor with Lagrange polynomials in closed form, passes; 23 single breakages (a matrix coefficient, a power in either group, `τ = 0`, `τ` on the domain, a zero `γ` or `δ`, mismatched `β` or `δ`, one point in A, B1, B2, IC, L or H, a wrong `γ`, another C matrix through a private and a public signal, a key for another `τ`) each fail exactly the check that names them, so every check is load-bearing. H tolerates exactly `κ_i·Z` (fast-check: any other error fails).
- Parsers: the toy key, powers and R1CS round-trip through encoders written from the format descriptions; wrong magic, trailing bytes, truncation, a coordinate at or above `p`, an off-curve point, a G2 point outside the subgroup, another field, a repeated section, too few powers and a non-canonical R1CS coefficient are rejected.
- The root of unity is ffjavascript's `Fr.w[28]`; the inverse FFT matches evaluation (fast-check).
- The real check runs as a test when `MEMBERSHIP_CRS_DIR` names a directory with the three files (run locally; passes).

Known-weak:

- CI doesn't rerun the real check: its inputs (about 25 MB) aren't in the repository and their hosts aren't allowed publishers. Anyone can rerun it from the recipe; CI runs the toy tests.
- The R1CS is recompiled, not published by Semaphore; that it is the ceremony's circuit rests on the key's A and B matrices matching it exactly (check 2 and 3) and on snarkjs's `zkey verify`, which rebuilds the initial key from it.
- The check covers the key, not the prover: P2-8 and P5-4 still must use exactly this key (by sha256) and CSPRNG randomisers.
