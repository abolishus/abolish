# T-02: Sybil rings at Tier 1

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-1, A-9
- Status: **Planned**, weak by design ([[0014-tier1-vouching]]): a ring of `c ≥ 3` colluding Tier 2 members can admit at most `4c` fake Tier 1 identities, plus a third of the vouches deception extracts from honest members. A person with two disjoint groups of three vouchers gets two identities, a Tier 2 member can vouch for their own Tier 1 commitment, slots can be lent between colluders, and vouchers can't be held to account.

## Threat

Colluding vouchers vouch for fake identities.

## Mitigation

Vouching rules and limits (P3-3). Reported per tier.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P3-3): [[0014-tier1-vouching]]: only Tier 2 (document-verified) members vouch, and only anonymously, with two Semaphore proofs per vouch. A Tier 1 addition needs vouches from 3 distinct Tier 2 members using 3 distinct, unspent slots. Each Tier 2 secret has 12 slots across the deployment, ever, and the slots aren't scoped to a group, so a second Tier 1 group gives no fresh budget. So collusion is bounded at 4 identities per colluding document, ever (`(c·L + d)/k`, where `d` is the vouches obtained by deception), and every Tier 2 residual (T-03) carries over, multiplied by up to 4. A transitive web of trust was rejected, because fake identities could vouch for more. Not mitigated: double admission through two disjoint groups of vouchers, self-vouching, slot lending, and voucher accountability (the vouchers are anonymous).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-3 part 2a, #144): [[vouching]] fixes one addition: exactly 3 vouches with pairwise-distinct vouchee nullifiers (distinct Tier 2 secrets) and pairwise-distinct slot nullifiers (one slot funds one vouch), each slot below 12, both proofs verified under scopes and messages the verifier recomputes. Each proof binds the vouchee, the group, the Tier 2 root it names and the other proof's nullifier, so no vouch moves to another vouchee or group. Pooling one member's vouchee proof with another's slot proof stays possible, as the ADR's bound assumes. Slot consumption across additions, and which roots count, are part 2b's; until then nothing counts toward Tier 1.
- 2026-10-16 (P3-3 part 2b-1, [[vouching]], Counting across the deployment): the bound is now checkable across the deployment: an addition counts only if none of its slot nullifiers was used by an earlier counted vouch in any Tier 1 group of the registry, in one total log order, and a counted addition consumes its slots for good, while a failing one consumes nothing. Each vouch's Tier 2 root must be the verifier's own rebuild of a tier-2 group of the same registry at a block on a 300-block grid within 302,400 blocks of the addition.
