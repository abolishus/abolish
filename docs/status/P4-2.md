# P4-2: Direct submit end to end

- State: in progress (part 1, the ballot client's direct submission to the board contract in `packages/sdk`, merged in #297; part 2, the forced path through the L1 relay, next)
- Branch: `claude/p4-2`
- Phase: Phase 4: Censorship resistance

## Scope

Direct-submit path, end to end, with servers refusing.

## Plan

The contracts are P1-18's (the board, its ballot check and the L1 relay); this item is the client side and the end-to-end proof.

1. **The board's ordinary path** (this part): a client in `packages/sdk` that sends a cast ballot record to the board contract on L2 through the voter's own wallet, with the board's refusals decoded, repeats found rather than resent, and the board's event confirmed.
2. **The forced path:** the same ballot through the L1 relay (`relayBallot`) when the sequencer censors the voter, with the gas limit the board's check needs, watching for the deposit's `RelayedBallotSubmitted` on L2; and the relay's price, which [[contracts]] (Board, Known-weak) leaves to this item to measure.
3. **The ballot client's fallback** in `apps/ballot`: when our API refuses or can't be reached, or the operator's receipt fails its checks at cast ([[receipts]], Checks at cast), offer direct submission, telling the voter that the paying account is public (T-25, Residual) and that a direct-submitted ballot still needs its receipts or postings ([[delivery]], Client rules).
4. **The reference election's stage 5:** a ballot submitted directly while the API refuses it, counted from the board's event. Waits on casting in the reference election ([[P1-20]] part 2b), which waits on a membership prover: the board admits only ballots whose Groth16 membership proof verifies.

## Part 1

- `packages/sdk/src/direct-submit.ts`, exported from the package:
  - `directSubmitBallot` takes an EIP-1193 provider, the definition's `l2_chain_id` and `board`, the paying account, the record and the block to search for copies from (the election's registration block). It holds no key, and the record's definition and ballot hashes are `@abolishus/crypto`'s `frameBallot`'s, never the caller's. One call is one attempt: it refuses a wallet on another chain, checks again after sending, and names the chain in the transaction (T-34, T-41); asks the board with `eth_call`, without the account, and returns its refusal without sending (so a voter's gas isn't spent on a revert), and does the same for a refusal at the estimate; looks for a byte-identical copy the board already holds, from either path (`BallotSubmitted`, or a relay deposit's `RelayedBallotSubmitted`), since a repeat is a silent no-op on chain ([[contracts]], Board, Calls, Repeats; Relayed ballots; T-17); and otherwise sends the call with a quarter more gas than the estimate (the membership check reverts unless the pairing's gas is left) and reports where the board's event is: from the board, not removed, under the record's definition and ballot hashes, carrying the record whole, in its own transaction's receipt. A reverted transaction is reported by the board's answer afterwards (the window closed, gas ran short, or another call put the bytes on first).
  - `submitBallotCalldata`: `submitBallot(record)` in the one encoding the board accepts (`CanonicalCalldata`).
  - `decodeBoardBallotRevert`: the board's revert data as the spec's codes, in the contracts' enum order: `malformed` (a decode error and its offset), `refused` (an admission code), `window`, `gas`, `calldata`, and `unknown` for anything else, including a known error with extra bytes or a code past the enum.
  - `revertDataOf`: the revert data wherever a node or wallet puts it in its error; an error without it (a rejected request, a network failure) is passed on.
- [[T-25]]: history.

## Tested (part 1)

- `packages/sdk/tests/direct-submit.test.ts`, against the real board on Anvil (forge's build of `Board` with the Foundry suite's registry stubs, set from `docs/spec/vectors/ballot.json`, Anvil's unlocked accounts signing): every vector ballot gets the board's verdict through the client, and the client sends nothing for each refused one (the account's nonce is unchanged); every decode vector is refused as `malformed` with its code and offset (the file's other-type vector is `unexpected-record-type` on the board, which knows every listed type, contracts.md step 1); five non-canonical encodings of the call are each `calldata`; before the window opens and after it closes a ballot is refused as `window` with the definition's times, sending nothing; an honest ballot in the window lands with its event, which `eth_getLogs` agrees with; the same bytes again are found as `already-on-board`, before and after close, without a transaction; and a ballot forced through the relay (the relay's alias impersonated, calling the board with `relayDepositData`) is found as `already-on-board` on the `relayed` path, without a transaction.
- Against a scripted provider: another chain is refused before anything else is asked; the preflight carries no account, the copy search asks for both events under the record's own hashes from the caller's block, and the transaction carries exactly the canonical calldata, the estimate plus a quarter and the chain; a copy on either path is found without sending; none of a log from another address, with a fourth topic, another topic 0, another definition hash, other data, a longer record, trailing words, another offset, a relayed event with ordinary data or a word past 2⁶⁴, or a removed log, is taken for the record's event; an event under another ballot hash is `event-mismatch`; another transaction's receipt is `bad-response`, and its logs aren't taken; a call that emitted nothing finds the copy put first; `receipt-timeout` after the polls allowed; a refusal at the estimate is the board's, sending nothing; a reverted transaction is reported as the board's refusal afterwards, as the copy another call put first, or as `reverted` when the board accepts the record again; a wallet's rejection passed on unchanged; an acceptance of a record that doesn't frame, and malformed receipts, are `bad-response`. By hand: removing the removed-log check, the address check, the relayed event from the search, the window's 2⁶⁴ bounds or the chain in the transaction each fails a test.
- The calldata's layout as a property (fast-check, `FC_NUM_RUNS`); every code of both enums, the window and the gas errors decoded; codes past the enum, a window time past 2⁶⁴, wrong lengths, extra bytes and other errors `unknown`; decoding never throws on any bytes; revert data found in each place nodes and wallets put it, and not in errors without it, including a cyclic one.

## Independent review (part 1)

A fresh in-session subagent reviewed the diff against `.github/review/crypto-review.md` and confirmed the enum orders, error signatures, calldata, event matching, chain check and gas margin against the contracts.

- **Blocking, fixed:** a ballot forced through the L1 relay is held by the board under `RelayedBallotSubmitted`, which the copy search didn't read, so the client would resend it as a paid no-op and report `event-mismatch`. The search now reads both events, with the relayed data's layout checked, and results name the path.
- **Fixed:** the ballot hash is computed from the record (`frameBallot`), not taken from the caller; a refusal at the estimate is decoded; the transaction names the chain and the chain is checked again after sending; the preflight carries no account, and the RPC's view of the record, account and network address is stated; a refusal is described as the provider's report, to be confirmed through a node the client chose; `fromBlock` is required; the window's times are bounded to 2⁶⁴; the pairing's gas is cited exactly; removed logs and other transactions' receipts and logs aren't taken; the missing tests above are added.

## Known-weak (part 1)

- The paying account is public and linked to the ballot on chain, so funding it can link the voter (T-25, Residual; T-11); the client neither chooses nor checks the account, and the warning to the voter is part 3's.
- What the client reports is what the provider's node says. A wallet's node can lie about a log or a receipt, so `submitted` and `already-on-board` are never grounds to stand down: until a storage proof covers the event, the voter's client keeps resubmitting by every path ([[delivery]], Client rules; [[contracts]], Contract-event record). Checking the event against the board's event accumulator is left to the client rules' later checks.
- The search for an earlier copy reads from the caller's `fromBlock` to the head in one request; a public RPC that caps `eth_getLogs` ranges fails it, as a provider error, for a long election.
- A refusal is the provider's report too: a censoring RPC can forge one, so the ballot client (part 3) must confirm a refusal through a node the voter chose before giving up on this path.
- No forced path yet: a sequencer that censors the voter's transaction leaves the ballot off the board until part 2's relay path, and a transaction that never lands ends in `receipt-timeout`.
- No end-to-end run with our servers refusing until the reference election casts (part 4).
