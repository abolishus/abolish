# T-27: Wealth-gated paths

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-1, A-9
- Status: **Partial by design.** Residual: the censorship-resistant fallback (direct submit) costs gas. Under P1-3 (option B), each share posted for an unresponsive trustee adds about 1.8 KB of gas-paid data, and a fully direct submit posts all n.

## Threat

Gas costs for direct submit, or a future project token, make voting or registration easier for those who can pay, or let weight depend on holdings.

## Mitigation

Weight is one per nullifier per poll, never a function of balances (P1-12, P1-18). Gas sponsorship covers registration; voters never transact to vote on the normal path. A future token never touches eligibility or weight.
