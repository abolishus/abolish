# T-71: L2 governance rewrites state or forges transactions

- Area: Availability and censorship resistance (G-10)
- Adversary: A-1 (and third-party L2 governance, not yet an adversary class)
- Status: **Planned.** Detection only. Residual: tampering can be detected, not prevented; Base upgrades have no delay. Governance changes elsewhere on the L2 (gas, deposit limits, sequencing bounds) can still censor or delay us.

## Threat

A Stage 1 L2's Security Council (with Coinbase, on Base) changes the rollup's rules and so its state, or forges a transaction from any address, by choice or under compulsion; on Base, Coinbase alone also sets the chain's configuration and allowlists state proposers. In April 2026 Arbitrum's Security Council, acting alone and instantly, spoofed a transfer from an exploiter's address.

## Mitigation

No contract trusts `msg.sender` alone for anything that affects a result: ballots carry membership proofs, trustee keys their DKG transcript hash, Tier 2 additions their document proof, Tier 0 additions a threshold of dedicated registrar keys pinned in the group registry and the verifier's release ([[contracts]], Group registry, departing from [[0004-l2-choice]]'s Safe owners; see STATUS, Decisions to review), Tier 1 additions their vouches ([[0014-tier1-vouching]]). Deposits and forced ballots reaching our contracts are checked against their L1 source; the verifier recomputes our contracts' state from their events and calldata, and anchored group roots come from the board's signed additions, never from L2 state (P1-19, P4-1). Each contract keeps an event accumulator ([[contracts]]): a running hash and count over every log it emits, at a fixed storage slot, so the archived event sequence can be checked against L2 state at an output root ([[verifier]] 4.4).

## History

- 2026-10-12 (P1-13b part 1, [[0023-receipt-evidence]]): receipt keys, whose signatures are attributed to the anchoring authority, are registered on L1 by that authority, never on L2, where governance could forge a registration from the authority's address and frame it with receipts under a key of its own. Verifiers and clients prove the registrations from L1 state.
- 2026-10-11 (P1-18): the event accumulator is specified ([[contracts]]), implemented in `packages/contracts` (`EventAccumulator`) and `packages/crypto` (replay), and vectored. Status stays Planned: no contract that emits result-affecting events exists yet.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-18 part 3): the trustee registry records a ceremony transcript only with signatures from a quorum of distinct seats of a pinned panel, checked on chain against the pinned keys; anyone may submit, and the sender is never checked or recorded ([[contracts]], Trustee registry). Its events go through the event accumulator.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-2 part 1): group additions and removals carry their registrars' signatures over their position (a removal, over the member count it saw), and the verifier replays each group with its own count, so a history rewritten to replay, reorder or drop a batch before a later one fails as `group-history` ([[contracts]], Group registry, Verification). Dropping the latest additions before a root's block, or any removal, leaves a history that still checks; only an honest accumulator storage proof catches it.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-18 part 4a, [[0025-contract-event-evidence]]): each event's L2 block number and timestamp are bound into its contract's event accumulator, so position, like completeness, rests on the accumulator's storage proof under an L1 output root, and no event is evidenced by a transaction proof. A contract that accepts anything because of its caller (the forced-ballot relay) records it in its event, so the verifier still traces it to its L1 call (verifier 4.5). Status unchanged.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-18 part 4b-1): the event accumulator binds each log's L2 block number and timestamp (`abolish/v1/event-accumulator-2`), implemented in `packages/contracts` and `packages/crypto` (`checkEventAccumulatorProof`) and vectored, so an archive that moves an event to another block, or across a storage proof's block, fails the check. Status unchanged: no contract is deployed.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4b-2a): `ElectionCreated` and `DefinitionRegistered` carry their block's L1 origin, authenticated by the registry's event accumulator, so no L1-attributes transaction is archived for `f` or a version cutoff; the verifier checks the origin against finalized L1, the event's L2 timestamp and the release's pinned drift, and checks that a registration's origin is no earlier than its own election's creation's ([[contracts]], Election registry, Events, The L1 origin). Status unchanged: nothing is deployed and the verifier's check is P1-19's.
