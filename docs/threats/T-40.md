# T-40: DKG manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: custody of per-election KEM keys and of the stored share ciphertexts until they are destroyed after the tally is each trustee's responsibility; T-14 covers collusion.

## Threat

A rogue-key attack, or an adversary biasing the joint key (possible in plain Pedersen/Feldman DKG, per Gennaro, Jarecki, Krawczyk and Rabin), or a trustee leaking or losing a share during or after the ceremony.

## Mitigation

No DKG for plurality and approval: voters share each ballot, so there is no joint key to bias ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]]); a ranked-choice mixnet that needs a threshold key uses a DKG variant secure against bias (GJKR-style), chosen in its follow-up ADR (P1-14); complaints and disqualification recorded in the public transcript; share custody guidance in a runbook.
