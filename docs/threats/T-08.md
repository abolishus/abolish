# T-08: Electorate definition manipulation

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.** Who may define an organisation's electorate is the organisation's problem, not ours; we make the definition public and fixed.

## Threat

A closed electorate (organisational vote) is defined with extra or missing members.

## Mitigation

The electorate (group root at a fixed block) is bound into the election definition, whose hash is on L2 before voting opens (P1-18).
