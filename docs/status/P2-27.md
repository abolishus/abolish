- State: todo

## Scope

The rest of the account side ([[P2-5]]): who you are to the app, never how you voted.

- A creator's public profile: the polls they asked, nothing else.
- Settings: name, sign-in passkeys, notification choices, data export and account deletion.
- Notifications by email, and by web push where the browser allows: results ready, live batches on followed polls ([[P2-25]]), moderation decisions on your polls ([[P2-10]]).
- The account is also what voting needs at Tier 0 ([[P2-30]]); the account page shows that the device holds a voting identity and offers its backup, never anything about answers.

Rules for every part, as [[P2-19]]'s: everyday register by default ([[language]]); no hard-coded strings (`messages.ts`); CSP-safe (no inline `style`); server-rendered where the page is the website's; a fixed analytics template per route, never a path or poll ID (T-67); each component a story with a play function passing axe at desktop width and 360px; SSR and serve tests per route; the mockup's story deleted in the PR whose page matches it. Privacy holds everywhere: nothing an account does on the website (following, reacting, sharing, notifications) is ever stored, logged or keyed with a ballot, nullifier, receipt or the fact of having answered, and the T-10 no-join tests cover every new table, queue and log line ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Identity).
