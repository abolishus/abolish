# T-16: Small-count disclosure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-10
- Status: **Not mitigated.** A tally is public by design; a poll with one Tier 2 voter reveals that voter's choice to anyone who knows they voted. Stated next to every per-tier result. Regional cells, which [[0002-everlasting-privacy]] allows where results are regional, would increase this exposure; verifier 2.8 rejects them until P1-15 specifies them.

## Threat

A per-tier or per-region tally over very few voters reveals how individuals voted (unanimity, or one voter in a tier).

## Mitigation

Results are published per tier as the brief requires. The tally partition is fixed in the election definition registered before voting opens and is the tiers only until P1-15 specifies regional cells, whose region must then come from a membership proof, never a voter-chosen, grindable or per-ballot field, with a protocol-wide floor on each cell's admitted electorate ([[0003-tally-scheme]], Decision; verifier 2.8). Trustee nodes and the ballot client check the partition before releasing a share or casting. Openings are checked per registered cell (7.4), small tiers are flagged (8.2) and the result pages and API flag them too (P3-6). Whether a tier below the minimum may be withheld or merged is the owner's question in [[0006-trustees]]; until then every tier is opened and flagged.

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-10 (P0-ops-5): mitigation updated for the registered partition (tiers only until P1-15 specifies proven regional cells with a size floor), checked before any share is released or ballot cast, per-cell openings (verifier 2.8, 7.4) and per-tier flags (8.2). This closes two paths `crypto-review` found in a looser draft: a per-ballot partition that opened every ballot, and a voter-ground regional cell that made a vote-sale receipt.
