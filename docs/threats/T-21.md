# T-21: Tier 2 proof front-running and trust roots

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-9, A-1, A-6
- Status: **Planned** ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]). No third-chain dependency: zkPassport verifies off-chain or on Base. Residual: the master-list signers themselves, and an operator who omits genuine certificates (which only excludes people).

## Threat

A Tier 2 proof relayed through the QR handoff is front-run to register an attacker's identity commitment and burn the victim's document nullifier; or the proof app's trust roots (issuer certificate registry, any third-chain dependency) are compromised.

## Mitigation

The Tier 2 proof binds the identity commitment being registered; trust roots are public, versioned and checkable; the ADR confirms no third-chain dependency (P3-1, P3-4).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]] chooses zkPassport. The proof binds the identity commitment, tier group, chain ID and registry; our verifier checks proofs locally with the pinned bb.js against vendored verification keys, never zkPassport's hosted verifier or upgradeable contracts, pins the inner-circuit set itself, chains every certificate to a pinned ICAO or national master list, requires each root to have appeared in zkPassport's Base registry, and checks each addition against the root valid at its own L2 block. Self was rejected because its verification depends on Celo state.
