## Scope

The author's side beyond [[P2-22]]'s first flow: making a good poll fast, and managing it after.

- Templates and examples for common questions; a live preview of the card as voters will see it.
- Topics and places, chosen at creation, for [[P2-23]].
- Editing before a poll opens, closing early (the close rule permitting, [[close]]), duplicating, and archiving from the author's list.
- "Setting up your poll" stays under a minute from submit to open, measured on staging, with honest progress.

Rules for every part, as [[P2-19]]'s: everyday register by default ([[language]]); no hard-coded strings (`messages.ts`); CSP-safe (no inline `style`); server-rendered where the page is the website's; a fixed analytics template per route, never a path or poll ID (T-67); each component a story with a play function passing axe at desktop width and 360px; SSR and serve tests per route; the mockup's story deleted in the PR whose page matches it. Privacy holds everywhere: nothing an account does on the website (following, reacting, sharing, notifications) is ever stored, logged or keyed with a ballot, nullifier, receipt or the fact of having answered, and the T-10 no-join tests cover every new table, queue and log line ([[PROJECT_BRIEF]], Identity).

## Notes

- **Part 1a**: [[0041-poll-topics-and-places]] (option B): a poll's topic and place are listing tags the operator keeps beside the definition, not words of the display text, which would take display-text version 3 and definition version 8. `PollDraft` (`packages/core/src/poll-draft-rules.ts`) gains an optional `topic`, one of `POLL_TOPICS` (fourteen codes, only ever added to), and an optional `place`, 1 to `MAX_PLACE_LENGTH` (80) code points in NFC of single-spaced words with no space at either end and no control characters, line or paragraph separators, bidi marks, embeddings, overrides or isolates, or invisible characters other than the zero-width joiner and non-joiner (`placeIsWellFormed`; T-67). `pollDraftProblem` refuses them as `topic` and `place`, after `tiers` (a place's lone surrogate or U+0000 is `text`, as for every string). The stored draft keeps them only when given, so drafts saved before keep loading. The website's drafts handler (`apps/web/src/node/drafts.ts`) takes both, bounded for transport only (16 and 160 UTF-16 units), and its client reads them back (`src/account/drafts.ts`). Tested in `packages/core/tests/poll-drafts.test.ts` (the rules' order, every topic and a fast-check property that nothing else passes, the place's refusals, a fast-check property that a well-formed place is its own normal form and that a tidied one is well formed, a place of the wrong JSON type, the tags left out of the display text and questions the definition commits to, a tagged draft submitted and read back, and the stored keys) and `apps/web/tests/serve.test.ts` and `tests/drafts-client.test.ts` (round trip, 422 with the rule, 400 past the transport bounds, a stored answer of the wrong type refused).
- **Part 1a known-weak**: nothing sets or shows the tags yet (the form's fields are part 1b; the listing and its filters part 1c). The tags are the operator's claim, not committed by the definition, so the operator could refile a poll (T-68), and a wipe of Postgres loses them; no result depends on them. Two spellings of one place are two places, and confusable letters (Latin and Cyrillic a) still let two places look alike.
