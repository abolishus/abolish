# T-45: Observed coercion

- Area: Coercion and vote buying (G-11)
- Adversary: A-4
- Status: **Partial.** Defeated by a coercer who watches until the poll closes; who votes last with the voter's secret; who watches a cast in the last blocks before close, leaving no time for a newer L1 block (the voter can then only void the watched ballot with a tie, [[revoting]], Residuals); or who demands the voter abstain (forced abstention is visible through the nullifier to anyone holding the secret). Re-voting also gives no deniability against anyone who has seen one of the voter's ballots or receipts, since later ballots under that nullifier are public ([[verifier]] check 6.1). Where ballots are decrypted individually (a ranked-choice mixnet, P1-4), re-voting doesn't stop a pattern attack (T-70).

## Threat

A coercer watches the voter vote, or demands the receipt.

## Mitigation

Re-voting where the last ballot counts, protected against replay by T-38; receipts prove inclusion, not content. "Last" is the ballot naming the newest L1 block ([[revoting]], [[0011-revote-rule]]): a block hash can't be known in advance, so ballots made while watched once and posted later all lose to any re-vote made after them, and a watched voter can void the watched ballot at once by casting on the same block, then outrank it about 36 seconds later.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-13): pre-made ballots defeated by binding a fresh L1 block into each ballot; the last-moment window and tie escape stated ([[revoting]]).
