# T-29: Tally or decryption manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A trustee or coordinator publishes a wrong decryption share or combines shares wrongly.

## Mitigation

Each trustee's summed shares are checked by the other trustees against the product of the public VSS commitments, and the published opening of each cell must open the product of the public commitments, which every verifier recomputes from the board ([[0002-everlasting-privacy]], [[0015-tally-publication]], [[tally]]; P1-15, P1-19). A wrong opening is attributed to its signer; a wrong summed share is known only to the trustees, because summed shares aren't published (T-15).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-15, part 1): the tally is specified ([[tally]], [[0015-tally-publication]] option C): per cell, the product of the counted ballots' VSS commitments; trustees exchange summed shares privately and check each against it, so a wrong sum or a sum over another set of ballots is excluded; any trustee holding `k` passing sums publishes the cell's opening, which everyone checks against the aggregate commitment (`tally-opening`) with a count of at most the cell's ballots (`tally-range`). A wrong result can't pass; a wrong opening is attributed to its signer, but a wrong summed share only among the trustees, since publishing per-seat sums would leak ballots (T-15). `packages/crypto` implements it (`openCell`, `checkOpening`), with vectors from an independent implementation.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15, part 2a, [[tally]], Opening record): an opening is published as a tally opening (record type `0x0006`), signed with the combining seat's per-election signing key over the definition hash, the cell, the counted set (`aggregate_hash`) and the opening, so a failing opening is attributed only to a registered seat whose signature verifies over this election and cell; a replayed, relabelled or re-signed opening is unattributed and never frames its signer. A passing opening counts whoever signed it, so stripping signatures can't stop the tally. `checkTally` implements verifier 7.2 to 7.4, with vectors from an independent implementation.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-15, part 2b, [[tally]], Summed-share message): summed shares travel signed by the sender's per-election key, bound to the definition, cell, counted set and both seats, so a recipient knows which seat sent a failing sum; `aggregate_hash` names the sender's counted set by its constant-term aggregates, and the summed-share check against the recipient's own aggregate is what shows the sums are over the same set. The signature gives no formal public proof of a wrong sum (checking a decryption needs the recipient's ML-KEM secret), though a recipient that publishes a message's AEAD key gives convincing evidence of what the sender sent; the protocol defines no such complaint, since it would publish per-seat values (ADR 0015), so a wrong summed share stays attributed only among the trustees.
- 2026-10-12 (P1-12 part 3b, [[delivery]]): a trustee's complaint is signed with its per-election key over the definition, ballot hash, seat and proof, so an unfounded complaint is attributed only to a registered seat whose signature verifies over this election, ballot and seat; a proven complaint excludes the ballot whoever signed it. A share receipt contradicted by a proven complaint about the same seat and ballot is reported against that seat.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-1, [[keyed-nullifier]]): a seat's keyed-nullifier answer is signed with its identity key and checked signature first, so an answer failing only its proof is a wrong answer attributed to its seat; anything failing earlier is attributed to no one.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2b-2a, [[nullifier-key]]): keyed-nullifier answers are signed with the seat's registration signing key from the key's transcript rather than its identity key; a seat's failures in the key's rounds (no dealing, an unanswered complaint, a reveal that doesn't open its commitments) are attributed to it from its identity-signed messages, while a withheld dealt share is attributed to no one.
- 2026-10-13 (P3-4 part 2b-2b, [[nullifier-reshare]]): in a reshare, an old seat that deals something other than its own share (`constant`), leaves a complaint unanswered or reveals a share that doesn't open its commitments is attributed from its identity-signed messages and dropped; a new seat's bad signing-key proof or reused key is attributed to it; a withheld reshared share is attributed to no one.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-19 part 4h, [[verifier]], Tally openings): the verifier sorts every tally opening on the board copy into a cell or stray and attributes it by the registered signing keys, as `checkTally` does (`sortTallyOpenings`), and reports the boarding-rule breaches it can decide without the counted set: stray, another number of entries than options, unattributed, and a seat's second opening of a cell: evidence against that seat only when its signed message differs from the first one's, since anyone can replay a signed record, so a replay (`tally-repeat`) is the operator's breach alone and never frames its signer. Whether an opening is over the counted set and opens it, and so the tally itself (7.1 to 7.3), waits on selection.
- 2026-10-13 (P2-14 part 4, `packages/crypto`): the tally reads definition version 5 (`tallyRules`, from the registered definition alone, refusing one that isn't well-formed and a version-6 one whose two panels' tally isn't built): a trustee's `openCell` and the verifier's `checkOpening` and `checkTally` run `tally-question-range` after `tally-range`, naming the first question whose total lies outside `[min_q · |S_c|, max_q · |S_c|]`, so a trustee never publishes, and no verifier accepts, a cell whose per-question result no admitted ballots could give; each question's result is its slice of the opening (`questionResults`), with nothing across questions. Summed-share messages run under definition versions 3 to 5, over every cell of their partitions. Vectors from the independent generator exercise the check with ballots no admission would let through.
- 2026-10-15 (P1-19 part 4o, [[verifier]], Counted set and tally): once selection passes, the verifier recomputes each cell's counted set (the selected ballots the delivery check keeps) and runs the tally checks over it with `checkTally`: 7.2 fails at the first cell with no passing opening of its aggregate, 7.4 adds each opening's `tally-aggregate` (over another counted set) or `tally-opening` break, attributed as before, and the report gives each cell's counts. `tally-late` can't fail where this runs, since it runs only when every entry that could change a counted set lies at or below the floors of `s_S`; a board holding a contract-path delivery record stays unverifiable until 4.4 authenticates its timestamp.
- 2026-10-15 (P1-12e, [[delivery]], Share-complaint batch, Capacity; [[contracts]], Event tables (version 4)): each entry of a complaint batch is attributed by the batch's one signature under its own tag, `abolish/v1/sig/share-complaint-batch`, so an unfounded entry is its seat's exactly as a single complaint would be.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-15 part 1b, [[tally]], Running opening record): a running opening is signed under its own tag, `abolish/v1/sig/running-opening`, over its header, definition, cell, snapshot, aggregate hash, seat and opening, so a signed release replayed into another election, cell or snapshot, or as a tally opening, is unattributed and never frames its signer; a passing one counts whoever made it, as a tally opening does.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-16 part 3b, [[tally]], Opening record (version 3)): a partial opening is signed under its own tag, `abolish/v1/sig/partial-opening`, over the whole record but its signature, header and panel included, and attributed only to a seat of the panel it names, by that panel's registered key set, so it is never blamed on the other panel's seat of the same index nor re-framed as a version-1 opening; each panel's partial opening is checked against that panel's aggregate, and the count they sum to is range-checked.
- 2026-10-16 (P2-15 part 2a, `packages/crypto` `running-opening.ts`): a running opening is attributed only when its `signer` is a registered seat and the signature verifies under that seat's key over the header and every field, so a signed release moved to another snapshot, cell, definition or version, or signed under the tally opening's tag, is unattributed; tested against `running-opening.json` and by fast-check.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-15 part 3a, `packages/verifier` `releases.ts`): every running opening the boarding rule refuses is reported against the operator and, except a replay of a message its seat already signed (`running-repeat`), against the seat its signature attributes it to; 7.5 fails only on evidence that binding is broken (two passing openings of one release and cell with different `(V, R)`, or counts above the running set's size or outside a question's limits), never on a signature. Not wired into a verdict until part 3b.
- 2026-10-17 (P2-16 part 3c-2, `packages/crypto` `tally-opening.ts`): `checkPartialTally` checks each panel's partial opening against that panel's aggregate and the summed count's range, attributes an opening only to a seat of the panel it names under `abolish/v1/sig/partial-opening`, and reports `tally-author-late` and `tally-early`, checked against `panel-tally.json` from an independent implementation (a relabelled author opening is unattributed and fails `tally-aggregate`).
- 2026-10-17 (P2-16 part 4d-3d-2, `packages/verifier`): beside an author panel 7.4 sorts each partial opening by its panel byte and attributes it only by that panel's registered key set, reads `tally-second` and `tally-repeat` per cell, panel and seat over version 3's signed message, so an author seat's opening is never a second of the default seat with its index, and reports `tally-aggregate` and `tally-opening` against the opening's own panel's aggregate once the counted set is known.
- 2026-10-18 (P2-13 part 4b-3a, `apps/trustee-node` `ComplaintDuty`): a seat complains only about a ballot of the complaint scope it decided at `c_I`, whose contract-path posting for its seat, submitted by `t_D`, passes the public check and opens to shares that fail decryption or the share check, so it never reveals a DH value for shares that open and never signs an unfounded complaint; it decides once, from `t_D + W_C` with its own L2 node's finalized history past it, and sends the stored batches by `c_K`. Still open: no deployment steps the duty yet (part 4b-3b).
