# P1-12d: Share receipts carried many to a signature

- State: done (#281, one change made in one session at the owner's request rather than as separate parts; the complaint side and the national-scale obligation are [[P1-12e]])
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Raise the cap that [[P1-12c]] part 2's capacity check puts on an election's electorate ([[delivery]], Capacity; STATUS, Known-weak). On the forced path a contract-path receipt cost about 67,000 gas, because each carried its own P-256 signature, storage key and event. With the deployed windows and `R` of one hour, that limited an election to about 1,150 members with 3 seats, or about 23,600 with `R` of 21 hours (T-25, T-52, T-54).

## Known-weak

- **Complaints now bind about as much as receipts.** At about 157,000 gas a complaint, two per member, the cap at `R` of one hour (20,992 with 3 seats) is within 12% of the complaint-bound cap at 21 hours, and two elections at their caps whose complaint intervals overlap don't fit together. [[P1-12e]].
- **A batch's pairs are free to its seat**, as a single receipt's are: 64 chosen bytes per pair, attributed to the seat (T-17).
- **No author-panel batch yet.** Ballot versions 4 and 5 fix no batch version; P2-16 part 5, which brings version-2 delivery records to the contract path, MUST bring a batch version 2 naming the panel.
- **Contract-event version 3 must be retired** with versions 1 and 2 before any board is deployed; the registry refuses it, so a board deployed beside another registry depends on that registry refusing it too.
- **The trustee node doesn't send anything through the contract path yet**; it is sized for batches (stub check 4). The simulation sends batches of one pair per ballot, not a node's full obligation at `c_I`.
