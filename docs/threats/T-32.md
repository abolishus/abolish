# T-32: Ballot copying and replay

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-9, A-4
- Status: **Planned.**

## Threat

A voter copies another voter's ciphertext (to vote the same way, which also leaks information about a target's vote in small polls) or replays a ballot into another poll.

## Mitigation

Validity proofs are bound to the ballot's nullifier and the poll's context, so a copied ciphertext can't carry a valid proof under a different nullifier (P1-12, following Helios ballot-independence fixes).

Index: [[THREAT_MODEL]].

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-12, part 1): every validity challenge binds the ballot's nullifier and tier through `statement_hash` ([[ballot-validity]]), so a ballot's commitments and proofs copied under another nullifier fail `option-proof`; vectors and a property test check it.
