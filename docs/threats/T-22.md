# T-22: Tier 1 social graph exposure

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1, A-10
- Status: **Planned** ([[0014-tier1-vouching]], P3-3). Residual: the timing of vouches around an addition, and network metadata (T-11).

## Threat

The vouching graph is sensitive personal data, and publishing or storing it links people to each other and to their Tier 1 membership.

## Mitigation

Store only what Tier 1 strictly needs; the vouching design states what is public and what the server learns (P3-3).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P3-3): [[0014-tier1-vouching]]: vouches are Semaphore proofs, so nobody, including us, learns who vouched. A voucher's vouches have nullifiers unrelated to each other, to their votes and to their Tier 2 commitment. Vouching runs device to device with no account. A public signed web of trust (it publishes the graph) and an operator-held graph were rejected. The graph's privacy is computational (Poseidon preimages, T-15). Residuals: vouches arriving close together in time, and IP addresses seen by a server or relay (T-11).
