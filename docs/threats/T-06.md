# T-06: Group stuffing by the operator

- Area: Eligibility and Sybil resistance (G-1, G-2)
- Adversary: A-2
- Status: **Planned** for Tier 2 ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]). It holds only with the verifier pinning the inner-circuit set and accepting only US-issued documents chained to pinned US CSCA certificates (an operator-published certificate list, or a foreign CSCA, could otherwise mint members), and only if Tier 2 proofs are published (the ADR's zero-knowledge gate; its fallback is trustee-attested additions). For Tier 1, **planned** ([[0014-tier1-vouching]]): an addition counts only with vouches from 3 distinct Tier 2 members within their budgets, which aren't scoped to a group, checked from the board, so Tier 1 stuffing is bounded by Tier 2's. For Tier 0, the operator can add members with no external check: **not mitigated** there beyond public counts.

## Threat

Whoever adds commitments to a Semaphore group adds fake members.

## Mitigation

Tier 2 additions carry a verifiable proof of the document (P3-1 ADR must require that the proof, or its verification, be public and re-checkable). Group sizes are public.

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-17): an operator who adds one commitment adds at most one voting identity: membership is proven against a complete depth-32 tier tree that verifiers rebuild from the board, never a LeanIMT root, in which one added value could hide a private tree of identities ([[membership]], Tier tree).
- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]: each Tier 2 addition carries a zkPassport proof that the verifier re-checks from the board. The verifier pins the inner-circuit set and the US CSCA anchors, so neither our operator nor zkPassport's oracle can admit a circuit or a CSCA of their own. Publishing the proofs waits on P3-4 showing they are statistically zero-knowledge; if not, additions are attested by a trustee threshold, and Tier 2 stuffing is then checkable only by trusting the attesters.
- 2026-10-11 (P3-3): [[0014-tier1-vouching]]: Tier 1 additions are authorised by `k = 3` anonymous vouches from distinct Tier 2 members, each with a lifetime budget of `L = 12`, which the verifier checks from the board. This replaces our Safe's signature for Tier 1 ([[verifier]] 2.4 changes in P3-3 part 2). The operator can still refuse to post vouches; direct submit covers that (T-25).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-2 part 1): [[contracts]], Group registry: a Tier 0 addition counts only with EIP-712 approvals from a threshold (at least 2) of registrars pinned in the registry at deployment and in the verifier's release, binding the chain, the registry, the group, its tier and the batch's position, so no relay or single key adds, replays or reorders members, and a history rewritten by L2 governance to do so fails verification (`group-history`; dropping the latest additions, or any removal, is the T-71 residual), and each addition is attributable to named keys. Tier 0 stays **not mitigated** against the registrars themselves, who hold their keys online for automatic registration (T-69).
- 2026-10-12 (P1-17b): the tier tree and the lenient replay of group contracts without a signed sequence are implemented; a proof from a private tree whose root is a registered leaf is a rejection vector.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-3 part 2a, #144): a Tier 1 addition carries its own authorisation (3 vouches, [[vouching]]), checkable from the record and the chain's binding alone, never from who sent it. The slot scope binds only the chain and the registry, so creating another Tier 1 group gives no voucher fresh slots. Part 2b replaces the Safe for Tier 1 in [[verifier]] 2.4.
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 1, [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]]): Tier 2 uniqueness stays a trust assumption in the attesters, and the trustee-keyed nullifier stays off the board even though the owner's decision would allow boarding proofs once it exists. The bound is now explicit: an addition counts only with `max(k, n − 1)` attesters signing its group position, each only once every earlier nullifier reached it, so a duplicate needs 5 corrupt seats of 7. That holds only if the registry and verifier 2.4 require that many attestation signatures and honest seats sign at most one addition per position, which part 2 specifies.
- 2026-10-14 (P2-17 part 2b-1, [[membership]], Reported counts): in a rolling poll the registrars could add identities during the poll; each tier's growth per anchoring period is reported from the group registry's events, so a stuffing run is public as a shape, though not distinguishable from a genuine surge.
- 2026-10-16 (P3-3 part 2b-1, [[vouching]], Counting across the deployment): [[verifier]] 2.4 now counts Tier 1 additions by their vouches across the registry instead of the Safe's signature, so neither the operator nor any registrar can add a Tier 1 member without 3 unspent vouches from Tier 2 members, and an addition is attributed to its group by what its vouches bind.
- 2026-10-16 (P3-3 part 3a, [[contracts]], Group registry, Tier 1 additions; [[0014-tier1-vouching]], Amendment 2): specified, not built. The registry admits a Tier 1 addition on its vouches alone, never a registrar's or a sender's signature, and has no Tier 1 removal, so the registrars can neither add a Tier 1 member nor exclude one.
- 2026-10-16 (P3-2 part 4d-2a, [[registration]], The batch): the batch job signs a group's next position only once its last batch is final and the group's latest count equals its finalized count, keeps each record before first sending it and resends it unchanged until it lands, and abandons it only once finalized history holds another record at its position; it also never signs below the end of a batch of its own seen final, or where any addition already sits, so L2 nodes at different blocks can't lead it to sign a second record for a position. That rests on Redis keeping the in-flight record ([[registration]], Queue: no eviction, persistence): a record lost from the store while still only in a bundler's mempool isn't covered. It never signs a commitment live in any Tier 0 group as of the latest block it read before signing, a self-paid copy made while the commitment waited included (the decision [[0024-tier0-registration]] option D left open). Tested against an in-memory registry; the real chain adapter is part 4d-2b.
