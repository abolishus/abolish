# P4-1: Hourly L1 anchoring

- State: in progress (part 1, the L1 board anchor contract, merged in #73)
- Phase: Phase 4: Censorship resistance

## Scope

Hourly L1 anchoring of board roots (worker + contract).

## Plan

1. **The board anchor** (this part): the L1 contract and its spec, [[contracts]], Board anchor. The anchoring clock's values (hourly periods, a 12-hour deadline), the anchoring authority, the window and one-checkpoint-per-segment rules, and how a verifier reads every anchor of an election from state.
2. **The hourly job and the deployment:** a worker job (P2-4, #64) that computes each election's checkpoint after every period and sends them through the Safe, from two independent runners, with alerts well before the deadline; a deploy script and a `ci:` workflow deploying the anchor to Sepolia with source verification, and the deployment record. Needs #64 merged and a `ci:` PR (rule B).
3. **Reading anchors:** the verifier's L1 inputs (P1-19 part 3) and the receipt check (P1-13) read the per-segment slots with storage proofs.

## What was built (part 1)

- `packages/contracts`:
  - `BoardAnchor` (`src/BoardAnchor.sol`): immutable `epoch`, `period`, `deadline` and `authority`; `anchor(bytes checkpoints, bytes locator)` takes concatenated checkpoint encodings from the authority only, refuses an empty board, a checkpoint outside `[end, end + deadline]` of its period, and a second checkpoint for an election and segment (equal or not, across calls or within one); stores the checkpoint hash at an ERC-7201 slot per election and segment; keeps each election's lowest anchored segment, so a verifier proves every slot from there to the seal; emits `CheckpointAnchored` with the full checkpoint through the event accumulator. Each call may also carry the `archiveLocator` of [[0005-permanent-archive]] (`u16(version) ‖ body`, framed, not interpreted, at most 128 bytes), logged as `ArchiveLocated`, alone or with checkpoints. Deployment refuses a zero period and an authority without code or with only an EIP-7702 delegation.
  - `CheckpointLib`: the checkpoint hash, `H(DS("abolish/v1/board-checkpoint", encode(checkpoint)))`.
  - `CanonicalCalldata` (`src/CanonicalCalldata.sol`): the election registry's canonical-calldata check, moved to a library both contracts use (the registry's revert data is unchanged), plus the two-argument form the anchor uses.
- [[contracts]], Board anchor: deployment values and why the deadline is 12 hours, the anchoring rules, the event, and verification from state; [[board]], Owned elsewhere, points there.
- Threats: T-23, T-24, T-52, T-53, T-49, T-50, T-17, T-69.

## Tested (part 1)

- Every checkpoint in `docs/spec/vectors/board.json` (from the independent Python generator) hashes to its published hash in Solidity, and the `valid` board's anchors, each sent at its vector time under the vectors' clock, are all accepted and stored under those hashes.
- A unit test per refusal (caller, empty board, before the period ends, past the deadline, before the epoch, a second checkpoint equal or not, twice in one call, malformed lengths, non-canonical calldata: a trailing word or byte, dirty padding, a moved argument), the window's exact boundaries on both sides, all-or-nothing calls, several elections in one call, the log's topics and data and the accumulator, and the hash at the documented storage slot.
- The locator: kept in state with a count at the documented slots, logged after the checkpoints, alone at any time, framing refused (lengths 1, 2, 129, version 0) without anchoring the call's checkpoints, refused from anyone but the authority; a fuzz test that any accepted mutation of a canonical call is canonical; two-argument calldata refused when trailing, dirty after either argument, swapped, with a junk word, or with the second argument pointing back into the first.
- No personal data: the calldata and log of a real anchor pass the registry's text detector, and text appended to a checkpoint is refused.
- The lowest anchored segment, lowered only, at its documented slot; a delegated EOA refused as the authority.
- Fuzz: any checkpoint at a time around its window is stored exactly when it is non-empty and inside the window, under its hash; far from the window, nothing is.
- Invariant suite (time moves, three elections, eight segments, batches of up to three, calls from strangers): the stored hashes equal a shadow of the first in-window checkpoint per election and segment, calls succeed exactly when the rules allow, each election's lowest segment matches, and the accumulator counts every anchored checkpoint; a unit test shows the handler does anchor. Passes under `FOUNDRY_PROFILE=ci`.
- Mutation checks by hand: loosening the deadline by one second, opening the window one second early, dropping the empty-board, duplicate, authority, canonical-calldata, code or EIP-7702 check, raising the lowest segment instead of lowering it, or changing the tag's length byte each fail a test.

## Known-weak

- Nothing is deployed and nothing anchors yet (part 2), so every board is unanchored and the `reference-election` check can't cover anchoring until P1-20 uses this contract on Anvil.
- The anchoring authority can still fail a running election, attributably, by anchoring a checkpoint no board matches first; the contract then keeps it. A Safe makes that a quorum's act, not one key's ([[contracts]], Board anchor, Known-weak).
- The anchor can't refuse a segment past an election's seal (it can't read the definition); verifiers ignore those.
- A call with one checkpoint costs about 84,000 gas, each further one about 34,000, an election's first about 22,000 more; on mainnet that is a recurring cost for the owner to fund.
- The anchoring runners hold the Safe's quorum online, and the Safe's configuration (owners, threshold, modules, guard, singleton) can change after deployment; the verifier doesn't check it yet, and part 2 must use no module or guard that bypasses the threshold.
- A quorum held at some moment could anchor checkpoints for elections not yet created. Verifiers ignore anchors for segments before an election's first segment (its creation's L1 origin), and the creator MUST check that the few slots from that segment on are empty once creation is final, abandoning the identifier otherwise (a per-slot check, so a briefly held quorum can't block future identifiers wholesale); a creator that skips the check can lose its election to an anchor made in the drift window before creation.
- The anchored checkpoint carries no content address of its segment object ([[board]], Residuals); adding one (P4-3) means a new anchor deployment.
