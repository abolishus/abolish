# T-19: Third-party proof app privacy

- Area: Ballot secrecy, unlinkability and privacy (G-3, G-4, G-5, G-12)
- Adversary: A-1, A-2
- Status: **Planned** ([[0010-tier2-identity-provider]]: zkPassport, which proves on the device). Residual: until P5-3 and P5-4 ship our own app, voters run zkPassport's app, whose installed binary nobody checks against its open source.

## Threat

The Tier 2 proof apps (zkPassport, Self) see the document; a malicious or compromised app leaks it or links it to the identity commitment.

## Mitigation

ADR compares apps and requires client-side proving (P3-1); our native NFC plugin later removes the dependency (P5-3).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P3-1): [[0010-tier2-identity-provider]] chooses zkPassport, whose proving is client-side; Self was rejected partly because its registration proof is generated in a TEE that receives the encrypted document data and the user's secret. If zkPassport's non-salted nullifier mode is used (an owner question), an issuing state can test whether a document it issued registered at Tier 2 (registration, never a vote).
- 2026-10-12 (P3-4 part 2a, [[0022-tier2-keyed-nullifier]], Amendment): zkPassport SDK's request can't name our panel (only an OPRF key ID for TACEO's node set), and its salted mode needs strict FaceMatch. So the zkPassport app can't be used for the panel's keyed-nullifier query. The app that learns `x` and `N` will be our own (P5-3, P5-4), and real-document Tier 2 through the panel waits for it.
