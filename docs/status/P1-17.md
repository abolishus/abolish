## Scope

Originally "`packages/circuits` scaffold (Noir 1.0.0-beta.22, bb 5.0.0-nightly.20260522): a membership + per-poll nullifier circuit compatible with Semaphore groups, with soundness tests". [[0002-everlasting-privacy]] requires membership proofs to be statistically or perfectly zero-knowledge, and asks this item to show that for bb's zero-knowledge UltraHonk or fall back to Semaphore's Groth16 circuit. [[0009-membership-proof]] takes the fallback, so the item is re-scoped:

- the ADR choosing the proof system, the scope and the tier binding;
- [[membership]]: the statement, public inputs, scope, message and field mappings, tree depth and the verifier's checks;
- `packages/crypto`: the scope, the field mapping and the depth, with cross-language vectors.

The verification keys and Groth16 verification move to [[P1-17b]]. The Noir workspace scaffold comes with the first custom circuit (Tier 2 predicates, [[P3-1]]).

Requirement carried from verifier 2.4, 2.8 and 5.3 (P0-ops-8): a ballot's tier is fixed by its membership proof's public inputs. Met twice: by the root (P1-10b requires distinct roots per tier) and by the per-tier-group scope.

## Tested

- `docs/spec/vectors/membership.json`, from an independent Python implementation (`scripts/membership-vectors.py`) with its own Keccak-256, checked against `hashlib.sha3_256` (same permutation) and the published Keccak-256 of the empty string and of 32 zero bytes: 8 field-mapping vectors, 5 scopes and 2 rejected roots (0, and `r`), 13 depths.
- fast-check properties (passing at `FC_NUM_RUNS=1000`): `F` stays below 2²⁴⁸; scopes differ across tier roots and across definitions; malformed lengths, out-of-field roots and root 0 are rejected; the depth is the smallest that fits the tree.

## Independent review

A fresh in-session subagent reviewed the diff against `.github/review/crypto-review.md`. It confirmed the upstream facts (circuit hash, Semaphore's `hash` and public-signal order, depth inference in `generateProof`), the choice of Groth16 under ADR 0002, and the scope against replay across polls, chains, deployments and tiers, and regenerated the vectors independently. Findings:

- Blocking, fixed: Semaphore's circuit doesn't constrain the proof length, so root 0 is provable by anyone with unlimited nullifiers. A tier root of 0 now fails the definition ([[membership]], [[verifier]] 5.1), `nullifierScope` rejects it, and a vector covers it.
- Blocking, fixed: the depth's `n_g` was ambiguous. It is now the LeanIMT leaf count, removed members included, distinct from the anonymity-set size.
- Non-blocking, fixed: the witness is described exactly as the circuit's (`merkleProofIndex` is one field element); chosen-scope linkage by another Semaphore app is stated, with a prover rule; the reference-string check is mandatory, done once by P1-17b, with CSPRNG randomisers at the prover; the ADR argues why Groth16 verification over `@noble/curves` is composition and lists its mandatory checks.
- Non-blocking, handed to P1-17b: no published Semaphore answer ties `F` to Semaphore's output yet; P1-17b's real proofs will.

## Known-weak

- No membership proof is verified yet: Groth16 verification, the pinned keys and real Semaphore proofs as vectors are [[P1-17b]].
- The Semaphore source is cited by tag, commit and file hash, not vendored; P1-17b vendors what it verifies against.
- `F` is checked against an independent Keccak and published Keccak answers, not yet against Semaphore's own output (P1-17b).
- Root 0 is the one degenerate root the circuit admits for anyone; any other root needs a real member's secret.
- Membership soundness rests on Semaphore's ceremony and on BN254 (about 100–110 bits); privacy against a subverted ceremony needs the prover's proving-key check (P2-8, P5-4).
- One identity in two tier groups can have one ballot counted in each tier (G-2 allows it); results are only ever reported per tier.
- The message `M` is P1-12's and P1-13's to define.
