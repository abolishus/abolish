# T-35: Time manipulation

- Area: Integrity and verifiability (G-7, G-8, G-9)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual ([[0030-close-rule]]): within δ, a sequencer withholding batches can still admit ballots made after close (beyond it the election fails, unless `k` trustees lie), and the board operator can through the board path within `s_A`, T-50.

## Threat

Ballots are accepted after close, or rejected before, by a server clock, or by the L2 sequencer choosing timestamps, lagging its L1 origin or rebuilding unposted blocks with earlier timestamps.

## Mitigation

Opening and closing are defined by L2 timestamps in the election definition, not server time, together with a bound on when the ballot's batch reached L1; forced ballots carry an L1 timestamp stamped by our relay contract; the verifier applies the same rule ([[0004-l2-choice]], P1-18, P1-19).

## History

- 2026-10-11 (P1-13): `l1-ref-after-close` bounds the block a ballot names, not when it was made; P1-18's close rule must not admit ballots made after close, or a coercer with the sequencer could outrank every honest re-vote ([[revoting]], Residuals).
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-1, [[0030-close-rule]]): decided, not built. An ordinary direct-submitted ballot counts if its L2 block is at or before close; a relayed ballot by its L1 stamp; a board-path ballot by the operator's word within `s_A`. The panel's close statements give a close head at `closes_at + δ`, and a ballot after it that would change a result fails the election (`close-contested`), so a sequencer rebuilding pre-close blocks gets δ silently and beyond δ only with `k` seats lying (checkable only while the L1 blobs live); a compromised operator can admit ballots made after close through the board path for up to about two periods.
- 2026-10-13 (P1-18 part 4c-2a, [[close]]): specified. 5.5 judges each copy by its own path (`window-before-open`, `window-after-close`, `window-boarded-late`), and 6.3 fails `close-contested` when removing the ordinary ballots in blocks after the close head `B` changes a cell's counted set; `B` is the `k`-th largest of the default panel's close statements (`0x0015`), which can only fail an election, never change what counts. [[revoting]]'s MUST now names the three ways a ballot made after close can still count.
