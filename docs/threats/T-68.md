# T-68: Moderation abuse

- Area: Development, supply chain and operations (G-14)
- Adversary: A-2, A-1
- Status: **Planned.** Residual: content anchored on-chain or on IPFS can't be erased, only hidden by our front ends.

## Threat

Moderation is used to suppress polls or results, or anchored content can't be moderated at all.

## Mitigation

A written policy; moderation acts on poll content, never results (P2-10).
