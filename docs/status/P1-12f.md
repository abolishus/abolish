# P1-12f: The committed obligation root

- State: in progress (part 1a, the root record, its leaves, leaf availability, the delivery check and capacity in the spec with vectors, merged in #287; part 1b-1, the board's root call and contract-event version 5, merged in #295; part 1b-2, the registry's rule 9 and verifier 2.3 on version 5, next)
- Branch: `claude/p1-12f`
- Phase: Phase 1: Threat model, spec, crypto core, circuits, verifier, reference election (CLI only)

## Scope

Build [[0031-national-delivery-obligation]] (option C, accepted by the owner on 2026-10-16). Needed before the first civic poll, not before launch: at launch every trustee is synthetic (the transitional panel, every seat ours), so a root adds nothing checkable against us until independent trustees hold seats. at `c_I` each trustee seat commits what it owes as one signed Merkle root through the contract path, its leaves (receipts, then complaints over a second root) published under [[0029-board-data-availability]]'s availability rule, and the delivery check reads a seat as delivered for a ballot from a leaf with its inclusion proof. Per-record batches ([[P1-12d]], [[P1-12e]]) stay the path for small elections and clients.

- [[delivery]]: the root record and its signed tag, its leaves' layout and content address, what makes a root's leaves unavailable and the report code against the seat, and the capacity check reduced to a constant per seat for elections that use roots.
- [[contracts]]: the board call and its event, in a new contract-event version (5), which rule 9 then pins and 2.3 requires; decided before any registry is deployed.
- `packages/crypto` with an independent Python model and vectors; `packages/contracts`; `packages/verifier` (coordinate with [[P1-19]] and [[P4-3]]).

## What was built (part 1a)

- [[delivery]], Obligation root: record type `0x001a`, version 1 (fixed by ballot versions 1 to 3; versions 4 and 5 fix none yet). `definition_hash`, `seat`, `kind` (1 receipts, 2 complaints), `count`, `root`, `leaves_cid` and one `p256-sig` under `abolish/v1/sig/obligation-root` over the 106 bytes before it; 173 bytes. Leaves are batches' pairs and entries, strictly ascending by ballot hash; the leaf object is their concatenation, named by its canonical `objectCid`; the obligation tree is RFC 9162 under `abolish/v1/obligation/leaf` and `/node`, with RFC 9162 inclusion proofs. At most one root per seat and kind fills its slot.
- [[delivery]], Leaf availability: the seat serves its leaves from submission; each counted-extent attester names, by `H(DS("abolish/v1/obligation-root", record))`, every root in time whose leaves it holds and has checked; a root is available with `q − k + 1` of the default panel's seats naming it, and no seat sums before it holds every available root's leaves, otherwise withheld (`root-withheld`, against its seat, with who named it and who attested without). Consistency (`root-inconsistent`), the bound `2M` (`root-count`), the fetch cap, and unverifiable rather than failed when an available root's leaves can't be fetched.
- [[delivery]], Delivery check: a root is read once, with `delivery-stray`, `delivery-seat`, `root-signature`, `root-repeat`, `delivery-late`, `root-count`, `root-withheld` and `root-inconsistent` in that order, then leaf by leaf as a batch's pairs and entries are, reports carrying `leaf`.
- [[delivery]], What a node owes and Capacity: an election commits roots when, alone, its batched obligation doesn't fit; its seats then send one receipt root by `c_D` and one complaint root by `c_K`, each interval carrying `s · ROOT_CALL_GAS` (200,000, provisional until part 1b measures it). Until the call, the code and P4-3 part 2c exist, `forcedPathFits` counts every election with batches, so nodes refuse those elections.
- Client rules (a client may check its leaf's inclusion as evidence, and never stands down on it), Decoding, Boarding, Direct submit, Residuals (the availability committee, the fetch, a root binding the seat's view) and Test vectors; [[verifier]] 5.9, [[versioning]], [[domain-separation]], the spec index, `RECORD_TYPES` and `TAG_REGISTRY`.
- `docs/spec/vectors/delivery-root.json` from `packages/crypto/scripts/delivery-root-vectors.py`, which reuses the delivery, batch, board and obligation generators and writes its own dag-pb CIDs, checked against all 15 CIDs of `content-addressing.json` (Kubo's and ipfs-unixfs-importer's) before printing.
- Threat files T-17, T-24, T-25, T-31, T-52, T-53 and T-54; STATUS Known-weak (the cap).

## Known-weak (part 1a)

- **Spec ahead of code.** Nothing reads or sends a root yet: the board has no root call (part 1b), `packages/crypto` and the verifier don't read roots (parts 2 and 3), and the trustee node doesn't send them. Until then no root exists, so nothing is weakened: the forced path's cap stands, and nodes refuse elections above it.
- **Availability waits on [[P4-3]] part 2c.** The counted-extent attestation that names roots isn't specified yet; this part fixes what it must bind (the record hashes of the roots its seat holds), and part 2c brings the record.
- **`ROOT_CALL_GAS` is provisional**, 200,000 from an estimate of one P-256 check, two storage keys, cold registry reads and an event; part 1b binds it to measured gas, which may change the capacity vectors.
- **The transitional panel.** Every seat is ours at launch, so every root's availability is the operator's word ([[0031-national-delivery-obligation]], Consequences); roots matter only once independent trustees hold seats.
- **Version 1 only** (ballot versions 1 to 3, no author panel); ballot versions 4 and 5 need a version naming the panel, with [[P2-16]].

## Independent review (part 1a)

An in-session subagent reviewed the diff against `.github/review/crypto-review.md` and reproduced the vectors. It found two blocking issues and five others, all addressed:

- **Blocking: a source could make a root inconsistent.** Bytes that don't hash to `leaves_cid` are now a failed fetch from that source (unverifiable if none serves the object), never `root-inconsistent`; the CID's framing is checked at decode from the signed kind and count; vectors for wrong and missing bytes replace the one that judged a root by a source's bytes.
- **Blocking: honest seats could sum over different sets.** Leaf availability (Summing) and [[tally]] now make the counted set a function of the attestations and the available roots' leaves, and forbid any summed share before a seat holds every available root's leaves, so no recipient holds `k` points for two sets; P4-3 part 2c's no-summing rule and `A'` must cover them.
- A root is available with `q − k + 1` names, not `k`: withholding an honest root then takes `k` seats, a coalition that can open ballots, and the residual says roots add this where batches don't.
- `leaves_cid` framing at decode (above); `root-repeat`'s order is the contract's event sequence, never the operator's; the T-54 line reworded; `A'` sizing recorded for P4-3 part 2c.

## What was built (part 1b-1)

- [[contracts]], Board: `submitObligationRoot(bytes)` checks a root as [[delivery]] decodes it (type and election, version 1, `kind` 1 or 2 `invalid-enum` at 36, `count` non-zero at 37, every field present before the CID's first four bytes are judged against its kind and count at 73, a canonical low-`s` signature at 109, 173 bytes), its seat in the registered key set, one P-256 signature under `abolish/v1/sig/obligation-root` over the 106 bytes before it, then `root-repeat` (a new `DeliveryCheckLib.Code`) if the seat's slot for that kind is filled; it emits `ObligationRootSubmitted(bytes32 indexed definitionHash, uint8 indexed seat, bytes record)`. One bit per kind in a word per definition and seat. `LAST_KNOWN_TYPE` is now `0x001a`, so types `0x0018` to `0x001a` are `unexpected-record-type` on every call. The board is 22,025 bytes, 2,551 under the limit.
- [[contracts]], Contract-event record: Event tables (version 5), the root event; Delivery records step 8 and the Calls, Repeats, Events, Vectors, Gas and Known-weak rows; [[delivery]]'s Direct submit and Capacity on the call; [[versioning]], [[verifier]] 2.3 and rule 9 noting that version 4 MUST be retired before a board with the call is deployed, which part 1b-2 does.
- `docs/spec/vectors/contract-event-v5.json` from `packages/crypto/scripts/contract-event-v5-vectors.py`, over the version-4 generator and delivery-root-vectors.py's decoder: a ballot, a batch and the five roots of `delivery-root.json`, 42 failures (every check, every root decode rejection carried in the event, a root under either batch event and a batch under the root event), 4 decode rejections and the board's accumulator; every version-4 vector re-checked under version 5.
- `packages/crypto`: `decodeObligationRoot` and `encodeObligationRoot` (the record only; the tree, CID and delivery check over roots are part 2's), contract-event version 5 and `ObligationRootSubmitted` in `checkContractEvent`.
- `delivery-root.json` gains seven decode rejections from its generator, whose header now gives `unknown-record-type` for an unlisted type as the board does; nothing else in it changes.
- `ROOT_CALL_GAS` stays 200,000: a root call with dense calldata costs about 176,000 cold against real registries under `osaka` with its intrinsic gas, so `delivery-root.json`'s capacity vectors stand.

## Known-weak (part 1b-1)

- **The board may not be deployed yet.** Its root event needs contract-event version 5, but the registry's rule 9 and verifier 2.3 still pin version 4 until part 1b-2; an election pinning version 4 would fail 4.4 at the first root on a shared board (T-53). Nothing is deployed, and no trustee node sends a root.
- **The verifier doesn't read version 5** (it reads 1 to 4), so a profile pinning it is unverifiable until part 1b-2, and a root's leaves aren't read until part 3.
- **A root's fields are free to its seat** within its slot: about 68 chosen bytes per root, two roots per seat and election (T-17).
